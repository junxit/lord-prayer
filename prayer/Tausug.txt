Bahasa Sūg

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tausug New Testament (tsg), © 1998, 2018 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ū Amaꞌ namuꞌ ha surgaꞌ,
bang mayan lagguun sin mānusiyaꞌ in ngān mu mahasuchi.
Bang mayan ikaw na in mamarinta ha mānusiyaꞌ katān.
Bang mayan maagad dī ha dunya in kabayaan mu,
biyaꞌ sin ha surgaꞌ, asal maagad in kabayaan mu.
Dihilan kāmuꞌ adlaw ini in kakaun kagunahan namuꞌ.
Ampuna in manga dusa namuꞌ
biyaꞌ sin pagmaap namuꞌ ha manga tau nakarusa kāmuꞌ.
Palayua kami dayn ha kasigpitan makapilad sin īman namuꞌ
iban tampani kami dayn ha Saytan, amu in puunan sin katān kangīan.’ (Na, biyaꞌ ha yan in suntuan sin pagpangayuꞌ niyu duwaa pa Tuhan.)
