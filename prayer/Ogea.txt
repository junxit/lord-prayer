Erima

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from ITOU NOMO HILOU BEELE (eri), © 2013 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Oo ebere Jijei,
ne ha laluwa langa yafani,
ne unyite guuni inyou.
Name huuna la yafou wa tigini mayou.
Ere oula la ne name gauwou oojo
wei nomo,
nere ha laluwa langa oojode dorofe.
Elebe fati beha nomo
da edomu higau.
Onodu ne edo,
ere ebere nyabuluwou uulu hagau,
nere ila une la tafa higigane waha
ere uulu nagiya dorofe.
Onodu ne wala-walawou awa nigahe
ere langa adai mayogu,
onou wa ne edo dada
inyabaha, ege gidu haigau.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
