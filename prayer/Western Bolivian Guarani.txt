=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nuevo Testamento Guaraní Pe (gnwNT), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘OreRu ara pe neĩ vae, tuyembɨadoraca nderee.
Tou ndeporoyocuia.
Tuyeyapo nde ndere vae cua ɨvɨ pe ara pe uyeyapo oĩ rami.
Emee oreve opa ara orerembiurã.
Embɨasa oreve orembaeyoa reta, ore numbɨasa ipotave iyoa orecotɨ vae pe rami.
Jare agüɨye emaeño aña guasu re uyapoca vaerã oreve mbaeyoa.
Orerepɨ aña guasu güi.
Echa'ã nembae co mboroyocuia.
Nde nenoi opaete mbaepuere.
Opaete vae nembɨadorata jaeño mai pegua.
Amén.’ Jucuarãi piyerure —jei—.
