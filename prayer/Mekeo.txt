Mekeo

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mauni Mamaga Iifaga (mek), © 1998 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amamai ufai loague aumu, Akamu galolofa aoma.
Ke emu agofaꞌa gamai.
Oi emu logoai kapaꞌina anina loani ufai kekapa auga iꞌopoga mo aagoai isafa gaikapa.
Aufa kina foꞌamaga pau mopenimai.
Ke lai papiau eꞌi apala aꞌagegeainiꞌi koa iꞌopoga,
Oi isafa lai emai apala moꞌagegeainiꞌi.
Ke mopapeaimai iꞌopo apalaꞌi aloꞌiai falakoko,
ke tiapolo apala auga eegai mopapealaimai.’”

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
