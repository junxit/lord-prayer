Dakelh

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yakʼusda bughunek: k’andit khunek neba lhaidinla (cafNT), © 2002 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ne'aBá yak'uz sinda.
Nyoozi dóoti.
Hoh hubudayi cho inli,
whusahóolts'it.
Hukwa' ninzun t'eh, ndoh honeh,
ndi yun k'ut 'ink'ez yak'uz chah.
K'an dzin uzte'alh,
'i negha iní'ai.
'Uyoon bulh tink'us ts'uwhulh'ai t'eh, buba whunats'odoolnah.
'Et whuz na'a zeh tink'us 'uts'ut'ih 'et neba whunaoodolnah.
Nts'e tink'us ts'unint'uk, whuch'a neghinli.
'Ink'ez ndun ntsi', 'en chah buch'a neghinli.
'Et yak'uz k'une' 'int'en 'int'ah, 'ink'ez be ilhtus,
be dénti, 'i chah, 'i 'ilhuz wheni, 'i ts'iyawh nye'ildzun 'int'ah.
'Et ndoh honeh.’
