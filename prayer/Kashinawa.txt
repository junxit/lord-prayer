Hãtxa Kuĩ

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Diosun Jesúswen taexun yuba bena yiniki (cbsNT), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

—Nukun Epa nai anuatuun, min kena medibidan,
mia duawakin jau mia kenwankubainunbunwen.
Nai anuxun min yunua jaska chibanmisbu keska wakin
nenuxundi dasibibundi jau min shinan besti chibankubainunbunwen.
Ja inun, nuku mekekin na jabiatian nun piyunun
jaska min shinainwen piti nuku inanwen.
Ja inun, nuku chakabuwaibu jatu jakimawakin
jatun chakabu nun ana jaska shinanmawen taexun
nun mia chakabuwai buaxuntan ana jaskakidi nuku shinanmayamawe.
Nuku mekekin jawen chakabuti tibi nuku kainmaxunyamawe.
Ja inun, jawada chakabu tibi nuku nemaxundiakubainwen, atiki— atan
