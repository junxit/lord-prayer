=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kasuyatan to Diyus (msmNT, ISO 639-3 msm), © 1999 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Amoy noy no diya to yangit,
tahudon ka podom yagboy.
Muhari ka podom to tibo kaotawan,
aw matuman to pagboot nu kani to babow to pasak
angod to pagkatuman to pagboot nu diya to yangit.
Bogaji koy to pagkoon noy kuntoon no aedow.
Pasayluha koy to mgo sae noy,
su pigpasaylu noy on to namakasae kanami.
Tabangi koy agun kona koy matintal,
aw saganga kanami to gahom ni Satanas.
