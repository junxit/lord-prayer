pãmié

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Majepacʉ jʉ̃menijicʉi yávaiye mamaene coyʉitucubo (cubNT), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Mʉ, ñʉjepacʉ, cavarõ mearocacʉ, meacʉ bajubu mʉ. Que baru caivʉ põeva mʉre pued̶arĩ mearore jíjarãri.
Bʉ́re d̶ajacʉ mʉ mi jabotequiyede põevare. Caivʉ ijãravʉcavʉ d̶ajarãri mearore yópe mi ʉrõpe, yópe ina cavarõ mearocavʉ ne d̶aiyepe mi ʉrõpe cainʉmʉa.
Jíjacʉ ñʉjare jãravʉa coapa ñʉje ãiyeva.
Aru ãrʉmetejacʉ ñʉje ãmeina teiyede, yópe ñʉja máre ñʉje ãrʉmeteiyepe caiye apevʉ ne ãmeina d̶aiyede ñʉjaque.
Ñai abujuvai jabocʉ ʉ̃i jʉjovajʉroede ñʉjare vainí tʉicõjemejacʉ ñʉjare, ñʉje d̶abenajiyepe ãmenore. Quénora ñʉjare ãmed̶are d̶aicõjemejacʉ ñai abujuvai jabocʉre, arejamed̶a Jesús, náre bueyʉ Jʉ̃menijicʉque jẽniaiyede.
