Ulingan

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from YEESUS OPOR ELIWA (mhl), © 1998 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Niŋore Mama, go Sombuŋgo goge. Gore tina tiriine peine.
“Go siŋaŋ nobende qorumaŋgore fori fukeine. Gore maŋ aŋi Sombuŋgo reŋgaegobi, ore so moreŋgo oŋuakoŋ fukeine.
“Muŋambere uqonoŋuŋ oi sonoŋuŋgo noreigoŋ.
“Goine yoŋore tife niŋoreo pega, niŋo oi boke yaregobeneŋ ine, go ore so niŋore tife jure noreigoŋ.
“Eesoigo egu waki nobeiŋ ore sabare noberu Sembene Rauinere meone bomuku nobeigoŋ. (Go gake qorumaŋ Rauine, usuŋ Rauine eru kuririquraŋ Rauine oi damaŋine damaŋine go waperu gomeŋ eru tatariine tomiri go ropemiŋ. Oi foriine.)
