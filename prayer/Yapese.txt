thin ni Waqab

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Bible Ni Thothup (yap), © 2007 Bible Society of Micronesia, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Chitamangimad ni ga bay u tharmiy:
Ngan tay fan fithingam nib thothup,
ma ga par ni gur e ga be gagiyeg nagmad,
min rin’ e tin nib m’agan’um ngay u roy u but’ ni bod ni yibe rin’ u tharmiy.
Mpi’ ngomad e daba’ e ggan ni nge yag ngomad.
Ma ga n’ag fan u wun’um e kireb romad,
ni bod ni kug n’iged fan u wun’mad e kireb nike rin’ boch e girdi’ ngomad.
Dab mfekmad i yan ko sikeng nib mo’maw’,
machane ngam ayuwegmad rok Fa’anem nib Kireb.’
