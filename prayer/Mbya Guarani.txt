ayvu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nhanderuete ayvu iky'a e'ỹ va'e (gun), © 2004 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Oreru yva pygua, tove nderery tomboetea.
Tovaẽ ndepo'akaa ojekuaa aguã. Reipota va'e tojapoa yvy re, yva re ojapoa rami avi.
Ko'ẽ nhavõ ejou uka ro'u va'erã.
Ha'e emboguepa ke ndevy rorevea, ore voi orevy oreve va'e roperdoaa rami avi.
Orereity uka eme poromoangekoa pe, orereraa jepe ke mba'emo vai gui. [Mba'eta ndepo'akaa, nembaraetea ha'e nerexakãa oĩ riae 'rã raka'e rã peve guarã. Amém,]” peje 'rã.

Same as the Traditional form above.
