=== Traditional ===
[Verified from KAKAI HAEU (ssg, ISO 639-3 ssg), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“‘Tel Tamamite Haeu,
amite sameni o nake o pate tuah wanen.
Pahamite o neiniwa ma ngahatiwa amite.
Hualima amite usiam hanowi tetesol lato kapim
leili tonam.
Axima amite an tapai ti ngain tehu tehu.
Oxoxiwa amite ti kawatan ti lialui woumite,
masin ti amite oxoxi seilon liai ti kawatan
ti waliko lialun laha aile hani amite.
O kum hahani amite totohong ailan
lehe amite kum aile saun lialun.
Ape awaiwa amite ti Satan
ma teka spirit lialun tetan.’
