Ka Ktien Khasi

=== Traditional ===
Ko Kpa jong ngi uba ha bneng: Ai ba ka kyrteng bakhuid jong me kan long kaba la pyndonburom;
ai ba ka Hima jong me kan wan; ai ba ka mon jong me yn leh ha ka khyndew kumba ka long ha bneng.
To ai ha ngi mynta ka jingbam kaba ngi donkam.
To map ïa ngi ïa ki jingpalat kiba ngi la leh, kumba ngi map ïa ki jingpalat kiba kiwei ki la leh ïa ngi.
Wat ïalam ïa ngi sha ka jingtynjuh kaba eh, hynrei sumar ïada ïa ngi na Uba Sniew.

Naba ka jong me long ka hima, bad ka bor, bad ka burom junom. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
