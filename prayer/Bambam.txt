=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Suha' Maseho: Kadadiam anna Pa'dandiam Bakahu (ptu, ISO 639-3 ptu), © 2004 Lembaga Alkitab Indonesia, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ambeki yabo suhuga,
dipa'kasallei sanga masehomu,
kela mala Ikom ma'pahenta,
anna dituhu'i pa'elo'mu
illaam inde lino umba susi yabo suhuga.
Beengkanni kinande la sihuanna kiande allo temo.
Anna muampunniangkanni kasalaangki,
umba susi muampunnikam duka' to kasalaam lako kaleki.
Anna daa ullappa'kam tama pa'sändäsam,
sapo' la ullappasangkam dio mai kakuasaanna kakadakeam.
[Aka Iko to umpuängam kapahentaam, kakuasaam, anna kamatandeam sule lako salako-lakona. Amin.]’
