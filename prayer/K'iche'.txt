Kʼicheʼ

=== Traditional ===
Qa Tat ri kʼo la pa ri kaj,
chnimarisax ta na u qʼij ri bʼiʼ la.
Taqan na la pa ki wiʼ konojel winaq.
Bʼana bʼaʼ na la ri rayibʼal la cho ruwächulew, jas ri käbʼan chilaʼ chikaj.
Ya la chqawäch ri käqatijo, jas ri rajwaxik chqe kämik.
Sacha la ri qa mak ri qaʼnom jeʼ jas ri uj käqasach ri ki mak ri käkaʼn käx chqe.
Mäya la chqe chi kujtaqchiʼx che mak, xane chujtoʼ la cho ri Itzel.

Ronojel waʼ käqataʼ che la rumal chi ri lal kätaqan la puwiʼ ronojel. Kʼo nimalaj chuqʼabʼ ukʼ la puwiʼ ronojel, chnimarisax ta bʼaʼ qʼij la amaqʼel pa taq ri qʼij ri junabʼ ri kepetik. Amén.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.

(Source: Kʼicheʼ New Testament, New Orthography, © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, San Mateo 6:9-13 — modern orthography, includes the doxology. God is addressed with the reverential pronoun "la." An older Kʼicheʼ Bible edition omits the doxology and ends at "deliver us from evil.")
