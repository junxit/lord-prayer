=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kisi Bible (kiz), © 2020 Door 43 International Mission Community, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Tata ghwetu j'ha uj'hele kunani, ulitukusiaghe litabhu liaku.
Bhufalme bhuaku uhidai mapenzi ghaku ghabhombekai apa paduniani kama okhu kumbinguni.
Utupelaghe n'kate ghwitu ghwa kila ligono.
utusameayi lideni lyitu, kama tete kyatukabhasamehe bhadeni bhitu.
Na usituleti mu majaribu, lakini utuj'hepusiaghe kuhoma kwa j'hola mwovu.
