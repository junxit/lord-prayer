[Verified from Tiits nii xnee kyalmbañ; El Nuevo Testamento en el zapoteco de Quioquitani (ztqNT), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tat Tios, lii nii soboo kpaa, xñab noo nii kchë meñ kuuñ ksak lii.
Ni xñab noo nii ktsiñ tse nii kñabeyoo lo kislyu sinak nii xñabeyoo kpaa.
Bneetsy nii tyaaw noo natse.
Beeñ noo perdón sinak nii chuuñ noo perdón meñ nii xko falt loo noo.
Klaadyoo jkaal xaatox lëë noo parñee kyaabty noo lo tol. Pteelaa lëë noo lo xaa.
