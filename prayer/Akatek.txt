Kuti'

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ja' An Nuevo Testamento (knjNT), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Mamin, ey jaach ec' bey satcan. Miman ojab yel oc apnoj ja bi.
Titan ja wak' Yaawilal titi'. Jaxca ojab yunen tzet cha woche bey satcan tu', quey ojab tu' yunen sat tx'otx' ti'.
Ak' cu lo'be oj cu lo' yet jun jun c'u on.
Ak' miman c'ulal kiban yu cu penail, jaxca chi kute kak'on miman c'ulal yiban eb ey tzet chi cu uteni.
Manchej jon ja cha' ijbaletoj yin spenail. Catu' chon ja colon pax el yul sk'ab jun yaawil spenail. [Yutol asan jaach Yaaw jaach tobal k'inal. Catu' miman ja wopiso, catu' miman pax ja wel oc apnoj. Amén.]
