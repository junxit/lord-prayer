=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Pwapwior we e Santus (stw), © 2024 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Semmaem wooai naaeng,
aemwo epwe ssoar minna itoamw ina e santus,
aemwo Mweoiumw epwe war,
aemwo nee tipoamw epwe no
wooan feoniufan wunuunun rhag noan naaeng.
Ngaennegemaem mwongoi raaeneei aeipwe mwongo igina.
Wopwe saeneti noa aemaem monofit,
wunuunun pwan aemaem aeiya saenetiir noa rhooakkena re monofit ngaenigemaem.
Wosoapw oatonongegemaem noan sessoat,
nge wopwe aengesagemaem saengi Man Aenneew we.
[Pwe oamw rhag mweoi me manaman me nning esoapw iug. Amen.]’
