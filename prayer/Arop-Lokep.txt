=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Rau Ke Maro (apr, ISO 639-3 apr), © 2022 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamamam yo kunepe ye malala kiong ete ni, in ampatarau pong nen,
bet di tooltool nga le imot tiparanaia em.
Inbe kusi a si matam kalam am le imot,
inbe di tooltool ke tana i bet titoo lom
dawa ben di tooltool ke malala kiong titoo lom nga.
Inbe kaningi yo ke katai nga, ngan kop pam.
Inbe kugiri sennene kiam yo amyei nga,
dawa ben am lapau amgiri sennene kidi diemam yo tiyei pam nga.
Inbe kupasongosongam le ken amkapsap ye dada dook tiap ke touanga be,
bong kou am le kupalisam ye tool dook tiap Satan.’
