=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Jesucristo oy oa’pak: Kenda Jesucristoa; monigka’uyatenok; Diostaj mo’manopo’yarea’ika’ne (amrNT, ISO 639-3 amr), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Oroʼen Apag i̱ʼe̱ne, Apag Dios. Kurudyo̱ i̱ʼi̱kaʼne. Kenpaʼda ʼurunanada i̱ʼi̱kaʼne. O̱ʼnen huadik kenpaʼda huadakda o̱ʼi̱kaʼ. Ayaʼada kaʼdikeʼ.
Huakhuairi i̱ʼe̱nok i̱da ayaʼada ontaj moʼnigpe̱i̱keʼ. Dios Huairi i̱ʼe̱po ayaʼda iʼpakpo iʼmanikaʼ. Kurudyo̱ hua̱e̱ʼeria moʼnigpe̱i̱kaʼpo o̱gkikaʼ konigti i̱yo̱ huadariyo ayaʼada huakaʼ.
Inmeʼnoete aypo oroʼtaj aʼnigya̱ya̱ʼpo boʼtihuapok.
Oroʼtaj dakhueaʼ mogkameʼnigtaj oroa oʼbakkahueay konigti oroʼen dakhueaʼ eʼkikaʼtaj bokkahueaʼ.
Dakhueaʼ eʼkaʼ nopo̱e̱hueʼ ka̱i̱keʼpo montihuapok. Namaʼda eʼkaʼtaʼ eʼpaknayo kikahueʼ ka̱e̱ʼpo boʼtihuapok, Apag Dios. Dios Huairinayo ayaʼenda Huakhuairinanada i̱ʼe̱po teynananada i̱ʼe̱po kenpaʼda huadaknananada i̱ʼe̱nok kenda ayaʼada kanʼmanbatiaʼpakikeʼ, ken. Ken konig yanʼtionaʼpakikatiʼ.
