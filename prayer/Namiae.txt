Namiai (Barai)

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Godon Vuak Ma Ijin (nvm), © 2004 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

God Asoinuvuon kaf gufi samuamo. No if on akaij iji aroiken.
No vierafega a ro oinuvuon igi samuaguokfuon. No vierafega izeg varur gufia bu rejo ijeg e moikigia karinva iji bu irerag a vierafeno ijia keok.
Ivia duok no nakikmazak iokfuon iji vajuo.
Regavo ni izeg e kafobuon ise iji no giame arevo ijeg ni ise nuvuon guome are.
Regavo ab no aferduome una unam ise ijia biesuok. Regavo ni ifejduoga no kaven ise Setanon daro ijia no karioijo iji no areme vak.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
