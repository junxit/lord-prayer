Daai

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dai Cangcim Kthai (dao), © 2018 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Khana vekia kami pa,
na ngming ngcim naw leisawngnak yah se.
Na khaw pha law se;
Na hlüei khankhawa ami pawha kba khawmdeka pi pawh u se.
Tuhngawi kami ei khyawh vai ei jah pe bä.
Kami khana mkhyekatki he kami jah mhläta kba,
kami mkhyekatnak pi jah mhlät bä.
Mkhyekat vaia hlawhlepnaka käh jah cehpüi lü,
Khawyaipaa kut üngkhyüh pi jah yung kawpi. Khaw ni, johit ja hlüngtainak he cun na kaa kya se. Amen.
