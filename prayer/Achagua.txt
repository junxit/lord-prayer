Achawa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Liáꞌa Chuánshi Dios Shínaa (aca), © 2013 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Wasálijinaa yáairri áacairra,
chóniwenai cawáunta nacába jijí'inaa.
Jíinu chérra jiwánacaala áani,
jiméda jiwánacaalau áani cáinabi íta'aa,
chaléjta jimédau'inaa áacairra.
Jiá walí íyacaishi warrúnaanica éerri jútainchu.
Jipérdona wáucha máashii wamédanica,
chaléjta wapérdonau wayá,
náa'a médenai walí máashii.
Ujínda wacácoo máashii rícula,
jitúya wayá liúcha liá'a wawásimica.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
