Gã

=== Traditional ===
Wɔ-Tsɛ ni yɔɔ ŋwɛi!
Ogbei lɛ he atse!
Omaŋtsɛyeli lɛ aba!
Afee nɔ ni osumɔɔ yɛ shikpɔŋ lɛ nɔ, taakɛ afeɔ yɛ ŋwɛi lɛ!
Hã wɔ ŋmɛnɛ wɔdaa ŋmãa.
Ni okɛ wɔtɔmɔi lɛ afa wɔ, tamɔ bɔ ni wɔkɛfaa mɛi ni tɔɔ wɔnɔ lɛ.
Ni okɛ wɔ akaya kaa mli;
shi jiemɔ wɔ kɛje ɛfɔŋ lɛ mli.

Shi onɔ ji maŋtsɛyeli lɛ, kɛ hewalɛ lɛ kɛ anunyam lɛ, kɛyashiɔ naanɔ. Amen!

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
