=== Traditional ===
[Verified from LOINA HAUHAUNA (bdd, ISO 639-3 bdd), © 1991 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Tamama galewa, saniu ꞌaheꞌasisiye, ma ꞌaꞌahwanoiyego ma ꞌowa ꞌuloinemai,
ma ꞌoinega ꞌino nuwatuhuwedi ꞌapaihowadi, gide tuwa megalewa sipaipaihowa.
Cama ꞌeꞌahai sabwelo ꞌaigeda ꞌaigeda ꞌoina ꞌuꞌebwaꞌebwaemai.
Ma ꞌima mumuga galadi ꞌunuwasamudi, gide wete tauma ꞌiyamayao ꞌidi mumuga galadi ꞌoima ꞌanuwasamudi.
Ma sahena ꞌutohotohomai, ma hesi togalaina ꞌoinega ꞌuꞌabihetemai.”
