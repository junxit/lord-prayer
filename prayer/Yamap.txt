=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yamap (ymp, ISO 639-3 ymp), © 2025 SIL Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“ ‘Yoô ning Kamik hômô melak leng,
nombong yong athêng injêk matheng.
Me yong lêkliŋyak bêng êlêm.
Me yoô sopa yong lem hiving injêk pîk hîtôm esopa hêk melak leng.
Me nôŋgêv nôm wak iti êndêng yoô.
Me notak yoô ning kambom hîtôm thak yoôtak avîlanô vi ning kambom takatu be evong ethak yoô.
Me dô notak yoôndôk nôm takatu be tem neyek yoô andô. Me nôŋgêv yoô vê injêk anô kambom bang.’
