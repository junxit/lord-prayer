=== Traditional ===
[Verified from YISU KILISI ANE BINGE VIE GIENGK IWAL AVOS (kbm, ISO 639-3 kbm), © 1984 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamangg-amei ebe gubweg gulumb ok, amei tangg-amei givin anei amolmol avos nivwat mie arem nemb ta-ngge ma.
Mie nomb amei dabin ve am gwang-ne be amei anvang mul ve miam tam givin ge nangge nalk, denang ebe nangge gulumb ok.
Amei-mei ben asmate walang ok ane galkik etek mie gob gitangi amei.
Mie tam nivalngan amei-mei tiate ebe algum ok, dang ete amei tangg-amei givalngan tiate ebe amolmol ilgum gitangi amei ok.
Mi nung-gas amei antangi gen tiate ande bwaya, be nung-gas amei ankwai Sadam tis ane gen tiate. (Mie-ngge gitangi ve nob amolmol dabin, be Amol gwangne be Amol bogbogo ane, be asonge numbweg dang etok nemb ta-ngge. Bingano.’)
