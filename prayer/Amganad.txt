[Verified from Ifugao, Amganad (ifa, ISO 639-3 ifa), © 2014 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ama an wah ad abunyan, hanat he''ay dayawon an namin di tatagu.
Ya hanat he''ay mun'ap'apu i da'mi ta nan pohdom di ma'at hitun luta an umat hi ad abunyan.
Ya hanat abigabigat on idatmuy itanudmi.
Ya hanat pakawanon da'mi hinadan numbahulanmi te teen pakawanonmi damdama nadan numbahul i da'mi.
Ya adim bahan iyabulut di nunhiglan apatnaanmi ta wan adi da'mi baliyan hinan diyablu.
