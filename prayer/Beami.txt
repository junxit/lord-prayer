Bedamuni

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from GODE EA SIA: IDA:IWANE GALA (beo), © 2000 Tom Hoey, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

ne gadosea, agoane sia:ma, ‘Ninia Ada Gode Hebene ganodini esala! Dia hadigi Dio amoma dunu ilia nodone sia:mu da defea.
Hebene ganodini fi da Dia Hinadafa Hou hamonana. Amo hou da osobo bagadega doaga:mu da defea.
Ninia wali eso ha:i manu defele, ha:i manu ninima sagoma.
Ninia eno dunu ilia wadela:i hou ninima hamoi gogolema:ne olofosu, amo defele ninia wadela:i hou hamoi, Di gogolema:ne olofoma.
Be Sa:ida:ne da ninima dafama:ne ado ba:su hou mae olelema:ne, Di fidima. Amola Sa:ida:ne da nini wadela:sa:besa:le, Di gaga:ma.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
