=== Traditional ===
[Verified from In Yancuic Tlahtolsintilil (nhiNT, ISO 639-3 nhi), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

ToTahtzin Dios naquin tonmeuiltihticah iluicac,
macsili mouisticuehcapantlalilis motoocaatzin.
Maualmouica motiquiuahcayotzin nican in tlalticpac.
Mamochiua motlaniquilitzin, quemeh itich in iluicac,
ohcon noiuqui nican in tlalticpac.
Tlen mostlahtica itmomahseuiah ixtechmomactilihtzino axan.
Uan ixtechontlapohpolui in totlateuiquil monauactzinco,
ohcon quemeh tehuan itquintlapohpoluiah naquin itlah techuiquiliah.
Uan amo ixcomocauili matuitzican itich itlah amo cuali tlen techyoltilana.
Yehyeh ixtechmopaleuilihtzinoh amo matuitzican imac in tlen amo cuali;
itmitzontlahtlaniliah nochi nin nic Tehuatzin moyohcatzin in tiquiuahcayotl,
in tlanauatihcayotl, uan in uehcapantlalilis, ica in sintitl nochipa. Amén.
