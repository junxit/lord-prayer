کٲشُر (कॉशुर)

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from a 19th-century missionary translation as recorded in R. Rost, "The Lord's Prayer in Five Hundred Languages" (Kashmiri entry, p. 81), in Perso-Arabic (Nastaliq) script. This is an older orthography than the modern Bible Society of India Kashmiri Bible (1988/2011), whose Matthew text was not freely available for verbatim quotation. The doxology is present in the source (see transliteration below); its Nastaliq line was not transcribed verbatim, so the script below runs through "deliver us from evil."]

اي ساﻧِﮧ مالہِ یُس اَسمانَس پٹھہِ چھُك چُون ناو سَپَنِن پاک۔
چآنِ پادشاهَت یِبِین۔
چآنِ مرضی یِتَھہِ پاتَھہِ اَسمانَس پٹھہِ چھہِ زمینَس پٹھہِ تہ سَپَنِن۔
ساﻧِﮧ دُھچ چِٹ بَخش از اَسہ۔
تَہ سآنِ قرض كَر اَسہِ معاف چِنایُپہِ اَسِ تہ چھہِ پَنَنِن قرضدارن معاف كرمت۔
تَہ اَسہ مَہ آن اَزمایشہِ اَندر، بلكہ بدَس نِشہ رچھہ۔

[Doxology, verified in the source's Latin transliteration: "…tikya zi pādshāhat, ta qudrat, ta jalāl, chhu hamesha chhonaⁱ. Āmīn." ("For the kingdom, and the power, and the glory are ever thine. Amen.")]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Transliteration as printed in the source (includes the doxology):

Aī sāni māli yus āsmānas peṭh chhu, Chon nāw sapanin pak.
Chönⁱ pādshāhat yiyin.
Chönⁱ marzī yitha pöth āsmānas peṭh chhe zamīnas peṭh ti sapanin.
Sāni dohach tsot bakhsh az asi.
Ta sönⁱ qarz kar asi muʼāf, yuth asⁱ ti pananⁱen qarzdāran chhi muʼāf karān.
Ta asi ma an āzmaishi andar; balki badas nishi rachh:
tikya zi pādshāhat, ta qudrat, ta jalāl, chhu hamesha chhonaⁱ. Āmīn.
