Taqbaylit

=== Traditional ===
A Baba-tneɣ yellan deg igenwan, isem-ik ad ițwaqeddes,
lḥekma n tgeldit-ik a d-tass, llebɣi-k ad idṛu di lqaɛa akken yedṛa deg igenwan.
Efk-aɣ-d mkul ass tamɛict-nneɣ.
Semmeḥ-aɣ ddnubat-nneɣ akken i nețsamaḥ i wid i ɣ-iḍelmen.
Ssebɛed fell-aɣ ajeṛṛeb, ssellek-aɣ si tḥila n Cciṭan.

Axaṭer ɣuṛ-ek i tella tgeldit, ttazmert d lɛaḍima i dayem. Amin!

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.

(Source: the published Kabyle New Testament, Matthew 6:9-13. The doxology — "Axaṭer ɣuṛ-ek i tella tgeldit, ttazmert d lɛaḍima i dayem. Amin!" / "For thine is the kingdom, the power and the glory forever. Amen!" — is included in this edition.)
