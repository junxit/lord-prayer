=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Chru (cje, ISO 639-3 cje), © 2020 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Khoai Ama buơl dơlhă dò tơ ngŏ lơngì; Angăn Ama hũ Sơc̆ih-Sri;
Lơgăr Ama truh, Hơtai Ama hũ jiơ̆ng, lăm ala lơ̆n kŭng yơu tơ ngŏ lơngì.
Yòng brơi buơl dơlhă hơrơi ni phơ̆n-kơya 'bơ̆ng tơ̆l hơrơi.
Yòng anĭt toh-luơi glài-sơnoh tơ buơl dơlhă, Yơu buơl dơlhă kŭng anĭt toh-luơi tơ du mơnih hũ sŭng glài sơ̆ng buơl dơlhă.
Yòng juơi luơi buơl dơlhă kơđòng pơnuaĭ pơluơ-ơñiai, Bloh dŏng buơl dơlhă klàh mư̆ng pơnuaĭ mơsak-jơhà! (Kơyoa lơgăr, gơnăp-gơnuăr, sơ̆ng màng-kơc̆rà, dò abih găm sơ̆ng Ama rài pơdò rài 'buh luĭ. Amen.)’
