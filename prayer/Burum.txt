Mindik

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Burum-Mindik Bible (bmu), © 1992, 2002 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Neŋgö Iwinini, gi Suepnöŋ maljan. Göhö qetki mönö töröŋi ahöma.
Mönö galöm köl neŋginöŋga bemtohoŋgahö ölŋi asuhuma. Göhö jitsihitki Suepnöŋ tem kölakzei, mewöŋanök mönö gölmenöŋ asuhuma.
Merakö neneŋi mönö dopnine neŋgiman.
Tosatŋi yeŋgöreŋ tosa neŋgöreŋ ahözawi, nini mi mosöt eŋgizin ewö, gi mönö miaŋgö dop neŋgö tosanini saŋgoŋman.
Esapesapnöŋ et neŋgubapuköra mönö aŋgön köl neŋgiba Bölöŋi Toŋaŋgö böröŋeyök meköm neŋgiman. (Gi nangak bemtohoŋ Toŋi, kukösum Toŋi aka asakmararaŋ Toŋi mi nalöŋi nalöŋi mala kota malnöŋ aka teteköŋi qahö mal öŋgöman. Mi ölŋa.)
