Armãneashti

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Biblija tu limba Rrãmãnã (rup), © 2024 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tatãl a nostru tsi eshti tu tseruri,
si sãmtseascã numa a ta.
S-yinã amirãriljea a ta. Si s-facã vrearea a ta tu loc, ashã di cum tu tser.
Pãnea a noastrã di cathi dzuã, dã-nã-u a noauã azã.
Sh-ljeartã-nã amãrtiili a noastri, ashã di-cum sh-noi lã ljirtãm a amãrtioshilor a noshtri.
Sh-nu nã alasã s-cãdem tu cãrteari, ma nã ascapã di atsel arãu. [Cã a ta easti amirãriljea, sh-putearea, sh-mãrirea di totãna]. Amen.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
