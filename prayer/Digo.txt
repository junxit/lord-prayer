Digo

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Digo (dig), © 2019 Bible Translation and Literacy, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Baba, uriye mlunguni, dzinaro nariogophewe.
Utawalao nauonekane wazi,
na ulondaro rihendeke himu duniani dza irivyo hiko mlunguni.
Huphe ihi rero chakurya chehu cha kuhutosha.
Na huswamehe makosa gehu,
dza viratu hunavyoswamehe ahukoserao.
Usihuriche hukatiywa dambini ni Shetani,
ela hutivye na ye mui.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
