Sinaugoro

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from GINITAḠO VARIḠUNA (snc), © 1995 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ḡai Tamama o, gubai otanuni; ḡoi aramu baḡana vaḡa-veaḡaia;
ḡemu Basileia bene iaḡoma; ḡemu ura tanobarai baḡana veia, gubai ḡeveiani kavana.
Ḡema-ḡama toma bono vinima.
Ḡema rakava notuḡamaḡi-fitoḡari, ḡai ḡevaḡa-rakavamani tarimari ḡeri rakava ḡatuḡamaḡi-fitoḡarini vaḡa-ilailana.
Veribaḡani ai asi bono ḡori-kauma, a Rakava Tarimana, Satani, ḡimana na bono vaḡa-maḡurima. [Korana Basileia tu ḡoi ḡemu, seḡuka ḡoi ḡemu, mareva baregona ḡoi ḡemu, toma bene iaḡo mo vanaḡivanaḡi, Amen.]’
