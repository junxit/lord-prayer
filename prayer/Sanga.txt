Isanga

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Sanga Language Project (xsn), © 2022 Luke Initiative for Scripture Translation, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Ate aro ozaa shili,
a monte leze leo a sangsang.
A togomo to de,
aa eze epaaka oya yeme me
a lerambo leena,
nangka ezeze azaa shili.
Nyaro epaolya o ngmeengme.
O pisi ro madinǝ maro,
nangka te pisizi andaaka ne ezeze ro madinǝ.
Tana o ale kaaro ara o kedeya ne,
o wobe ro a taara te ogomo o bere o buru ona.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
