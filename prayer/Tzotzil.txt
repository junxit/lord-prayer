Bats'i k'op

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Jaʼ ti achʼ Testamento: jaʼ scʼopilal ti Jesucristoe jaʼ ti Jcoltavanej cuʼuntique (tzoNT), © 1995 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Jtotic Dios, te oyot ti vinajel.
Ac'o xajpasotutic ti muc'.
Ac'o sta yorail chapas mantal atuc li' ti balumile.
Ac'o c'otuc ti pasel li' ti balumile ti c'usi tsc'an ti avo'ntone hech chaj c'u che'el c'otem ti pasel ti vinajel.
Ac'bun talel jve'eltutic c'ac'al li'to.
Pasbuntutic perdón yu'un jmultutic hech chaj c'u che'el jpasojbetutic perdón much'u oy smul cu'untutic.
Mu me xibatutic yo' bu chisujat ti mulil.
Coltauntutic me hech mu xistsaluntutic ti pucuje.
Ho'ot aventa chapas mantal sbatel osil.
Ho'ot jelaven ap'ijil atuc sbatel osil.
Ho'ot chapasat ti muc' sbatel osil.
Jun yutsil ti hech chc'ot ti pasel, hech chavutic ti stojol.
