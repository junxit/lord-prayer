=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Loina Tabu Auwauna (dob, ISO 639-3 dob), © 1985 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Tamama galewaya ꞌamu esana ꞌaꞌamayabei, ta ꞌasideyo be ꞌoyo ꞌuda loinema,
be ꞌenega ꞌimu nuwena ꞌada guinuwe nadigega taudi me galewa siguiguinuwaꞌesa.
ꞌAma masula ꞌasiyata ꞌebweuna ꞌebweuna ꞌenaya ꞌuda ꞌebwaꞌema.
Ta ꞌima bubuna toꞌumalina ꞌuda nuwataudi, nadigega ꞌaboꞌama wate gosemao ꞌidi bubuna toꞌumalina ꞌemaya ꞌanuwataudi.
Ta geyaꞌabo ꞌutootooma, ta esi tauna totoꞌumalina nina ꞌenega ꞌuda ꞌetoseyema.”
