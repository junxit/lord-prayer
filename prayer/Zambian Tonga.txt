Chitonga

=== Traditional ===
Taata wesu ooli kujulu,
Alilemekwe izina lyako.
Bwami bwako buboole.
Luyando lwako alucitwe,
ansi ano mbubonya mbolucitwa akujulu.
Kotupa zilyo zyitweelede.
Utujatile milandu yesu,
mbubwenya mbotujatila aswe bali amilandu kulindiswe.
Utatweenzyi mukutepaulwa,
kotuvwuna kumubi.

Nkaambo bwami mbubwako, anguzu, abulemu, kukabe kutamani. Ameni.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Taateesu ooli kujulu, alilemekwe izina lyako.
Bwami bwako buboole, luyando lwako alucitwe, ansi ano mbubonya mbolucitwa akujulu.
Kotupa sunu zilyo zyesu zya buzuba abuzuba;
ulekelele milandu yesu,
mbubwenya mbotulekelela aswe bali amilandu kulindiswe;
utatweenzyi mukutepaulwa,
pele kotuvwuna kumubi.

Nkaambo bwami mbubwako, anguzu, abulemu, kukabe kutamani. Ameni.
