Tamasheq

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tamasheq (taq), © 2003 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‹Abba-nănăɣ-i ihăn išənnawăn,
səmɣărnet ăddinăt isəm-năk wa šăddijăn.
Taset-dd Təmmənəya -năk,
ăjet erhet-năk făll-ărori n-ăkall,
s-əmmək wa s-ija dăɣ-išənnawăn.
Ăkfʼ-anăɣ isudar wi s-nămiɣatăr ašăl-i-dăɣ,
ănš-anăɣ uḍlemăn-nănăɣ
s-əmmək wa s-nənašš i-inaḍlamăn-nănăɣ.
Wăr toyyed Iblis ăžžurrăbăt-anăɣ
isiḍəw-anăɣ dăɣ-abăkkaḍ,
agəẓ-anăɣ dăɣ-s,
[ed Təmmənəya, tărna hakd ălxurmăt, i-năk hărkuk. Amin!]›
