Nchane

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nchane (Ncane) (ncr), © 2020 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Chiji wese wù le fɛwe, gêe lɛ bamii ŋgvûune jee chuŋ,
wo gê lɛ bvunfoŋ bwuŋ to, wo gê fiɛɛ fì wo goone fɛkuu fɛnɛ fi nyûme no fi le fɛwe.
Nya bee bɛ mwɛɛ munjile mu abɛŋ.
Lɛɛshɛ bimbefɛ biɛsa, no bee tɛ lɛɛshi bimbefɛ bì bamii bamu gee fɛ bee le.
Keefɛ wo chînɛ bee lee moŋ wù ya bee le gɛ. Fi bee chiaaŋ yi muh wu kimbefɛ wɛɛ le.’ [Bvunfoŋ le bwuŋ, bvuŋga nyu bwuŋ, bvukugɛ nyu bwuŋ segechii bvu kage gɛ. Fi nyûme noo.]
