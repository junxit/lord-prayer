Rote Lole

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Rote Lole Alkitab (llg, ISO 639-3 llg), © 2004 Unit Bahasa dan Budaya, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amak manai nusa-sodak! Ama naden malalaok nalan seli.
Ela basa hataholi la soꞌu lamadedema Ama naden fo ana seli ndia.
Ela Ama dadik Manek neu basa hataholi la!
Ela basa hataholi la tao tungga Ama palendan nai daebafok ia,
sama leo Ama atan nala tao tungga Ama palenda nai nusa-sodak.
Ama tulun fee ai nanaꞌak mana daik tungga faik.
Ai moke Ama koka heni ai sala-singgo nala,
sama leo ai mafalende heni hataholi sala nala, fo taon neu ai.
Ama, manea fo ai taa tao manggalauk.
Ma masalaꞌe mala ai, mima nitu la koasan mai.
[Te Ama ia, Manek mana koasa manai lain seli, ma ana seli.
Ama toꞌu palenda nakandondoo henin.
Ai huhule-haladoin, kada leondiak Ama. Amin.]
