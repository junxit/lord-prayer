Tok Pisin

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Buk Baibel long Tok Pisin (tpiOTNT), © 1969–2008 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13. The wording in this file matches that edition word for word; collated against the text published at eBible.org.]

Papa bilong mipela, yu stap long heven,
nem bilong yu i mas i stap holi.
Kingdom bilong yu i mas i kam.
Mipela i mas bihainim laik bilong yu long graun olsem ol i save bihainim long heven.
Nau yu ken givim mipela kaikai inap long dispela de.
Na yu ken lusim ol rong bilong mipela,
olsem mipela i save lusim ol rong ol arapela i mekim long mipela.
Na yu no ken larim ol traim i kamap long mipela,
tasol yu ken kisim bek mipela long ol samting nogut.

Long wanem, kingdom na strong na glori, em i bilong yu tasol, oltaim oltaim. Amen.
