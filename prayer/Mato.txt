=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Urana Xuana (met, ISO 639-3 met), © 2018 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Tibumam, uwa long xaiya,
yama am gabo ba lipu longgalo dahatum ba maringing sibuna.
Yonggaxima bing ma.
Am gabo ba lipu titiamdi dilibu ung murungama,
bila dilibu long xaiya.
Uliam anginga sanga mana xaidapka baguli.
Uyunga kubolumam diandi,
bila am gayunga lipu longgalo kuboluding diandi dilibudi mam.
Saing labu uyungam mala tubaigiau tai,
ning oxopkam muli sangua lipu diana rimanoa.
