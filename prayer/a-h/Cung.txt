Chung

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kiŋwakti ki Nyɔ Miŋkaiŋ Mifiaŋ (cug), © 2021 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ba wibuku wə wi kɔ i bɛiŋ,
fə̂ bəni kɔksiki yɛli wa,
fə̂ ŋkuŋ bia dzə,
fə̂ a gia yə wɔ nəŋki yi numki i fa kuku asi yi kɔ i bɛiŋ.
Nyâ buku bəh dzɛiŋ bidaiŋ.
Ma wɔ dâlinya chu bibuku,
asi buku dalinyaki chu biə bəni bədɔkɔ fəki i buku.
Kiə wɔ ki bêe kə a buku liə i mmɔmsi wə.
Bwîli buku i kaŋ yi mi wichu wə.’ [Ŋkuŋ bi bɛiŋ kɔ mbia, ŋga num mbia, mbum num mbia i jɔbi wichi wə kiŋgɔksi kɔbi. Ma yi num ayaka.]
