Banjaal

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Firim fafu fal Aláemit (bqj), © 2015 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

« Ᵽayóli aamme fatiya, Gajai gumaleni,
Jávi jaju jíya jújoul ! Mbi wo numaŋ me ubaj n’ettam ti fatiya !
Usenóli jama mitiñ mamu maa funah faufe.
Uboketóli utilóli ti may wóli jubokete me gatile me ni wóli,
Ban jamb’uhalóli n’julo ni gabut, bare uᵽagenóli ni maarat mánoman ; [mata aw uĉile Jávi, sembe ni gasal bi nánonan. Amen !] »
