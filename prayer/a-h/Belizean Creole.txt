Bileez Kriol

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Di Nyoo Testiment eena Bileez Kriol (bzj), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Fi wee Faada weh deh da hevn,
mek wi kip yu naym hoali.
Kohn kohn set op yu kingdom,
ahn mek evribadi obay yu pahn ert
jos laik how evribadi obay yu da hevn.
Pleez gi wi food evri day.
Ahn fagiv wi fi evriting rang weh wi du
jos laik how wee fagiv peepl weh du wee rang.
Kip wi fahn temtayshan,
ahn protek wi fahn eevl.’
[Da yoo rool yu kingdom,
ahn ga aala di powa ahn di gloari fareva ahn eva. Aymen.]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
