Kuku Yalanji

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Godumu Kuku (gvn), © 1984 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the opening quotation mark the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Nganjinanga nganjan jiringa, yundu ngulkurr bajaku. Nganjin yunun kuku nyakaku.
Yundu yunduku majamaka bubungu yala yundu maja jiringa.
Nganjinanga mayi daya nyikunku.
Nganjinandamunku buyun-buyunku milka-wulay, yala nganjin buyun-buyunku milka-wulay kanbalda balkan nganjinanda.
Yundu nganjinin nyakaku. Dubu kanbi, nyulu kari milka-bakanka, buyun-damanka. Yunduku majakuda, yunduku junkurrjikuda, yunduku dayirr-dayirrkuda. Yalakubada.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
