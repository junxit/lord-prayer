Kosorong

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Uumeleembaa Buŋa Tere Soomoŋgo Gbilia (ksr), © 2002, 2011 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Nonoo Maŋnana, gii Siwenoŋ laligojaŋ. Goo qaga mono toroya ewa.
Mono galeŋ koma nonona bentotoŋgaa hoŋa koloowa. Goo uusiiŋga Siwenoŋ teŋ komakejuti, ii mono namonoŋ kaaŋiadeeŋ koloowa.
Ketewaa nenenana ii mono sonananoŋ nonomba.
Tosia yoŋoonoŋ tosa nonoonoŋ eji, nono ii mesaoŋ oŋonjoŋi eeŋ, gii mono iikawaa so nonoonoŋ tosa soŋgbama nonomba.
Aŋgobatonoŋ kamaaŋ nunuwabotiwaajoŋ mono aŋgoŋ koma nonoma Kileŋaa Toyaa borianoŋga metogoŋ nonomba. (Gii geeŋgo bentotoŋ Toya, ku-usuŋ Toya ano asamararaŋ Toya ii kambaŋa kambaŋa laligoŋ kouma laligona ano tetegoya qaa laligoŋ ubaga. Ii oŋanoŋ.)
