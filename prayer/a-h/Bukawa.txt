=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Anötö ndê Yom Lêŋsêm Wakuc (buk, ISO 639-3 buk), © 2000 Lutheran Bible Translators Australia, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Damaŋ, am naŋ mbo undambê. Am nem ŋaê lau sênem dabuŋ maŋ.
Mweŋ ti lau hoŋ si Pômdau. Kêŋ lau daŋga wambu nem yom ŋapep yêc nom, gitôm yêc undambê.
Kêŋ yac mba gö têŋ ac dindec.
Suc yac mba sac ŋatôp kwi, tôm yac asuc lau naŋ sêkôm sac têŋ yac, naŋ si sac ŋatôp kwi.
Yob yac têŋ lêtôm bu tap yac sa dom, ma têŋ ŋgac sac Sadaŋ bu kôm yac ŋayom dom.
