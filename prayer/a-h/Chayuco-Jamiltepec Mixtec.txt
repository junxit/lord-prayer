=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tuhun Ndyoo sihin tyehen ñi (mihNT, ISO 639-3 mih), © 1979 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ndyoo Zutu ndi, ra ndyaa andɨvɨ, ii xaan cuu zɨvɨ moo.
Nasaa quɨvɨ cua cundyaca ñohon ihya ñuu ñiyɨvɨ.
Nazavaha ñiyɨvɨ cuhva cuñi moo ihya ñuu ñiyɨvɨ tañi cuu si ndya gloria.
Cuhvon sa siñi ñuhu sii ndi minoo minoo quɨvɨ.
Ta zohon tucahnu iñi sii ndi sa cuenda cuatyi ndi tañi maa ndi zaha ndi tucahnu iñi sii ñiyɨvɨ zavaha sa ña vaha sihin ndi.
Ma cuhvon sa nducu coto ihñi cuihna sii ndi coto zavaha ndi sa ña vaha.
Vatyi moo cuu ra ndyaca ñaha, ta moo cuu ra iyo xaan tundyee iñi sii, ta tahan si zacahnu tandɨhɨ ñiyɨvɨ suun tandɨhɨ cuii tyiemvu. Ta nacuu si sa ndisa. Tacuan cahan ndo sihin ra Ndyoo.
