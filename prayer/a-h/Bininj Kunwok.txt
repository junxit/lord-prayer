Gunwinggu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from God Nuye Kunwok (gup), © 2018 Bible Society of Australia, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the opening quotation mark the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Ngabba nawu ngadberre, yihni kore heaven, ngarridi yiwarrudj kore kunngey ke, bu ngundidjalngeyburlume munguyh nawu bininj rowk.
Ngarridjare bu ngudda Kanmarnewohrnan munguyh. Kaluk ngarridjare kamkurduyimerran kore ngudda yidjare, yiman kore heaven, dja mak kondah kurorre.
Dja mak kanwo manme ngadberre bu bolkkime munguyh.
Dja mak yibengmidjda ngadberre kunwarre, yiman bu ngad ngarrbenbengmidjdan bedberre birribuyika bininj nawu ngandimarnekurduyimeng kunwarre.
Mak kanbidyikarrmen ngadberre, ba kunwarre minj ngarrikurduyime, dja mak kannahna warridj wardi ngandjurrkkan ngadberre nawu namarnde duninjh.’” Kuhni wanjh Jesus benmarneyimeng.
