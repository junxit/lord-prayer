Aymar aru

=== Traditional ===
Nanakan Awkixa, alaxpachankirixa,
sutimax yäupaychatäpan.
Qhapaq kankañamax nanakar jutpan,
munañamasti luratäpan, kamisatix alaxpachana, ukhamaraki akapachana.
Sapüru tʼantʼa jichhüru churapxita.
Juchanakatsti pampachapxarakita,
kamisatix nanakaxa juchachasirurux pampachapkta ukhama.
Janiraki watiqaru tinkuñsa jaytapxistati,
ñanqhanakata qhispiyapxarakita.

Jumankiwa qhapaq kankañasa, chʼamasa, jachʼa kankañasa wiñayan wiñayapataki. Ukhamäpana.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.

(Traditional = the Catholic liturgical "Nanakan Awkixa." The doxology is supplied in the ecumenical/Bible-society form, modernized into unified Aymara orthography: "Jumankiwa qhapaq kankañasa, chʼamasa, jachʼa kankañasa wiñayan wiñayapataki. Ukhamäpana." / "For thine is the kingdom, the power and the glory, for ever and ever. Amen." Older Spanish-influenced Bolivian Bible spelling renders the same with jj/c/qu.)
