Fóósun Chuuk

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Paipel (chk), © 1984, 1989, 1991, 2001 Liebenzel Mission, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Semem lon läng.
Itom epwe pin,
Mwum epwe war, letipom epwe fis
won fanüfan usun chök lon läng.
Kopwe ngenikem anem mongö ikenai.
Kopwe amusala äm tipis,
usun pwal äm aia amusala chokana mi tipis ngenikem.
Iwe, kosap atolongakem lon sosot,
nge kopwe angasakem seni ewe mi ngau.
[Pun om chök mwü me manaman
me ling feilfeilachök. Amen’]
