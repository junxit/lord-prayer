Vaidida

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Surat Ralan na'a Vaidida (frd), © 2018 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Yamamami, Oa mdoku lanit ratan. Ami amalang Oa, tevek naram a nmerat urun.
Mwa ovu Mu Fareta a ma fwareta ami.
Fara amot-orang afa ovi ralam nfalak a
naꞌa lanit ivavan a, wean lahir i naꞌa lanit ratan a.
Amera ma mala afamtahan verin ami ma naran ma amaꞌan amar ini.
Fara maꞌi vatuk mami salasilan ra, wean ami amabun roak tamata ovi rotu salasilan verin ami.
Deka mtorung ma tamata te nitdawan nlabir teman ami,
ba fwaroa tali ami nitdawan ovu ni siweang.
[Tevek Oa mtaha fareta, Oa Mua ngrebat a, ovu vanam a nfitik nyadat ma ti wol ni wahan. Kena urun!]
