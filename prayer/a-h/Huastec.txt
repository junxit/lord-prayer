=== Traditional ===
[Verified from An it jilchith cau cʼal i Ajatic Jesucristo (husNT1971), © 1971 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

I Tata ti eb i c’ac’nanchal a bij.
Quit chich ca t’aja’ tit lej Ts’ale teje’ tsabal.
Quin t’ajchi jaye an inicchic a culbetal teje’ tsabal jant’ini’ tu lej t’ajnal a culbetal tihua’ ti eb.
Tucu pithanchi qui c’apu jant’ots i yejenchal axe’ xa q’uicha.
Tucu paculanchi i hualab cum i paculanchal jitats tucu t’ajchi i hualabtalab.
Tucu lotocna’ abal yab cu c’ale jun ti cu hualbin.
Tucu jec’ontha’ c’al an teneclab.
Tata’its expith it lej pulic Ts’ale. A cua’al a lej tsap ani a ts’alat axi ne’ets ca ohuey abal ets’ey. Amén.
