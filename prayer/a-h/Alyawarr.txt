[Verified from Alyawarr Bible (aly, ISO 639-3 aly), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Akngey anwekantherrenh, ngarl irrwerl aneyel. Ingkerrenh akalty-antha, anwantherr ngenh arraty iterl-aretyek.
Apey-alha, ingkerrenhek ingkart anetyek. Ingkerrenh arratyila, ngkweng awelhetyek, irrwerl-areny ingkerrenh ngkweng awelheyel-apeny.
Anwenhantherr intem-antey amern antherl-aynta.
Apmwa anwekantherrety iwa, anwernantherr arratyilerretyek, mwerrant-arey anwernantherr anetyek.
Anwernantherr apmwewarl irrekerr atnyena. Arrenty anwernekantherr irrpelthirreyelarl-kety, anwernenhantherr arntarnt-ara.’”
