=== Traditional ===
[Verified from the published Banjar Scripture (Wycliffe / Kulawarga Nabi Isa, 2019/2025), Lukas 11:2-4. No Banjar translation of Matthew 6 exists — that edition contains only Luke and John — so the text below is the shorter Lukan form, verbatim. It lacks "Thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven" and "deliver us from evil".]

Ya Allah, Abah kami nang di atas, kami maakui Pian tu Nang Maha Suci.
Bakuasalah atas kami wan samunyaan urang.
Bariꞌilah kami saban hari makanan nang samayunya.
Ampunilah dusa-dusa kami nang kaya kami mamaapakan urang nang basalah lawan kami.
Jangan mambiarakan kami kalah wayah digudai Iblis.

[No doxology in the Lukan form or in any published Banjar text; omitted rather than composed.]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
[UNVERIFIED — composed, not a published translation. No published Banjar Matthew 6 exists; the lines below mirror the canonical English clause order as a best-effort rendering and need review by a Banjar speaker. Prefer the verified Lukan text above for the petitions it covers.]

Abah kami nang di sruga, Pian tu Nang Maha Suci.
Datanglah karajaan Pian, jadilah kahandak Pian di bumi sama kaya di sruga.
Bariꞌilah kami saban hari makanan nang samayunya.
Ampunilah dusa-dusa kami, nang kaya kami mamaapakan urang nang basalah lawan kami.
Jangan mambiarakan kami digudai Iblis, tagal lapasakanlah kami matan nang jahat.

Karana Pian nang baisi karajaan, kakuasaan, wan kamuliaan, salalawasan. Amin.
