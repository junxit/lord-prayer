=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kitap molinas men ringkat na alaata'ala: perjanjian baru (blzNT), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamamai na surugaa! Bi angga'ion a ngaan-Muu.
Takakon a Batomundo'an-Muu kada' men kikira'-Muu bo lolo'on na tano' balaki' mongololo' koi men na surugaa.
Sulano taraion-Muu i kai kakaan men kosukupmai na ilio koini'i.
Kai miki'ampuni sala'mai koi i kai uga' mangampuni mian men isian sala'na na ko'omai.
Amo' dalaion i kai topongionna upa men mantakakon ba'idek, kasee oloakon i kai na giigii' iya'a. [Gause i Kuu a Tomundo' men mungkuasai ka' angga'ion pataka sidutu. Amin.]’
