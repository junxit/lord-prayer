Kuni

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mbumbukiam-qa Manqat Mbomambaqape (kvg), © 2015 Wycliffe Switzerland, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Nigi Tat, qo yan-te nqoqoto꞉go.
Qoqa iz, yà beto-eqawát.
Qoqa Megeat Matev bètøndowáv ni-te.
Qoqá poev matev mba bambupáev, nqanek manqei-qape-te, ate av-té, yan-te ndimbøe-paev.
Qo nqægo khaiya lou vø̀ni-etoám, av ni lou mbain nqati꞉gu.
Niqa manqa-zapazapa, sà betaqambo-naváp, ate av-té, ni ezoqa nøme-qa manqa-zapazapa sa qanimbo-navupatun, ni-te gimatønumatun.
Qo ni khamasim-te ndø-àmba᷄m. Geté gìnitøkeé, ezoqa soqaqape andé vǿe-ngiænō.’”
