Kurâ

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Deus Itaumbyry (bkqNT), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Pabai kaynonro, tynrenseim lelâlâ mawyly, izedy awyly warâ idânârâ nhutudyzemo xina.
Ânguy âwankuem âepyra nidâ.
Tarâ xina iwymâryem ikâ.
Xina xirâ onro anano modo ize mato ara lelâ xina aiedyse awyly,
târâ kaynâ agonro modo ize mato ara aito ara lelâ.
Âmâlâ kopaelâgâembaba xina ipyniry âundugâ.
Inakanhe xina aitobyry xygakegâ,
âkelomo inakanhe tynynâ aitobyry xina nhenanânehon-ho ara.
Satanás izepa mato aienehonze keduo, xina agâlâ ikâ,
izepa mato xina aniempyra itoem.
[Xina iwymâry âmâ.
Ton-honre kehoem âmâ, amânhedyse mato aiese.
Tynrenseim âmâ.
Aunlolâ ise âmâ, typygueim keba. Arâlâ ise”] — warâ aguewâtaungâ Deusram.
