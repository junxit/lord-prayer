=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ireclota Mene (bon, ISO 639-3 bon), © 1993 Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Cime Babe cabucewe je,
määme miijirage ngii ne opo gaabe me naacitesi.
Maane te biname bine ewademepi.
Määme singi ne gawecewe cabu jaawenesi,
cabucewe je lipu jewenenisi.
Page nicäranini cime bimu gaabegabe deedei.
Maane gone cime niiya mule bine yaawadimute,
cine lipu lica yaawadimusi biname cina lui niiya mule niwenininusi cibibine.
Maane gone cibibine niwadininute mäpurage apicetnära cabu me,
yepä niwärimtipi niiya te oomlana wälu pi.
