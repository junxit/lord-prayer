Qa'yol

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yi Antiw Bible / Yi Ac'aj Testament (aguBl), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Kataj yi atu' tcya'j, wi'nin xanil yi bi'u' Ta'.
Sajku' tzone'j wuxtx'otx' tan cawu'n skibaj.
Cawunku' tzone'j wuxtx'otx', chi na cawunu' tzi'n tcya'j.
Tak' tzaju' kawa' tetz jalu'.
Cuye'u' kapaj, chi na kacuy ketz chipaj yi e' mas wunak.
Quil ko' stzakpninu' le il,
ma na make'u' kawutz tan qui kabene'n tul.
Na ilu' ajcaw tibaj cyakil.
Nin chin cham nin ilu'.
Nin at teru' k'eju' ben k'ej ben sak. Amén.’
