Dinjii Zhuʼ Ginjik

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Vit'eegwijyąhchy'aa Vagwandak Nizįį (gwiNT), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Diiti' zheekat gwizhit Noozhri' shroodiinyąą nilii ts'ą' narahoh'e',
Niveegwiginiin'ee gwik'it gooli' deeyiindhan dzaa nankat ts'ą',
zheekat haa gwik'it gooli'!
Drin gwiteegwaanchy'aa shih tr'ihee'aa diits'an ąhtsii.
Diitr'agwaanduu diineejit oo'an gwihinlii,
ąįįts'ą' juu diits'ą' tr'agwaanduu t'ee'ya' nąįį chan gwik'it gooveenjit oo'an gwireheelyaa.
Ąįįts'ą' tr'agwaanduu t'agǫǫ'ya' gwiindhan gwits'į' an-diinoaan'ee,
ąįį tr'aanduu diits'ą' gwiizųų t'ihee'yaa ąįį gwits'į' diik'aahtii.’
