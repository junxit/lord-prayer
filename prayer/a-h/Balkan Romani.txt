Romani čhib (Arli)

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from I Biblija ki Arli romani čhib Srbija (rmna), © 2025 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Dade amareja, kova injan ano nebo,
to sveto anav barjardo te ovel.
To carstvo te avel.
Ti volja te ovel,
sar soi ano nebo, ađahar te ovel hem ki phuv.
De amen avdive o maro savo valjani amenđe svako dive
hem oprostin amenđe amare grehija sar so hem amen oprostinaja okolenđe kola grešinde premala amende.
Hem cide amen taro iskušenje, hem oslobodin amen e Bišukarestar.
[Adalese soi to carstvo, to zoralipe hem ti slava zauvek.]’

Same as the Traditional form above.
