Diꞌidzaꞌ xidzaꞌ

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Xtiʼidzaʼ Diuzi (zpcNT), © 1986 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Xuzindoꞌ Diuzi zuloꞌ guibá, naca laꞌiya laoloꞌ.
Rbezandoꞌ guida dza inabëꞌloꞌ lao yedyi layu cabëꞌ rnabëꞌloꞌ guibá ga zuloꞌ.
Gunoꞌ ta gaondoꞌ quie naꞌadza cabëꞌ ta rdzioguendoꞌ.
Beziꞌz̃e quiendoꞌ cabëꞌ tamala babëndoꞌ cabëꞌ reziꞌz̃endoꞌ tamala bë ja benëꞌ len nëtoꞌ.
Gacalaoꞌ nëtoꞌ ta biiguinondoꞌ lao tamala, ucuasa ucuiꞌoloꞌ tamala zaꞌ gaca quiendoꞌ.
Porque tuzoꞌ luëꞌ nacoꞌ ga zelaozi yelaꞌ rnabëꞌ quioꞌ, ga zelaozi yelaꞌ huaca quioꞌ, ga zelaozi yelaꞌ z̃e quioꞌ tuzioli. Amén.
