Bariba

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Bibeli Gusunɔn Gari (bba), © 2013 SIM, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

bɛsɛn Bɑɑbɑ wi u wɑ̃ɑ wɔllɔ, ɑ de bɑ n yɛ̃ mɑ wunɑ sɑ̃ɑ Dɛɛro.
A nɑ ɑ bɑndu swĩi.
A de bu wunɛn kĩru ko tem sɔɔ mini, nɡe mɛ bɑ mɔ̀ wɔllɔ.
A sun bɛsɛn ɡisɔn dĩɑnu kɛ̃.
A sun bɛsɛn torɑnu suuru kuo, nɡe mɛ be bɑ rɑ sun tore, sɑ bu suuru kuɑmmɛ.
A ku kɑ sun dɑ kɔkiribun berɑ ɡiɑ.
Adɑmɑ ɑ sun wunɑ sɑɑ tɔn kɔ̃son nɔmun di.
[Domi wunɑ ɑ bɑndu kɑ dɑm kɑ yiiko mɔ sere kɑ bɑɑdommɑɔ. Ami.]
