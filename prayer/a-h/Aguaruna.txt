Awajún

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Awajún [Aguaruna] (agr), © 2020 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“ ‘Iina Apajiyah, amek nayaimpinum pujuwaitme.
Pegkegmaitam ibau asam imá ameketme ememattaim.
Amek jutiishkam inajatjata.
Ame wakegamun nayaimpinmaya umigtamaina nunisag ju nugka juiyash aminak umigtamkatnume.
Dutiksamek ii yuwatnushkam kashí kashinig sukagtita.
Nuigtú ii tudaujishkam tsagkugkagtugta,
jutii tikich pegkegchau awajtamush tsagkujuinag aikasmek.
Pegkegchaun takastinme tusamek idaikagtutsuk, ayatak yaijatkam agkanmitkagtuata’, tiuwai.

Same as the Traditional form above.
