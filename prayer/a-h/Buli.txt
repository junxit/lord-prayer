Kanjaga

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Buli (bwu), © 2003 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ti Ko Naawen, fi waai ale bo wenŋmazuk la,
Basi ate fi yoi weleŋka a yiti ate fi nya zula.
Fi naamu abe jam, ate fi ale a yaali dii la, a nyɛ tɛŋzuk de, ase ku le ka dii wenŋmazuk la.
Te ti jinla de ŋandiin tii ate ti a yaali la.
Voŋ ti wabaata a te ti ase tama ale voŋ a te chaab dii la.
Kan basi ate ti jo nyenyaka po, abe fi vari ti Velinvendika jigi a basi.’
