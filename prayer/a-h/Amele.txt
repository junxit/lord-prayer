Sona

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from BAL CEHEC JE HAUN (aey), © 1997 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘O ege Memige, hina sao jobon biligina,
hina ijain gun biliale.
Hinana cul egeca hoiale.
Hina con toodu odoc sao jobon ihoc lenawe
odi mahana ha ihoc leiale.
Sab jecnu ba i ihoc igigale.
Dana age ege me qee odigeinnu ege dahinige celenwe
hinaha egena me qee odadec eunu dahin celeiale,
Hina ege temgecna cain ehi li migigaun.
Euqa qisol mec kobol eu cagasigagale,’ doigale,” aden.
