Toka-Leya-Dombe

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dombe New Testament (dov), © 2020 Door43 World Missions Community, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

'Taata wesu ooli kujulu, alilemekwe izina lyako, abuze bwami bwako.
Aluchitwe luyando lwako, ansi aano mbubonya mbuli kujulu.
Utupe sunu zilyo zitwelede.
Utulekelele milandu yesu, mbubonya mbuli mbutu balakelela aswe bali amilandu kuli ndiswe.
Utatwenzyi mukutempawulwa. Utuvune kumubi, nkambo bwami mbubwako anguzu, abulemu, mane kukabe kutamani. Ameni.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
