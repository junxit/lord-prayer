Akan (Twi)

=== Traditional ===
Yɛn Agya a wowɔ soro,
Wo din ho nte,
W'ahennie mmra,
Deɛ wopɛ nyɛ wɔ asase so, sɛdeɛ ɛyɛ wɔ ɔsoro.
Ma yɛn yɛn daa aduane nnɛ,
Na fa yɛn aka kyɛ yɛn, sɛdeɛ yɛde kyɛ wɔn a wɔde yɛn aka.
Na mfa yɛn nkɔ sɔhwɛ mu, Na yi yɛn firi bɔne mu.

Na wo na ahennie ne ahoɔden ne animuonyam yɛ wo dea daa. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
