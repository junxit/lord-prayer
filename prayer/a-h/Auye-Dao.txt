=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ogatamee Me Etokaa Gipii See Menaa (auu, ISO 639-3 auu), © 2020-2022 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Epoo to wodoo Eboo, Iniitaagi,
A ekaa da A too ebo kaekee.
A too ebo mée eetoowoo taatage kuwa mani mée yamiyaai.
A me gaata-gaata kuwa too epoo to wodoo miya daamaa kaukuwawookea see see,
magaa da kaa kiyoo miya kisee daamaa kaukuwawoosi yoka mée yaagoo kiyaai.
Eto kenaagoo naano agiyoo sikii-sikii mée manii.
Inii pedeo mée kiyaata bagee yuwa inii me epa ekeasiyaa see see,
A me miya inii me pedeo yuwa epa mée yaekeasii.
Bedaa inii mée gaadee see yoo mée peeyaakee se kiya
paa me agiyoo kuwa beesekaa mée yaabagaai.
[Aa ko ebo too toowoo see Mée, Se-pesee, We-pesee yago Mée, asii-sege see Mée kisee too iminoo miya, iminoo miya. Amin.]
