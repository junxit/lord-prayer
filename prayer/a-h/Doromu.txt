Doromu-Koki

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Sei di Uka Ago Ruaka (kqc), © 2011-2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Uni Baba omari ameisa amiye:
Yi roka bi kikifa iniye;
yi ourefeidena amena sana bi deyaine;
yi ura bi konori vata niyaine, omari kana.
Gua meda di iruku una mai baesa, mina una ma.
Ma uni vegu no koite maku,
ma una ka amiye di vegu no una rofu rededi moi erufusifa kana.
Ma uka maina regika nivena rofu ga una moi ourefeide,
idu amiye no rofu una moi vegu ri.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
