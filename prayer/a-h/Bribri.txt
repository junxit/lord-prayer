Su Uhtuk

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tte Pa̱'a̱li̱ Me' Skëköl tö Se' a̱ (bzdNTpo), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘A Sa' Yë́ tso' ká̱ jaì a̱,
a̱s be' dalöiö̀ sa' tö ì ulitane tsa̱ta̱.
A̱s be' dö̀ sa' blúie da'a̱ie.
Ì kiane be' ki̱ a̱s e' o̱r ká̱ i' ki̱
we̱s io̱rke ká̱ jaì a̱ es.
Chkè kiane sa' ki̱ i̱'ñe se̱noie e' mú sa' a̱.
Sa' nuì̱ olo'yö́ sa' ki̱
we̱s sa' tö s'male̱pa ki̱ inuì̱ olo'yeke es.
Kë̀ sa' marttsa̱ tö ì sulu tö sa' e̱rkiöù̱,
e' skéie sa' tsa̱tkö́ yi dör sulu ichö́k e' yöki̱.’
