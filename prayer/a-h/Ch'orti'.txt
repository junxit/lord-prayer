Ch'orti'

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Uyojroner e Dios xeꞌ Tzꞌijbꞌabꞌir Tama e Onian Tiempo (caa), © 1996, 2012, 2013, 2023 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc. and Evangel Bible Translators, International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Catata Dios xeꞌ turet tichan,
catattzꞌi acꞌabꞌa xeꞌ erach.
Lar tuaꞌ icꞌotori tara tor e rum wacchetaca.
Y chen lo que acꞌani tara tor e rum bꞌan cocha war ache tichan tut e qꞌuin.
Ajcꞌunon lo que ucꞌani tuaꞌ cacꞌuxi tama inteꞌ inteꞌ día.
Cꞌumpen tacaron tamar camabꞌambꞌanir lo que cay cache toit net, bꞌan cocha war cacꞌumpa taca tin e cay uchiobꞌ e mabꞌambꞌanir capater ubꞌan.
Ira awacton tuaꞌ capijchna sino que corpeson tama tunor ucꞌotorer e diablo. Porque net jax Careyet, y net ayan meyra acꞌotorer, y net ayan meyra atawarer xeꞌ machi tuaꞌ acꞌapa. Amén.
