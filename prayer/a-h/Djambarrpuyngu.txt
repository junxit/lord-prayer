Djambarrpuyŋu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from God-Waŋarrwu Walŋamirr Dhäruk (djr), © 2008 Bible Society of Australia, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Bäpa ŋanapurruŋ, ŋunhi nhe ga nhina dhuwaliyi djiwarr'ŋur,
Nheny yäku yindi warray, ga wokthunamirr.
Napurr nhuna ga dhuwal ŋäŋ'thundja nhe dhu dhanuŋdhunna nhuŋuwuy nhe God-Waŋarrwuny rom ŋayaŋulila ŋanapurruŋgal,
märr ŋanapurr dhu ga djämany dhiyalnydja munatha'ŋurnydja wäŋaŋur nhokiyingalaŋuwurra yan djälkurrnydja, bitjan yan bili nhakun walal ŋuli ga djäma dhiyaliyi djiwarr'ŋur nhokiyingalaŋuwurr yan djälkurr.
Ŋatha ŋanapurruny maranhu-gurrupul dhiyak bala waluw.
Bäy-lakaraŋun ŋanapurruŋ yätjkurruwuynydja djämapuy,
biyak yan bili nhakun napurr ŋuli ga bäy-lakaram ŋurikiwurruŋ ŋunhi wiripuwurruŋ yolŋu'-yulŋuw ŋunhi walal ŋuli ŋanapurruny ŋayaŋu-yätjam.
Yaka ŋanapurruny gäŋu gumurr-ḏälkurrnydja romgurr birrka'yunarawnydja.
Gunguŋ ŋanapurruny, märr ŋanapurruny dhu bäyŋun yätjkurruynydja birrimbirryu djuḻkmaram, ŋunhi napurruny ŋayi ŋuli marrtji guwaḻ-ṉirr'maramany.’ Ga bitjanna nhuma dhu ga ŋunhi bukumirriyamany nhanukalnydja God-Waŋarrwalnydja.”
