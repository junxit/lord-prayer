Dizhze

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Wdizh kwaan nyaadno yalnaban (zpoNT), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Xuz na, xaa nzob naz yiba,
nzhekla na wii re myet lo lu nel nambey doo lu.
Nzhekla na yaad lu nabeyy lu lo yizhyo ne
modxa nabeyy lu naz leen yiba.
Zaa kwaan gaw na nalzhe.
Bchee rese jwalt kwaan mle na,
modxa myenlazh na re jwalt kwaan mle re wechxinn na lo na.
Naleyat lu na la na lo re kwaan nawent
sinka bkanap na lo Maxuu.

Same as the Traditional form above.
