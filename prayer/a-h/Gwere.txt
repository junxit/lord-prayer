lugwere

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from ENdagaano eMpyaka oMu Lugwere (gwr), © 2017 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Itewaiswe aali omwigulu, eriinalyo baliwe ekitiisya,
obwakabakabwo bwize.
Ebyotaka babikole oku kyalo
ngʼowebabikola omwigulu.
Otuwe nabuli lunaku ekyokulya ekitwetaaga.
Era otusoniye ebibbibibbi byaiswe,
nga swena owetusoniya
abatukola ebitulumya.
Era tiwatuleka okugwa omu bikemo,
wazira otulamye eeri omubbimubbi.
[Ibwo obwakabaka, nʼobwezye nʼekitiisya bibyo emirembe nʼemirembe. Amiina].’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
