Bakovi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from A Nitana Vure (bnp), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamane mia meli na hunu,
mi ngaru e huriki ri gi togoa a ramu,
a harikianga vomu ge valai.
A ningaru vomu ni gi
ramai koea na malala, i manga a hini ni rata meli na hunu.
O habi a maki ni kani ne mia ge karea a parava mona.
O puga vuroki a naro hale ne mia,
i manga a hini mi puga a naro hale ne huriki ri rata hale mia.
Naha ni tuli mia mi gi dili na nitoni.
O korimule mia na bakovi hale.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
