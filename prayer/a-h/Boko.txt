Bokonya

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Bibeli Luayãtaalá (bqcsim), © 2010 SIM International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Wá Mae pↄ́ kú musu,
to wà n tↄ́ kuaadoakɛ dↄ̃,
to kpala pↄ́ bↄ̀ n kĩ́i bↄ gupuau,
wiↄ n pↄeã kɛ dṹnia guu lá wì kɛ n kĩ́iwa.
Wá gba ú pↄ́ wá ble gbã.
Wá tàaeↄ kɛ̃́wá,
lá wi gbɛ̃́ pↄ́ tàaekɛ̀wɛ̃ɛↄ kɛ̃wa.
Ńsu to wà fu yↄ̃awao,
wá kɛ̃́ vãiwa.
