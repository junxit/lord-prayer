Coatlán Mixe

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from El Nuevo Testamento en mixe de Coatlán (mcoNT, ISO 639-3 mco), © 1976 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Dios Teedy, miich huɨdibɨ tzajpjoty tzɨnaayb; huindzɨgɨ̈y miich mxɨɨ y'ity.
Huen miñ ya miich yɨ m'ane'mɨn. Huen mdzojcɨn yajcuyduñ ma je jäy yjot yhuinma'ñ jadu'n nej yajcuyduñ jiiby tzajpjoty.
Mooygɨch ajcxy ngaayɨn n'uucɨn jabom jabom.
Méecxɨbɨch ajcxy nbocy ngaydey huɨdibɨ ɨɨch ajcxy ndundɨgooyb, jadu'n nej ɨɨch ajcxy meecxyɨch huɨdibɨ ajcxy xymɨdundɨgooybɨch.
Ca' m'a'ɨxɨ̈y co ɨɨch ajcxy nhuinma'ñ tɨgoyɨpɨch. Yajnïhuaatzcɨch ajcxy ma yɨ jɨbɨcpɨ. Co miich yɨ' mje' je cötujcɨn, je mɨc'ajtɨn etz je oy'ajtɨn huinɨxɨɨ huinɨtiempo. Amén.
