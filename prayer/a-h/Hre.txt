=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Hset Hadròeh (hrebt, ISO 639-3 hre), © 2024 Baq Thi, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Waiq Baq nhèn oe ta plình; Yiniq Baq jah hadròeh;
Diac Baq jah trùh; Manoh Baq jah gêh, oe ta crŏng taneh tìah ka oe ta plình!
Am ka nhèn dahwèq acaq qnang lem ka hì kô;
Cađac da nhèn yiniq lui, qmang ka nhèn cađac ka mangai broq yiniq lui ka nhèn;
Paq dôq nhèn clìh ta kan padô qnùt, dèch nhèn claih enh kan dù! (Majah qmang aih: diac, kan itai, kan ranhòm, oe dìq ta Baq mòeq đòe.)

Same as the Traditional form above.
