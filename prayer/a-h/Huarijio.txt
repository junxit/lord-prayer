Warihó

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Riosia Warijio Nayewani Ahpo Tisiwa Kaaweruma Tuuyewari (var), © 2014 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Taamo Noono tewekachi kahtiame,
tísia iyoriwa taamo teerewia.
Ikanati enajerani teemea, aamo ye'kario, taamo mochikachi weeechio;
Wa'achi teerejerani teemea aamo e'rao, i'wa weeechiopua tewekachi tiiaopua tiame.
Senepi taawechi taamo oowepuapua, taamo nakisiao.
Taamo cheriwejerani teemea, taamo ki kaawe nokariao, taamoo aaata taamo na'aria, cheriwiachitia.
Muuua taamo ku'imitiapua ki ruhjimichioopua ki ka'karumachio,
ohso kaawe taamo neesetomitia muuua, ki taamo ruhjimichioopua, te'ta taamo seepurawa Satanasichio.
Yooma i'ka no'o aamo chaaniao, aamoo te'ta yomatiame ye'kari Teekorakopapua, amochiopua tiameo, tísia utewariemekopapua, amochiopua yomatiame te'ta iyoriwao, amochi tiiamekopapua, ahkasi ki wahjiamesiopua amochio. Wa'asi, wa'a teerejeriamenia amochio.
