Ñomndaa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ñʼoom xco na tqueⁿ tyʼo̱o̱tsʼom cantxja ʼnaaʼ Jesucristo (amuNT), © 1999 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tsotya̱a̱yâ na mꞌaaⁿꞌ cañoomꞌluee,
ñequiiꞌcheⁿ catseitꞌmaaⁿꞌñenaꞌ xueꞌ.
Candyo cantyja na matsa̱ꞌntjomꞌ.
Caluii cantyja na lꞌue tsꞌomꞌ nnom tsjoomnancue chaꞌxjeⁿ na macwiluii cañoomꞌluee.
Quiaaꞌ nantquie ꞌnaⁿꞌ na macaⁿnaꞌ jâ xuee jeꞌ.
Catseitꞌmaⁿ tsꞌomꞌ jâ natia na jnda̱ lꞌaayâ chaꞌxjeⁿ jâ cwilatꞌmaⁿ nꞌo̱o̱ⁿyâ nnꞌaⁿ na cwilaꞌtjo̱o̱ndye nda̱a̱yâ.
Cateijndeiꞌ na ticjaachuunaꞌ jâ na nlꞌaayâ yuu na ticatsa̱ꞌntjomnaꞌ.
Ndoꞌ catseicandyaandyuꞌ jâ cantyja ꞌnaaⁿꞌ juu natia.
Ee chaꞌwaa na yo na cwii wjaanaꞌ ꞌu cwiluiindyuꞌ na matsa̱ꞌntjomꞌ, ndoꞌ cwiluiindyuꞌ najnduꞌ,
ndoꞌ tseixmaⁿꞌ na catseitꞌmaaⁿꞌñenaꞌ ꞌu. Amén.
