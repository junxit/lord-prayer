Banna

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Haalin Mallano (amf), © 2014 SIM International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Cecinte dahaa Imbaa wontea,
nammo hasa ko gazisadhe.
Bitamono hanno ko nive.
Bhocchino hasa cecinte konin maatendettsi,
peentear yin ko maateka.
Kina rooroana wona zasken galan wona imaa.
Woam seesaynana wonin garendettsi,
wonnon seesonamaar wona ya garaa.
Siyondar arsota taysayno wodar konin nivoamadettsi woam gishaa.
Siyonkalanka woam dhacchaa.
[Bitamonbee, kantanbee, gobshinbe lay hantine; amiin.]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
