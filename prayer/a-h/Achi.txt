Achiʼ

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from I 'utz laj tzij re i dios (acr-acc), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ka Kajaw chicaj, chocsax u ʼij a bi.
Chapetsax a ʼatbal tzij,
cha ʼana na waral chuwach i jyub taʼaj, xak jun ruʼ pacha i ca ʼani chila chicaj.
Cha ya na chake wach i caka tijo, wach i rajwaxic chake.
Cha cuyu ni ka mac, xak jun ruʼ pacha ka ʼanom yoj, chi ka cuyum ni qui mac niʼpa i je macun-nak chikij.
Ma ya chake coj tzak chupam i mac atzalal, xui-ri choj awesaj puʼab itzel.
Man yet ʼo i ʼatbal tzij paʼab; nim a choʼab, xak nim a ʼij a chomal.
At queje ile queʼe ʼij sak chawij. Amen.
