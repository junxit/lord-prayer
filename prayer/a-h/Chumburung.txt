=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Abware̱se̱ŋ Wo̱re̱-ɔ (ncuNT, ISO 639-3 ncu), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ane̱ se̱ Wuribware̱ ne̱ fo̱ bo̱ so̱so̱‑ɔ,
sa a bo̱ kyo̱rɔ fo̱ ke̱nyare̱ timaa.
Na fo̱ baa fo̱ kuwure‑o bo̱ gyi mfe̱e̱.
Na fo̱ sa a fo̱ ke̱pre̱ waa swe̱e̱re̱ se̱ mfe̱e̱,
fe̱yɛ ane̱ŋ ne̱ ke̱ e̱ waa so̱so̱‑ɔ.
Sa ane̱ ateese ne̱ a tiri ane̱
ndɔɔ na kake ke̱maa‑ɔ.
Na fo̱ taa ane̱ e̱bɔye̱ bo̱ ke ane̱,
fe̱yɛ ane̱ŋ ne̱ bo̱ko̱ a waa e̱bɔye̱ bo̱ kye ane̱,
ne̱ ane̱ a taa bo̱ ke bamo̱‑ɔ.
Na fo̱ ma sa kpa a Ɔbɔnsam kyɔ ane̱‑rɔ ke̱e̱
fe̱yɛ ane̱ e̱ waa e̱bɔye̱ aaa.
Na fo̱ lee ane̱ le̱e̱ ɔkyɔrɔpo̱ a ke̱sare̱e̱‑ɔ‑rɔ.
