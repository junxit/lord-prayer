Nunashimi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Teyta Diospa Mushoq Testamentun (qwhNT), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Sielucho quecaq Dios Yayalläcuna,
jutillequi allapa respetuwan alabashqa catsun.
Llapan nunacuna mandadiquicho catsun.
¡Sielucho caqcuna qam mandaconqequita rurayanqannolla,
que patsachopis nunacuna rurayätsun!
Miquilläcuna cada junaq qoycalläyämë.
Noqacuna mana alli rurayämaqnïcunata perdonayanqänollä,
jutsalläcunapita perdonecalläyämë.
Jutsa rureman diablu ishquitsiyämeta munaptin
ama jaqiralläyämëtsu;
antis yanapecalläyämë jutsaman mana ishquilläyänäpaq.
˻¡Mandacoq queniqui, puedeq queniqui y shumaq queniqui wiñepa wiñenin catsun!˼ Tseno catsun, nir, mañacuyanqui.
