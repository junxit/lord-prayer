=== Traditional ===
[Verified from TUR GEWASIN O BAIBASIT BOUBUN (aai, ISO 639-3 aai), © 2009 Wyclyffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamai maramaim,
O wab i kakafiyin.
A aiwob tan,
tafaramamaim a kok atasinaf,
maramaim tisisinaf na’atube,
Biyai ana fair isan mar etei ai bay initi.
Ai kakafih kunotawiyen,
taituwai hai kakafin anonotawiyen na’atube.
Men routobonamaim inanawiyi’imih,
baise kakafihine inatar fafari, a aiwob, a fair, a marakaw nama wanatowan, Amen .’
