Wagap

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tii Iitihi: Élele Iitihi na mwo Coho (cam), © 2017-2018 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Caa teme jeda he miiden, wâé heme paciitihi a niim.
Wâé heme e âbé a Mwametau tem. Wâé heme pwo ana te nimem kon ne éni pwo bwohemwo, me pwohewii ne jeda he miiden.
Go ne teme ce wiinaado celi jan ne kome ati he ni tan.
Go pineuhi ni ta pwo teme, pwohewiin he geme pineuhi ni ta na lé pwo teme.
Go nemwo nekeme he ni tacebwén. Go celuimikeme koja a ta. {Be te etem a Mwametau, me a junuum me *a wâé tem, dieli mwo. Amen!}”

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
