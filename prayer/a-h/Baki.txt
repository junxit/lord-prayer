=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Verikariano Vou Na Baki (bki, ISO 639-3 bki), © 2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Karamememi napano jau kojoa vio mave,
memedu ga ka tomu nalo atu avatove ka siamo lu.
Kumemi nubolkouo ka kanamo navenatuboiano rimei tumolu tu sikili ea vio yetemeriba nei,
ka mia tomu nalo alaio tavukia ga napano jau kojidomio,
tibe ka napano nalo na vio mave ajum̃a amilaio.
Aiou, jau kesian van kumemi sememi sinaniano, banei a ea legiano nonovio.
A jau koviekokoa kumemi rivior kenememi kariano nalo,
tibe ka kumemi numitum̃a nubiekokoa tomu dolu nalo napano amila vite jorokor ban kumemi.
A jau kajamo ka re vite va nalo na ajukujuku kumemi m̃ele,
a kovuru bulag kumemi den sim̃aro napano jidom rila lelan kumemi.’
