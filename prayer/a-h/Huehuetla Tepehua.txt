Lhiimaqalhqama7

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ixchivinti Dios (teeNT), © 1976 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Quimpai ju vilhchi' lact'iyan
chach tac'atsa ju lapanacni ni na salaka'at ju uxint'i.
Chach chilh ju avilhchan acsni alhinona' ju uxint'i.
Chach navicalh ju mink'achat ju ani lacat'un tachi ju navicantacha ju lact'iyan.
Aquilaxtakniuch ju chavai ju vaiti ju icmaktasq'uiniyau.
Aquilamac'acxaniuch ju quintalak'alhin quinc'an ni vachu' ic'amac'acxanitauch ju quintanavitan tu'u' macxcai.
Jantu aquilalhi'au junta acmapast'ac'anicau tu'u' macxcai.
Alai aquilamak'osuniuch ju macxcai.
Pus uxint'i ju lhina'un chai uxint'i ju alin ju mintachaput chai uxint'i ju lak'ayac'an tus va tavananch. Slivasalh chunch ju unit'a. Chunch ju anona'it'it acsni at'ach'ivinina'it'it ju Dios.
