=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Godna mo Awai mona go (amn-amanab, ISO 639-3 amn), © 2001 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Kagelni Api ne heven la ga sama el neana unehlagim fingi fini.
Neana bog meg ma bloi,
neana sawieg asa heven la pugug
asa ma hwa bite la gwenaig ese.
Enag osuna sa kam fane faiba sa ma gwese gofi.
Asa ne kagelni amtakwalig mo esmunagim huguflaitini asa kagel gwenaig esig
el kagelim amtakwalig gonug asa kagel ehengelim esmunagim huguflaiti gonug.
Nofe ne kam fwati ma kam yuhnag pugunibi
eba sa kam amtakwalig la nai lambim.
