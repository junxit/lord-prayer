Senagi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Godɨndɨ Hoafɨ (agg), © 2001 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Yɨhoefɨ Ape sünambe amarɨfɨ,
sɨhafɨ ndürɨ ranahambo moanɨ ahɨnümbɨ hamɨndɨmbeyowamboane.
Sɨhafɨ ŋgɨnɨndɨ hɨfandarandɨ ra ndühɨ mbokoso-amboane.
Se hohoanɨmoayafɨ süŋgu yaŋgɨrɨ hɨfɨ ndanühɨ süŋgufemboane
sapo sünambe-amboanɨ süŋguarundɨ nou.
Yɨhoefɨmbo sesɨ ndanɨ sɨmboanɨ sesɨmbo sɨmogodühɨ ndawamunɨ.
Se yɨhoefɨmbo nɨne-moatükunɨ moaruwai ramarɨhundɨ ranɨmbo amboawiyei mbɨsafɨmunɨ
sapo ro nindou ai moaruwai yɨhoefɨmbo rarɨhimuna aheimbo amboawiyei sefɨ arɨhundɨ nou.
Se yɨhoefɨmbo refe hoefimunɨ moatükunɨ ranambe ndowandɨfɨmunɨ ŋgamboyafɨ,
ŋga se yɨhoefɨmbo moaruwai nendɨ ahandɨ warambeahɨndɨ aboedühɨ ndowandɨfɨ hɨnɨŋgɨndowamunɨ,’ mbɨsɨmo.
