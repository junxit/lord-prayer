=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Farongoe Lea Ana Fataleka (far, ISO 639-3 far), © 2017 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Maa kalu 'i langi 'ae,
Kalu doria wane ki tafau kera ka fabaita satamu, sulia nia 'e abu 'asiana.
Dao mai ana gwaungaie 'oe fafia maurilana wane ki tafau.
Ma kwaidoria 'oe kera kai sasia 'i ano dia laugo ne kera sasia 'i langi.
'Oe kwatea mai fua malu na fanga ne bobola fuana maedangi ne.
'Oe manatalugea ta'alae kalu ki, dia laugo ne kalu manatalugea ta'alae wane ki tafau ne kera fulia ru ta'a ki fua malu.
'Oe nao si ala'ani kalu fuana ilitoe ka liufi kalu.
Ma 'oko susi kalu fasia sa Saetan.’
