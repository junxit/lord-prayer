Chuj

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from A Ch'an Biblia D'a Chuj San Mateo (cac), © 2007 Sociedad Bíblica de Guatemala, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ach co Mam, ay ach ec' d'a satchaan̈. Nivanocab' yelc'och a b'i.
Ach ochocab' yajalil d'a tic. C'anab'ajaxocab' tas tza nib'ej d'a sat luum tic, icha tzach c'anab'ajax d'a satchaan̈.
Ac' tas sco va d'a c'u tic.
Ac' lajvoc co mul yujto a on̈ tic scac' nivanc'olal eb' tz'och smul d'ayon̈.
Man̈ on̈ a cha montajb'at d'a chucal.
Tzon̈ a colanel d'a yol sc'ab' vin̈ chuc. Yujto masanil tiempo Yajal achn̈ej. Te nivan a poder. Te nivan elc'ochi. Amén.
