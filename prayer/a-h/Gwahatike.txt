Dahating

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Al Kuruŋyen Mere Igiŋ (dah), © 2000 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Adoniniŋ, gebe kuruŋ wor po.
Hugiŋeŋ turuŋ girde hitek.
Gere alya bereya doyaŋ yird yird mata goyen kawan forok irayiŋ.
Irdeb dufayger meteŋ teŋ gote igineŋ ge hayende gor igiŋ wor po forok yirde hayen gwahade goyen po,
megen gayen wor forok yirde hayiŋ.
Irdeb haŋkayen biŋgebe gwaha mat nene ep nenayiŋ yeŋbe gwahade po duneŋ hayiŋ.
Irdeb al hoyaŋ beleŋ buluŋ dirke mata buluŋmiŋ halde yuneŋ hityen gwahade goyen po,
mata buluŋniniŋ halde duneŋ hayiŋ.
Irdeb dubul tike tuŋaŋ buluŋ bana ma kattek.
Irde Al Buluŋ haniŋde mat dumulgaŋ tiyayiŋ.
