Gwarayú

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tũpa Ñehengagüer (gyrNT), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ore Ru Tũpa, ɨvave ereico vahe, imboeteiprɨ nde rer tasecoi.
Tou nde mborerecuasa oreu.
Tayaposa nde remimbotar co ɨvɨ pɨpe, inungar ɨvave yaposa.
Embou ore rembihu arɨ yacatundar oreu co arɨ pɨpe.
Iyavei ndeñero ore angaipa rese oreu inungar ore oreñero ore amotarẽhɨmbar upe.
Ore mboangaipa ucai rene eve; eipeha mbahe naporai vahe ore sui’ peyera.
