Notu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from BINGA EWAMEI IESU KERISO DA JAWO DA (nou), © 1987 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Mamo nangoda utu da atima esa awa embo berari mi iso jawo jasiga ore. Nango imo burigi burigi bugudo gitijigasase dipapa era. Utu da iso wasiri era da awodo embo busu da awa imo ungo ro orase dipapa esa ore.
Nangoda indari boroko da awa inono da itigae.
Nangoda buria awa kotembedo dogae edo da awodo nango embo nenei da buria nangoda yei awa kotembedo dodo ore.
Edo nango bawari da kundo yiose ta ko nango biae da yeorase soregegae. Imo awa ewamei edo inemi busu da embo berari awa simba edo esa.’
