Bareji

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from God Da Gaga Reka Re (bjz), © 2017 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Afa uutuva vitera, námane inda ragaro
kakara urota, righieta rei-erorue.
Fu, inda natofo* kaifa uradi, namonde irore.
Inda uno uutuva siroreta rouvia aghago, eve endava beago siroroe!
Rifo inono námanda undari bofu!
Námane enemboda ari akokogo námandava eta rousua-nu,
nundubea gia dota rosora aghagonu
námanda ari akokogo beago, nundubea gia do!
Rourogo doadi-gea, kuvia gariva igigare-degea reisi-rere.
Kotú námane enembo akokogoda ingova rougea bu!’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
