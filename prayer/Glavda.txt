=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Glavda NT Portion (glwl), © 2024 Luke Initiative for Scripture Translation, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“ ‘Daadamnd kwama ghǝrazhigǝla,
aa fǝvaarafǝg nǝ ɗaagagha,
tlǝksǝragha aasasǝga, dǝgiti agha naag nǝng aa ɓagvǝnaɓag ma duni
bandkwa ɓagav ma ghǝrazhigǝla.
Vǝlkǝmndvǝlǝg kǝ dǝgzǝg haniina dlaɓa ba kǝlahǝnga.
Ɓǝshkǝmndanaɓǝshig kǝ haipahamnda,
bandkwa kǝm ɓǝshtǝr nǝmnd kǝ lii ɓagkǝmnd haipaha.
Langkǝmnd bi da vaka fǝshig biya,
katkǝmndkatǝg vak Shatanaha.’
