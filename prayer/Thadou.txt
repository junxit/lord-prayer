Thadoupao

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from PATHEN LEKHABU THENG (tczchongthu), © 2020 Chongthu Bible Translation Team, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Van'a um ka-Pau, namin jatah in umhen.
Na Lenggam hunglhung loihen, Van'a nalung lhaina aum tobanginlei chunga jong na lung lhaina umhen.
Tunia ka ngaichat'u anneh nei peuvin,
Keiho douna a chonse ho ka ngaidam bangun, keiho chonsetna jong ngaidam in.
Chule lhepna thua nei nunsah hih un, ahin agiloupa a kon'in nei huhdoh uvin.

Same as the Traditional form above.
