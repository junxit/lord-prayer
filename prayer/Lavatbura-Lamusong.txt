Nomad

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nomad New Testament Portions (lbv), ©  SIL International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tamak karama ukolok ke exalibet,
nima axava ba xip aurut easing kunu.
Eninto runu na vot.
Evavara runu na vot teren na evua,
langan te exalibet.
Rebe nima mi eanaan uningina.
Doxoma xepe erominingot tarama,
langan nima ba doxoma xepe erominingot tindi ti kolox i rimma.
Gus lok livi nima te eroloklok tong,
ma una sat asu xe nima xasinge eradi miningot.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
