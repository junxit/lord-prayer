Wixárika

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from KAKAƗYARI NIUKIEYA XAPAYARI WIXARIKAKƗ (hch), © 2020 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

»“Ta'ukiyari taheima pemakawe,
yuhemekɨ mɨpatsie peke'ayeitɨariwani,
'enata keneutsutɨa peti'aitatɨ,
yameketeyurieka kemɨmatinake
'ena kwiepa taheima kememɨteyurie.
'Ikwai ketaneumikwa hikɨ temeiyehɨwa.
Ketatineuyehɨwirieka 'axatemɨte'uyurikɨ
tameta temɨtewareuyehɨwirikɨ mɨkɨ 'axamemɨtatsi'uyuri.
Pepɨkatatsipitɨaka temɨta'inɨatsienikɨ,
matsi ketaneutawikweitsitɨa 'axatemɨka'itɨarienikɨ.
'Ekɨtsɨ 'aheyemekɨ pekatini'aitametɨni pekanitɨrɨkaɨyeni witsipekani'aneni 'aheyemekɨ.
Mɨpaɨ xeikɨa kani'aneni”.
