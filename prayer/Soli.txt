Chisoli

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Soli New Testament (sby), © 2018 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Bameshetu omuli kwilu,
lina lyenu lilemekwe,
Bwami bwenu bwise,
Kuyanda kwenu kwinshike.
Panshi pano mbuli ncekubele kwilu,
Mutupe lelo cakulya cetu ca masuba onse.
Mutulekeleleko bwipishi bwetu,
mbuli nenjafwe ncotukute kubalekelelako bakute kutwipishila,
Kamutasuminisha kwambeti tuwile mukweleshewa,
Nsombi tukwabilileni kuli Waipa usa. “Pakwinga Bwami ne ngofu kayi ne bulemeneno ni bwenu kwamuyayaya. Ameni
