=== Traditional ===
[Verified from KAEM KO DEN (wsk, ISO 639-3 wsk), © 1985, 2011, 2014 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Anananga Ba, ni duruk wonong te bagasam,
nika nip laili anirukko.
Nika kingdom am tairukko,
ali imi te mel aratukko ka gemang aniso,
mu am butata aratukko, duruk wonong te arataso iwitatala.
Anananga na inang kam imi ko mu nangaruko.
Anananga memek siwu nangaruko,
ana saki alo nunga memek siwu nungarman uwutatala.
Motam la nangarkiwer se ira ariga ningi me dagulanakko;
memek suen la ningi nanga bataguru toko.
