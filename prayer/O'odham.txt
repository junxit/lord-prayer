Tohono O'otham

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Jiosh Wechij O'ohana (oodNT), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

T‑ohg Jiosh, an ap kihdag dahm kahchim ch ed.
Kuch ab si has m‑elid ch si haꞌichuchud g m‑chehgig.
Ab ach m‑tahni mapt wo i geꞌedaj g e‑kownaltalig ia t‑shahgid mat g m‑tatchui am hab wo e junihid ia jewed dahm hab masma mo an hab e wua m‑kihdag ed.
Ab g i s‑t‑hoꞌigeꞌid k ab wo t‑mahkad g haꞌichu hugi wehs tashkaj.
Gam g hu hab i juhni g pi ap t‑chuꞌijig hab masma mach gam hu pi chechegitod g ha‑chuꞌijig hegam mat hedai pi ap has chuꞌi ab t‑wui.
Pi g am hiwgid g Jiawul mas am wo t‑shul pi apꞌekam ch ed.
[Ahpi ap d kownal wehs haꞌichu dahm ch wehs edgid g gewkdag.
Kutt ab wo si has m‑elidad chum hekid.]
