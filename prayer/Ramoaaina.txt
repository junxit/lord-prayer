=== Traditional ===
[Verified from BUK TAABU MATAKIN + TURPAAINA (rai, ISO 639-3 rai), © 2006, 2023 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc. and Wycliffe Sweden, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamaamiaat inaanga nate u ra maawa,
i koina baa din ru a iaam.
I koina baa anum mataanitu in waan paat.
I koina baa din taraam taam u ra rakrakaan buaal,
welaar ma di tartaraam taam inaanga nate u ra maawa.
A bung mi un tabaara miaat ma ra maamiaat utnaa.
Un dumaana wa anumiaat kum aakaina mangamangaan,
welaar ma miaat dumaana wa anundiat kum aakaina mangamangaan karom miaat.
Koku u ben miaat u ra walwalaam.
Un walaaun miaat ko ra Tena Aakaina. Aamen.’
