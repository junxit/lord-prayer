=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nukumanu (nuq, ISO 639-3 nuq), © 2020 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamana maatou i te vaelani:
te inoa e tapu Akoe ki hakammaha ria;
tiaki te Hakamaatua ana Akoe raa ki hamai;
tiaki maatou ki ppena hea Akoe e hihai maatou ki ppena i te maarama nei ki ssau pera ma i te vaelani.
Kou mai ni kai ma maatou ki tau ma te aho nei.
Ssirihia na hai sara maatou ni ppena raa,
e ssau pera ma maatou e ssirihia na hai sara na tama e ppena i maatou raa.
Auu se tiaki maatou ki ttiri na hahaaite e hainattaa,
tena ku rorohi hakaraoi maatou i taha ma na mahi te tama sakkino raa.’
[Te Hakamaatua ana, na mahi ia ma na mee taualleka Akoe raa ki mmoe tahi na vahao hakaatoa. Amen]
