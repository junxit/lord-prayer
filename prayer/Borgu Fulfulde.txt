Fulfulde Borgu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Alkawal Kesal (fue), © 2011 SIM International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‟Baaba amen tawaaɗo dow kammu,
inne maa e senii.
Laamaare maa wara.
Sago maa laatoo dow leydi,
hande no ŋo laatorii dow kammu.
Hokku men nyaamdu keƴannu men hanne.
Yaafana men hakke amen,
hande no minon bo men jaaforanto woofananɓe men.
Taa accu men naata der jarbeeji,
amma hisin men diga e Kalluɗo.
[Gam an nun laamaare, e baawɗe,
e tedduŋal fu woodani faa abada. Aami!]”
