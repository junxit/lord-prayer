=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Gotena Epe Agaa (kew, ISO 639-3 kew), © 2003 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea in cooperation with Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Niaana Aapa, nere yaa-para pi.
Nena biri oro yaalo epe-rupa ina.
Nena Surube Su epena.
Nena konemere so yaa-para peme-rupa su amaa niaame page goa palima.
Abia go yapi padane madaare neme niaana eta rumaaina.
Neme niaana wae yaenu mea rubaina-ga niaame page wae kone saapi onaanuna kone mea rubalima.
Neme niaare wae kedaa pi yaeme ko nataina pare Satan-me niaa natinalo surubape.
