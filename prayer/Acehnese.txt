Bahsa Acèh

=== Traditional ===
Bapak kamoe nyang na dalam syeuruga: Beuteupeu kudus kheueh nan Droeneueh,
beuteuka kheueh keurajeuen Droeneueh, beujeuet kheueh keuheundak Droeneueh, dibumoe lagée lam syeuruga.
Beuneubri kheueh peunajoh kamoe nibak uroenyoe nyang kamoe peureulée.
Beuneupeu amphon kheueh desya kamoe lagée kamoe ka meu peuamphon ureuëng nyang meudesya ateueh kamoe.
Dan bék kheueh neuba kamoe lam peucubaan, teuma beuneu peulheueh kheueh kamoe nibak si Jeuhet.

Kareuna Droeneueh kheueh nyang po keurajeuen, dan kuasa dan keumuliaan sampoe siumu masa. Amin.
