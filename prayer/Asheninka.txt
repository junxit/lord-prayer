=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Iwaperite tajorentsi ikenkithatakoetziri awinkatharite Jesokirishito: owakirari inimotakiri tajorentsi (cjoNT, ISO 639-3 cjo), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Pawa, niyotzi pijeeki jenokinta eejatzi jaawikika eejatzi pikemijanteeyanani.
Osheki pikameethaperotaki.
Kameethapero pipinkathawenteeyanani.
Nokowaki nantemiro kipatsikika pikowakaakinari, nokimityaari rantziri ponampiripaeni janta jenokinta. Rooma imatakimiro maawaeni pikowakaakiriri.
Maawaeni kitejiri pamitakoeyenani nantawaete, eero okoetyeemotantana noyaari.
Niyotzi eenitatsi kaari kameethatatsi nantziri, omaanta eero pikijawentanawo, inta pipeyakotenawo. Rooma nopeyakotziniri antakaanawori naari oetarikapaeni, tee nokijiri.
Eero pishinetziri kamaari rantakayenawo kaari kameethatatsi.
Pikanteetatyeeyaani peewawenteeyenani.
Ashi powantyaawo pitajonkawentena.
Eejatzi naari, ashi nowantyaawo noñaaperotemi.
Rootaki nokantakimiri, ari okaatzi”.
