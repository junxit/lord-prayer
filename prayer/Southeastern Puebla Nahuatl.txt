=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Itlajtol Totajtsi Dios (nplNT, ISO 639-3 npl), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Totajtsi Dios non timoitstika ilwikak,
nochipa makiyektenewakah motoka.
Xiwalmuika xitlanawatiki,
mamochiwa tlan Tejwatsi tikmonekiltia itech nin tlaltíkpaktli,
ijkó kemi mochiwa kan ilwikak.
Techmaka nochi tonati tlan tikmomajsewiskeh.
Techintlapojpolwili totlajtlakolwah,
ijkó kemi tejwah noiwa tikintlapojpolwiah akimeh techtoktiah tlan amo kuali.
Amo xiktekawili matechyoltilana n tlajtlákoli,
yej techmakixti imak n amokualitlakatl.
