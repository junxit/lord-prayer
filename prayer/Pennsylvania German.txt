Pennsilfaanisch Deitsch

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Pennsylvania Dutch Bible (pdc), © 2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Unsah Faddah im Himmel,
loss dei nohma heilich sei,
un loss dei Reich kumma.
Loss dei villa gedu sei,
uf di eaht vi im Himmel.
Gebb uns heit unsah tayklich broht.
Un fagebb unsah shulda,
vi miah dee fagevva vo uns shuldich sinn.
Un fiah uns nett in di fasuchung,
avvah hald uns fumm evila.
Fa dei is es Reich, di graft,
un di hallichkeit in ayvichkeit. Amen.
