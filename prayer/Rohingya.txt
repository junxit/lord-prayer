Ruáingga

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kitabul Mukaddos or Injil Córif (rhgc), © 2024 The Word for the World International and Rohingya Eastern Bible Translation, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ó añárar asmani Baf,
Tuáñr nam pak mana zouk.
Tuáñr raijjo aiyóuk.
Tuáñr moncá zendilla asman ot fura ó,
héndilla duniyait óu fura óuk.
Aijjar hána añára re aijja doo.
Añárar hosúri maf goró,
zendilla zetará añára llói hosúri goijjé, ítara re añára yó maf goijjí.
Añára re entán ot foitto no diyó,
bólke Ibilíc ottu basai rakó. Kiyólla-hoilé raijjo, taakot aar mohíma toh hámica Tuáñr.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
