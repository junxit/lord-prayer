=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Diiz kuu ndyaadno yalnaban (zaoNT, ISO 639-3 zao), © 2007 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Xutʼn, kuu ndob yibaa,
nlazʼn tsow nataak re mena tak nayon naka.
Taxal ntsow re zha kuu nzo yibaa kuu nlaaza,
leeka tata nlazʼn tsow re men yezlyu ree kuu nlaaza.
Nlazʼn yaada nabeeʼa lo yezlyu ree,
taxal nabeeʼa yibaa.
Taa kuu wun naltseree.
Ne btuyy re falta kuu mtsowʼn loʼa,
taxal mloʼa na yekʼn re falt kuu mtsow re taben da lon.
Btsow tsa tsowtʼn kuu went,
leettsa nkiil tedib zha na,
ne bkenapʼn lo kuu went kuu nlaaz Maxuuy tsow lon,
tak chaana nak ta nabeeʼa, yalney ne
ta nla xni chul chaana thitanax.
Tata gake.
