Esiluyana

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Luyana Bible Translation (lyn), © 2024 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Sitetu naku wilu,
kulumbiwe elitina lyenu kwiiye omubuso wenu,
esi sisaeliwa kwa wene sitediwe banji,
kanyi omo sitendeliwa kuwilu.
Otube balelo esilyanu setu eso tusaela,
Otulemene emibonda yetu,
kanyi omo tulemenanga awo sikutu foseza.
Ole kutu ingenyeka mu miliko,
kono utulamulele kwa woyo no kwiiba.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
