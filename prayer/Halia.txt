‘O Tamamulam i Kolö, a solomulö e gi hagoagoni.
A Nipepeito i tamulö e ga lama.
Alam go kato u turu ngil i tamulö i puta te kato has uarien i Kolö.
Alö go hala ramei lam a tsi kannou te noana i romana.
Alö go lu ban a ka a omi tu kati lam ba te solopale men, tu solopala hakapa has meni lam a ka a omi te kato u a tana katun i tamulam.
Alö go ma hala meri lam tara tou hiamus, kaba alö go lu ba beri lam a nomi. U mana.’
