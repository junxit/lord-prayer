=== Traditional ===
[Verified from El Nuevo Testament en el idioma Zapoteco de Loxicha (ztp), © 2025 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Xud náꞌ Dios jwanꞌ ndob lo beꞌ,
Nayon toz lea.
Yeꞌd yalnibeꞌ chanꞌ la.
Nde bli xa ncholaꞌs la, ta xa ndlia lo beꞌ taga bli lo izyo reꞌ.
Jwanꞌ nak la' náꞌ re wiz, bta'y na wiz.
Nde ptonꞌ re xken náꞌ, xa ntorid náꞌ yek náꞌ re jwanꞌ narax ndli men lo náꞌ.
Naꞌtat la diꞌs gab náꞌ lo re jwanꞌ narax, cheꞌla ptolaꞌ náꞌ lo re jwanꞌ narax, tak chanꞌ la nak yalnyebeꞌ, yalne nde yalnaro, dib adiꞌ. Ta naka.
