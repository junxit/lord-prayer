Limos Kalinga

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ugud apudyus = Ti baro tulag (kmk, ISO 639-3 kmk), © 2003 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Ama mi un awad langit, iluwaḻu mi un madaydayaw nat ngadan nu.
Iluwaḻu mi un dumatongon nat man-apuwam situn pita kan matungpal nat piyaom situn pita un padana’d langit.
Itdom dan kanon mi un inaḻgaw.
Pakawanom dan basuḻ mi un padan dit mamakawanan mi utdan makabasuḻ kan dikami.
Adim ipalubusa masugsugan kami, nu adi iyadayu dikami utdin nadadag.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
