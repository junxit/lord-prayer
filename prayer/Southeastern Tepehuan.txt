=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Gu ñio'ki'ñ gu Dios na bhaan tu a'ga guch xoi'kam (stpNT, ISO 639-3 stp), © 2007 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Aapiꞌ Gɇꞌkam jich Gɨꞌkoraꞌ nap jix dhaam jup tuꞌiiꞌ,
nap jix ioꞌm jir jix uañ,
iam jap ja mattudaꞌ gu jaꞌtkam yaꞌ dhi oiꞌñgaꞌn nam jax jaꞌk duuji miꞌ puiꞌ jimiaꞌ nap jax jix aaꞌ,
baꞌ gammɨjɨ ja aꞌm ba tɨ nɨidhidhaꞌ ap.
Puiꞌ tɨich aaꞌ ich ɨp nap gɨt puiꞌ joidham jix bhaiꞌm duukam jup tu buadaꞌ ya jaꞌp dhi oiꞌñgaꞌn bhaan,
jaꞌp nap jax jix bhaiꞌm duukam jup tu bua jix dhaam jaꞌk.
Ku guꞌ aap dɨɨlh ji noꞌp moo puiꞌp jix aaꞌndaꞌ gɨt,
aach puiꞌ pɨx tɨip jum kaiꞌchuꞌn ji.
Gio baꞌ dhich chutkuꞌ nam tuꞌ kɨꞌn jup dudua,
aap gammɨjɨ nabap tanoolh xi ja maakidhat nam baꞌ kɨꞌn jum guguuxiꞌñdhaꞌ.
Gio baꞌ nap iam tuch uañiꞌñkaꞌ ɨp nach jɨꞌx mach uaꞌtulhdhaꞌ aap jum bui,
nach guꞌ aach puiꞌp tu ja uañiꞌñ guiꞌ nam puiꞌp jum uaꞌtulhiꞌñ aach jich bui.
Gio baꞌ nap gammɨjɨ jich aꞌm tɨ nɨidhidhaꞌ na baꞌ cham jich maiꞌchiaꞌ gux buam jum duukam.
Jaꞌpni daipuꞌ.
