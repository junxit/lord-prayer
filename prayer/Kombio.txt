Kombio Wampukuamp

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kombio Wampukuamp Bible Portions (xbi-w), © 2000 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Waiek antelel ka ar yemp ekeipmelel, nio ntinkiraiel aiyiknel ko yer niorknel.
An aiyimp yikn ko yikn pelkel semp yemp omoule amampel, nako an nekerpm sank aiyiknel sukurel yer kitapmel ntoka nti ka ar no yemp ekeipmel ka nakerpm upuikn aiyiknel aninkaninkel.
An aiyimp yikn na yok an miepm yupulp awun keielel.
Yapmonemp kuoretel antelel ko yikn wonempsukurel sop ka an wonempsukurel yapmonemp kuoretel antuol ka nti apulp anel.
Et yikn korokn yekei niampepm ser setel kuoretel ninkemp wankepm. Ko yikn mpuanink an yupulp Satan kakorokn ukuap an na yupulp an ntokilelel. Kakilpe.
