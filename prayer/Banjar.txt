Bahasa Banjar

=== Traditional ===
[Note: No Banjar translation of Matthew 6 exists. The only published Banjar Scripture
(Wycliffe / Kulawarga Nabi Isa, 2019/2025) contains the Gospels of Luke and John.
Below is the Lord's Prayer in its shorter Lukan form (Lukas 11:2-4), verbatim from that
edition. The Lukan form lacks "Thy will be done on earth as in heaven," "deliver us from
evil," and the doxology; no established Banjar text exists for those lines.]

Ya Allah, Abah kami nang di atas, kami maakui Pian tu Nang Maha Suci.
Bakuasalah atas kami wan samunyaan urang.
Bariꞌilah kami saban hari makanan nang samayunya.
Ampunilah dusa-dusa kami nang kaya kami mamaapakan urang nang basalah lawan kami.
Jangan mambiarakan kami kalah wayah digudai Iblis.

[UNVERIFIED — no established Banjar translation of the doxology confirmed; the published
Banjar Scripture follows the Lukan text, which has no doxology. The doxology is therefore
omitted rather than fabricated.]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
[UNVERIFIED — no established Banjar Matthew 6 translation exists; the lines below mirror
the canonical English clause order as a best-effort rendering and need human review. The
verified Lukan text above should be preferred for the petitions it covers.]

Abah kami nang di sruga, Pian tu Nang Maha Suci.
Datanglah karajaan Pian, jadilah kahandak Pian di bumi sama kaya di sruga.
Bariꞌilah kami saban hari makanan nang samayunya.
Ampunilah dusa-dusa kami, nang kaya kami mamaapakan urang nang basalah lawan kami.
Jangan mambiarakan kami digudai Iblis, tagal lapasakanlah kami matan nang jahat.

Karana Pian nang baisi karajaan, kakuasaan, wan kamuliaan, salalawasan. Amin.
