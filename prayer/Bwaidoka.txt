=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yaubada Yana Veyavo Anawai Evaguna (bwd, ISO 639-3 bwd), © 2024 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamama umiyamiya gabameya,
ayegana tabu ana vemoimoinena.
Ya ebaveimeya gidaelai.
Ya nuwanuwa migabama igugai
anafaiweya, ime babiya kaduwe ada gugaiyena.
Amaga malinai faina uda velema.
Yama koyona uda venuwaegedi
anafaiweya ime kaliva yadi koyona
wagaimeya bademowa avenuwaegau.
Ada ebaaladibidibi wagaidiya keke uda awaegema,
aku koyona wagaine uda kibababalema.
[Faina Oyeni ya ebaveimeya,
ke Oyeni ya toyogina,
ada Oyeni ya iwakana mamalina
inamiya-vagata keke ginaavai. Anafaiweya.]’ Taidei anafaiweya wana veoveola.
