Bemal

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Uwait nugau Ze Naliu (bmh), © 2005 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the opening quotation mark the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Igual memenig, na Mesgai wag darem. Nait wanim noupet ig ulapwag mizem.
Nait gumiganem ziwas em e unum alai iboramau. Nait onsau Mesgai wag sesamoranemen sul, ig han em e ebu han ereg sesamoramam.
Ulis na ig ee migamam.
Iguanag dudu ipal agal igul eg ig ebiligeb miganemen zilagai maranem sul, nagen han igual igul eg zilagai migen.
Na ig ece abu eg ebu i amaigamam. Na ig utigemen, igul eg nug i imaigamau.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
