kahs Kosrae

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from BIBLE MUTAL (kos), public domain, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Papa tumasr su inkusrao;
Eelos oal pwaye;
Tokosrai lalos tuku;
Ma lungse lalos in orek fin faclu, ou elos oru inkusrao.
Kite kut len se inge ma kut mongo misenge.
Ac nunak munas nu sesr ke ma koluk lasr,
Oana kut nunak munas nu sin mwet su orekma koluk nu sesr.
Ac tia kol kut in mwel, a eiskutla liki ma koluk!’ [Tuh Tokosrai lalos, ac ku, ac wolana ma pahtpat, Amen.]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
