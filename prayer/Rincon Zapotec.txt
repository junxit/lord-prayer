Didza Xidza

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Didza' cubi rucá'ana tsahui' (zarNT), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Xuz netuʼ, zuʼ yehuaʼ yubá, gaca zxön Loʼ.
Gudá inná bëʼu luyú ni.
Ben ga gaca luyú ni ca raza ládxuʼu Liʼ
ca naʼ raca yehuaʼ yubá.
Benna le gágutuʼ naʼa dza.
Buniti lo netuʼ le huiáʼ döʼ bentuʼ Loʼ Liʼ
ca runtuʼ netuʼ, runiti lotuʼ nupa bi tun queë́tuʼ.
Bitiʼ guʼu lataj siʼ bëʼ tuʼ xihuiʼ netuʼ,
pero busölá netuʼ lu naʼ nu ruáʼ döʼ.
Nequi quiuʼ ga rinná bëʼu, en yöl-laʼ unná bëʼ, en yöl-laʼ ba.
Caní rë́ʼënituʼ gácaticaʼsö. ¡Caʼ gaca!
