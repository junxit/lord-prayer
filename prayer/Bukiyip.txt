=== Traditional ===
[Verified from God Ananin Balan (apeB, ISO 639-3 ape), © 2013 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Apakinyu Aninu nyape iluh heven uli, apak makli chunatimaguk elpech chutuk nyakin yopinyi yeul nyukih.
Makli nyak nyupe nebenyalimu wolobaichi elpech. Makli nyugakamapu mugipech enenyi enen nyak nyaklienyi agundak apudak atap, douk chape iluh heven uli i save chagipechen umu.
Nyak nyukapu woligun gnunokwnumu doumeih uli.
Apak douk wo munohwen mupemu yowenyi douk kipahechi cheneken umu apak uli e wak, mokleyenyuk. Douk namudak ali nyak kobi wata nyunohwen nyupemu yowenyi apak moneken uli wak, nyukleyenyuk.
Nyak kobi nyukutuk Satan nichakomapamu munek yowenyi, wak. Wakuli douk nyihiyaho-noguk umu apak mupe kalbu.’ [Nyak atinyu nyape nyohwalu elpech chawich agundak nyak nyape nebenyalimu nyakich elpech umu, nyanubu dodogowinyu atinyu ali wolobaichi elpech chatuk nyakin yeul nyakih eheh nyumneh, adulin.]
