Rugungu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Bitabbu bya Ndagaanu Ghyaka na Bitabbu 6 bya Ndagaanu Gi̱gu̱lu̱u̱su̱ (Lugungu) (rub), © 2025 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“ ‘Bbaaweetu̱ wa mwiguru, ibara lyamu liheeb̯we ki̱ti̱i̱ni̱sa,
b̯ukama b̯wamu b̯wi̱ze,
bi̱wendya bikorwe mu nsi nka kubikorwa mwiguru.
Otuhe hataati̱ bidyo byetu̱ bya b̯uli kiro;
Otuganyire bibii byetu̱,
nka twe kutuganyira bantu batukora kubiibi;
Otatuleka nitukooheb̯wa,
bei̱tu̱ otujune hali Mubii.’
