Sarnámi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Parmeswar ke naawa poestak (hnsNT), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Hamlogke swarag ke Pieta,
sab koi aapke naam pawietr maane,
aapke raadj aawe,
aur djaise tor hiettjha swarag me poera howe hai, oisane dhartie par bhie howe.
Hamlogke aadj ke khaana deihe.
Djaise hamlog aapan kasoerwaar waalan ke maaf kariela, oisane aap hamlogke kasoer maaf kar deihe.
Hamlogke laalietj me na paran de aur boeraai me se batjaaw.
[Aap raadj tjalaawe hai aur aapke saktie aur mahiemma hai sada ke lieje.
Aisahie to hai.]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
