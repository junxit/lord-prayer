=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ayta Abellen (abp, ISO 639-3 abp), © 2020-2024 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Bapa mi a anti ha langit,
galangen laka dayin kaganaan a tatao.
Apo, hakopen mo kayinan kaganaan.
Matupad dayi ye kalabayan mo ihti ha babe-luta a omen ha langit.
Biyan mo kayin pamangan mi ha minamangaamot.
Boy patawaden mo kayi ha kakahalanan mi
a omen ha pamatawad mi kanlan nagkahalanan kammi.
Adi mo kayi ampaolayan a matukho,
no aliwan iligtah mo kayi kanan Satanas.’
