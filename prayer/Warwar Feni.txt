Feni

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Konona Sunwar lo Yesus Kristus (hrw), © 2024 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Taumem ilo balambat,
Tafu e asam ku ta tarawen.
Tafu e kepmale kiam ku puek,
tafu e fabur kiam ku fuut lo nal,
are ilo balambat.
Fen kemem geinggii ine inen amem.
Sangintafngi e fan tubiil kemem,
are kemeu ming kemem sangintafngi e fan tubiil kere fa ri gow lo kemem.
Mele tule kemem tife falam,
sakle ong kep kemem konsge fan mok e sak.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
