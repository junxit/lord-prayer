Chirin nu Ibatan

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ivatan (ivv), © 2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Ama namen a mian do hanyit, aniven o ngaran mo.
Mangay diamen o paypatolan mo as kapakatongtong pa no ichakey mo diamen do tana aya akma so kawnot dia do hanyit.
Itoroh mo pa o ichaviay namen do sicharaw aya,
as kapakabo mo pa so gatos namen akma so kapakabo namen dira do makagatos diamen.
Pavawahen mo pa yamen do katentasionan diamen kan do atavo a marahet. [Ta imo o maypatol a mian so ipakapamarin a abo so pandan. Amen.]’

Same as the Traditional form above.
