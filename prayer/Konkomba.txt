Likpakpaanl

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Uwumbɔr aagbaŋ (xon), © 2014 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tite Uwumbɔr u bi paacham na,
cha binib li san saayimbil.
Saanaan dan.
Bi li ŋani saageehn dulnyaa wee ni
ke baah ŋani paacham pu na.
Tiin timi din aawiin aajikaar.
Cha timi aataani ki pinn timi,
ke ti mu aah di cha pinn binib
bi koo timi aataani ni pu na.
Taa cha ti kan ntɔŋ.
Nyan timi kinimbɔŋ aaŋaal ni.
[Si le yeh nnaan, ni mpɔɔn,
ni mpakm n‑yoonn mu kaa kpa ndoon na.’]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
