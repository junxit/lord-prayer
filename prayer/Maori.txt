Te Reo Māori

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Te Kawenata Hou (mri), © 1952,2008 Bible Society New Zealand Charitable Trust, Matthew 6:9-13. The wording in this file matches that edition word for word; collated against the text published at eBible.org.]

E tō mātou Matua i te rangi,
kia tapu tōu Ingoa.
Kia tae mai tōu rangatiratanga.
Kia meatia tāu e pai ai ki runga ki te whenua, kia rite anō ki tō te rangi.
Hōmai ki a mātou āianei he taro mā mātou mō tēnei rā.
Murua ō mātou hara, me mātou hoki e muru nei i ō te hunga e hara ana ki a mātou.
Aua hoki mātou e kawea kia whakawaia;
engari whakaorangia mātou i te kino.

Nōu hoki te rangatiratanga, te kaha, me te korōria, āke āke āke. Āmine.
