Movono

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tangoa New Testament (tgp), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

God Tamamam na tuka,
kama opoia vara tamlohi tari
la sile na oloolo tavera isam
mata engko ko tapu,
kama opoia vara nom
suiha aulu mata supe i pala,
o vai na masalom na varama
i tataholo sohen ko lo vaia na tuka,
o sile kamam na hanhani
matan nohorihi,
o r̃omi kamam
mata nomam hehe,
sohen kama lo r̃omi
na hehe nona tamlohi tinapua,
o lavi nar̃ihi kamam na vaihite,
o juri kamam hin Tiapolo.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
