=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Testamen Oauoau (mva, ISO 639-3 mva), © 1996 Papua New Guinea Bible Translation Association, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tama-ma lang anua-lo kusukoaki. Aram ratana-la ngaeneno.
‘Bong ono gotanepoa kana nge ngapura-kama. Rerengam lang anua-lo ditagatagadi bokana nge kateka-o datagatagadi.
‘Kangkang amari ngaena-lonalona goang-kama.
‘Oti nema gorokakile-kama, tamoata takadi oti nedi kirorokakiledi bokana.
‘Moaki toitoi-lo kulakuaki-kama. Ata Satang gorokakale-kama. [Bong ono gotanepoa kana, kaiboang, be ara bibia nge nem-doi, be nem-kueno daeneno. Moimoi.]’
