[Verified from Cohamacʉ Yare Yahari Tjuel (gvc, ISO 639-3 gvc), © 2007 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Sã Pʉcʉ, mʉano macariro mʉhʉ ñaa marieriro ã jirucura.
Mipʉre jipihtina bui pʉhtoro jiga.
Mʉanopʉ mʉ dutiro seheta ti yoariro seheta ahri yahpa macaina cʉ̃hʉ yoajaro.
Ã jicʉ dachoripe sã chʉhtire warucuga sãre.
Paina sãre ti ñano yoarire sã boja.
Ã jicʉ mʉhʉ cʉ̃hʉ sã ñano yoarire boga.
Ã jicʉ sã ñaare yoa duana sã goa pjachʉ ñʉcʉ tíre yoa dutii tjiga.
Ã jicʉ sãre ñaare yoaerachʉ yoaga.
[Mʉhʉ sã pʉhtoro tuariro ã jirucuihca. Jipihtina mʉhʉre ã ño payorucuahca]”, ni siniga Cohamacʉre.
