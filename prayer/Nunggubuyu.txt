Wubuy

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ana-Maṉngulg Ana-Wubiba -- Anu-gadhuwa Ana-lhaawu (nuy), © 2010 Bible Society of Australia, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Nurri-nyinyung Baba, nu-ngu-burraa anaarrwar.
Nirri-maṉngulg-gana nugawi ana-muwaj.
Ngunu-ngaynbandii ba-buunggawa-mana.
Ngunu-ngaynbandii baaṉbina nugawi-waj ana-lhal-lhangu-rruj, nguynju nunggaṉbina wuguuguni anaarrwar.
Numbiiyii nurri-wuy ana-marrya nurru-ngaynbandii ana-yimbaj.
Numbaagajij-garruyn anaaladi-wala nurru-waṉbini-yinyung, nguynju narraagajij-garruna warruburru anaaladi-wala wurru-waṉbina nurri-wuy.
Yagi numba-rawu anaangaḏajung. Numba-milimilidhii anaaladi-wala.’”
