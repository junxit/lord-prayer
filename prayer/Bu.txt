Jida-Abu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Bu New Testament (jid), © 2020 Door43 World Missions Community, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Iti mbu wa a he shulua, ndu san me tsratsra me,
mulki ndu ye, ikpie wa u son ndu ba tiewu ni ngbugblu'a nawa ba tiewu ni shulu'a.
Nnota rli u vi luwa.
U latre mbu uka uru hle tawu, towa kie uruhle ni bari'a.
Ndi na nji ta hi wrji u tsra-toh na, jbu ta rjuni wo bi brji(meme). Mulki mba ngbengble, mba girma(babran) bahi ume kakle'. Ameh
