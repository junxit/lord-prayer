=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yuusa chichame yaunchukia tura yamaram chichamjai aarmau (hubNT, ISO 639-3 hub), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Apaachi, ameka nayaimpinam pujuwaitme.
Pegkeraitam imau asaakmin, chikich ainasha ima aminak ashi emematramiarti.
Ashi shuartin amek inakratta.
Tura nayaimpinmaya shuar aina ame wakeramun umirtamin aina aitkasrik, ju nugkanmaya shuar ainatisha ame wakeramua nuke umikarmi.
Ame kashi kashinig ii yuatnusha sukartita.
Chikich aina pegkerchaun takartamainasha tsagkurin ainaj turusmek ina tunaurisha tsagkurkarturta.
Ina iwanch tunaun takasti tusa nekapramkaisha inaikartusaipa, amek yaigkratkata ii nepetkami’, nu tu aujsatarma.
