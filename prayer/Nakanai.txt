Lakalai

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nakanai New Testament (nak), © 1983 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Tama miteu, eme pou oata te la ilo la lage, taritigi amite ge togo la isamu,
taritigi veia la valalua me hatavivile tomi ge roromuli la masasagala taume, mai tai egiteu la angelo oata te la ilo la lage egite roromuli tatahome.
Taritigi eme ge abia miteu la ilali ale koramuli amiteu te la haro ale igoie.
Eme ge vimavulaha amiteu te la iruru tamiteu mai sesele amiteu vimavulaha egiteu ale igoti la iruru soio tamiteu.
Eme umala ge sugu taro amiteu soio te la gima tamiteu, eia soukama ge tovo lalai la tautaulailo tamite. Eala moli eme ge vimahuli amiteu taro la limale Satan. (La Viputumulila taume, la vagagarila taume, la matagagala taume, eme sekela moli sau tolea, la tapasila ge ouka. Amen.)
