Uruwa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Fat Mata ogepma (yuw), © 1997 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Sa Gapgap Nandöng noni, goc momdec iditaroc. Gochon maya nongnongo kunkun itun.
Engga irot danong guroc danong moröm tong nimpi. Momdec gochon duc sumbot sumbot itac. O ihorocgon gurocdec nondec öngkungbödeng tongoun.
Goc önga nacno toroc nonidec nimpi.
Goc turongo noni imi fat nimpun. O nonu ihorocgon amna wontucmuno tong nimongidangma yuhon turongno dongyana faarut.
Goc tonguc yecyecdec fohong nuirocha. Muno gocho non wontucmuno morömo yuhon oburodecma öcangyi möarut.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
