[Verified from Dios Chani (caoNT, ISO 639-3 cao), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Oquëhuahacaxëti xo mi janë,
noba naipá ca Jahëpá” icana.
“Nohiria bo mi ototsano.
Mai xo tsi mi shina mi ano,
naipa xo mi acai jascaria.
Jatiroha ca bari oriquiti noqui ana.
Noba jocha mi shina-bënono,
noqui pasomaha jochacanaiba jocha no shina-bënohai jascaria.
Jochati no tanamahacayamano.
Yoixëni cato chamá quima noqui mi tsëcano” tihi tsi nëcacana bëhoxhi na.
