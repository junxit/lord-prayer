Kiriwina

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Buki Pilabumaboma Kabutubogwa Deli Kabutuvau (kij), © 1984 Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamamasi wa labuma,
yagam bakatemmalaisi.
M Kwaraiwaga bima,
nanom bakavagaisi valu valu komwaidona,
makawala mina labuma ivagaisi.
Kamasi bukusakaimasi lagaila.
Bukuligaiwa ma mitugagasi,
makawala yakamaisi kaligaiwaisi si mitugaga matausina imitugagaimasi.
Taga kutagwala bitamnabaimasi,
mitugaga isisu baisa bukukolaimasi.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
