Burungaisoo

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Burunge (bds), © 2019 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Taataa goori goó rawaa gu rawge waara,
uma googu dugu muriidiye,
tawaaloo doogu yaa khayde daqa doorii,
slaꞌamuu koku digii gaasiye,
tiꞌii khoorotii giyoó iliiwane gooay taysi rawaa gu rawge.
Letuti sandi hadiside ⁄agoo doori.
Handi ilamooyinte tlakwaroo doori,
dandiray gooay goó ilii ilamooyine, hhakira goõ goó dakumisee daqa doorii.
Kara, handi hhanti hiilu⁄uda koimisuge,
handiti ilabuꞌunte daqa Biriisimoogo.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
