vantung a om kote Pa awng, na min thiangtho ki upat tahen.
Na kumpingam hong piang tahen. Vantung ah na deina a kicin bang in, leitung ah kicing tahen.
Tu ni in ka nisim an uh hong pia ta in.
Ngual dang mawna ka maisak uh bangma, kote mawna zong hong maisak ta in.
Ze-etna sungah hong paipui ngawl in, phatngawlna sung pan in hong tankhia tan: ukzawkna, vangletna le minthanna a tawntung in nangma a, a hihi. Amen.
