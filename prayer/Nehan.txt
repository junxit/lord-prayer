=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nehan New Testament (nsn, ISO 639-3 nsn), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tamamiueim, ingga kaeia i Heuen,
inggeim mi uouongono tar hangoumua.
O heiriha tenang Nitoia, doh tenang nikauek e kotpokosane i kot,
doh tereng nimalara ra uakelukinane i kot
ngohinanah i Heuen.
O heiriha tamiueim tar nieining daan.
O kuse luara teremi niguata uasaeim
misiana ring kuse luara pe inggeim tasisit ra guata tar niguata uasa tamiueim.
Ingga dehen to keip tamiueim tar uedang,
karo uelkarus tur tamiueim tar tang siokor Tamata Uasa.
Ge a Nitoia pe doha nitampopokoh, doha hangana uleik a uiniator a noumua tar mamang binakalik.”
