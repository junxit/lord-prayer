Santa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Hudani Kielien (sce), © 2020-2024 River Street Publishing, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

'Asiman jieredu mai Ada,
chi nierene zhiguishigha.
Chii gojiai iregha.
Chi isine asiman jiere mutun
zemin jiere chenjigha.
Niudunieku ijiuni enedu bijiende ogi.
Bijiende codasan kunlai bijien rouliesan mutun
bijien codasei roulie.
Bijienyi shiikude bu echighazhi
Ibilisii khase khizhegha.
Gojia, qienli, famashi chanqi chinughun wo. Amine!'

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
