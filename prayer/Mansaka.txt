Minansaka

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yang Bago na Togon Kanatu (msk), © 1975 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the opening quotation mark the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Pagaindoon ta kamo sang pagampo mayo sang Dios. Aw magampo kamo, maynini yang paglaongan mayo: “‘Ama nami na aglangit, gosto nami na abay kaw taodon nang kariko nang otaw, labaw pa sang kariko.
Gosto nami na madatung da yang allaw na ikaw yang magaari da kanami. Gosto nami na amatoman da nami yang kanmo pagbuut kanami asining donya, mayn aglangit na yabay pangagdan yang kanmo pagbuut.
Atagi kami nang kanami pagkaan allawallaw.
Pasaylowa kami sang kanami manga sara maynang kanami pagpasaylo sang yakasara kanami.
Paawata kami sang pagsoray ni Satanas, tabangi kami antak di kami daugun naan.’ “Maynaan,” laong ni Jesus, “yang pagampo mayo sang Dios.”
