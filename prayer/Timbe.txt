=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yusugât Pat Âlep Den (tim, ISO 639-3 tim), © 1987 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Himbimân Ewenenŋe gâŋgât kotge âlepŋe akto kotge are kârikŋe tato nâŋgâenŋe bunŋe akto manbaen.
Akto hân ârândâŋ Kembu aknengi. Himbimân gâtŋande lauge lokomai hainâgâk hânân gâtŋe nenŋe lauge lokoenŋe ârândâŋ agâk.
Akto uŋak ire gâŋe sotnenŋe are dopnenŋânâk nengimenâ nene.
Akto luâk âmbâle bikŋande bâleŋe siâ siâ aknengimbiâ âmâ are yeŋgât dosayeŋe hanâk hepun yeŋgim manmaen dop hainâk gâŋe dosanenŋe are hepun nengiwen.
Nen bo dop nenguwen. Akto bâleŋânba kotgâm menenekben. A bundâk.” Yeŋe hain dâm Anutu ulilaŋbei.
