Buamu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dónbeenì páaníi fĩnle vũahṹ (boxNT), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Wàn Maá na wi ho wáayi,
wé à ɓa nùpua zũń le fo lé le Dónbeenì na ɓàn síi mía.
Dí ũ bɛ́ɛnì hã lùa ɓúenɓúen yi.
Le ũ sĩi bìo wé ho tá wán làa bìo mu wee wéráa ho wáayi bìo síi.
Hã wɛn lè mu bè-dínii na ko làa wɛn ho zuia.
Sɛ́n wa bè-kora día, làa bìo á warɛ́n mún wee tà sɛ́n día à na bìa wó khon làa wɛn yi.
Pa wɛn bìo wee khà wɛn dé mu bè-kohó wéró yi bìo yi. Kã̀ní wɛn o *Satãni níi yi.
[Le bɛ́ɛnì, lè ho dàńló, lè ho cùkú bìo sã̀ foǹ fɛ́ɛɛ. *Amiina.]
