=== Traditional ===
[Verified from KUATE TUKU PASA (ssd, ISO 639-3 ssd), © 1975, 1999 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Siŋgine Mam ne samba mbolŋge minit. Naŋe nyu purfeŋnu ta taŋamba minmba minwa.
Ne naŋe gageu kilmba kulatkam tuku prowa. Ne tuku nzali samba mbolŋge mayok kinit taŋamba kilke te mbolŋge mata mayok kuwa.
Ki ait te tuku nyamagaŋ kumumbi siŋga.
Nane afu sine mbolŋge mbarde le sine ndek gilaiŋgeg taŋamba ndo ne sine tuku mbar mata sauka gilaiŋga.
Sine tagosiŋgam tuku ait prowa le piti ta peuwa. Satan tuku saŋgri mbolŋge ne sine kilmba kile-mayokka. [Ne ndo Gabat Suŋgo Ndindo. Ne saŋgri ŋakmba ŋak. Ne tuku nyu suŋgo pasa ŋak. Ne taŋamba minmba minamŋgat. Son.] Ne taŋamba Kuate yabaŋa.
