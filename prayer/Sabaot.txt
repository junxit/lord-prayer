=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Biibilya Nyēē Tiliil (spyNT), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Baaba Yēyiin nyoo imiitē kibkōōnkōy,
kutōrōriit ꞉kaayneeng'uung'.
Kuyit ꞉bāytooyiisyēēng'uung'
kuyeyakay ām wōlēēb ng'wēny ꞉kiyēē imāchē
kuu wōloo wuunto ām kibkōōnkōy.
Ikōōnēēch kiyēē kyoomē ām bēsyō ake tukul.
Inyōōwēēch kaat ām choolwookātēēnyoo,
kuu yoo kēēnyōōchinē ꞉nkacheek kaat
choo choolwookēnchēēch.
Itiiyēēch /simakeekenteech,
nteenee isateeneech wōlēē mii ꞉Buunyoontēēt.
