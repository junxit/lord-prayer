Sʉraya

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Marĩpʉya Kerere Wereri Turi (sri), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Gʉapʉ ʉ̃mʉgasigue ããrĩgʉ̃́, ããrĩpererã mʉrẽ goepeyari merã bʉremuburo.
Mʉ ããrĩpererãrẽ doreri aariburo. Ʉ̃mʉgasigue marã mʉ gããmerõ irirosũta i nikũ marãde iriburo.
Ʉ̃mʉrikʉ gʉare baari sĩka!
Gajerã gʉare ñerõ irideare gʉa kãtirosũta mʉde gʉa ñerõ irideare kãtika!
Gʉa ñerõ iridʉamakʉ̃ kãmutaka! Ñegʉ̃ gʉare ñerĩ ãrĩmesãrĩrẽ tau kãmutaka! Mʉ sugʉta gʉare doregʉ, turatarigʉ, gʉa bʉremugʉ̃ ããrĩnígʉkoa, ãrĩ sẽrẽka Marĩpʉre!
