isiNdebele

=== Traditional ===
Baba wethu osezulwini,
alicwengiswe ibizo lakho,
umbuso wakho awufike,
intando yakho yenziwe ephasini njengombana kunjalo ezulwini.
Usiphe namhlanjesi isikwa sethu sangemihla.
Usilibalele amacalethu njengombana sibalibalela abanamacala kithi.
Ungasingenisi ekulingweni,
kodwana yewusitjhaphulule kokumbi.

Ngombana umbuso ungowakho, namandla nephazimulo, ngokungapheliko. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
