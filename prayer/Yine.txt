=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Gerotu Tokanchi Gipiratkaluru (pibNT), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Wuru, tengogne Gajeru,
gipashichgiwakatkalurupyi.
Runanutka Pixa pogirewatyawaka.
Pixa galukyawaka wane gixkalurupni; tengogneko pixka rixko tyechijne.
Penekanwu xawakni wnikanrupa tyegogne chinanupa.
Pkaspukinwu wmukochire wixa pixka wkaspukyana pnu wixjene.
Gi wa yantaletikowakako putakanwu, seyni pushatkapanunwu galikaka mugletuya. Kaspukyegkaklewlu
