=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Pab tummad karta pab Jesucristobal igal-pin mezhijad (kvnNT, ISO 639-3 kvn), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Anmal-Pab-nikpa-neggin-maidye,
imi tulemal pegin chogmalalen, nabirin:
Pe-nugdin chwilimakalye.
Tenal pe-neg-takmaid nonikilen, nabirbalinye.
Tenal nikpa-neggin pe-itolegedbal pél immal-kunaidyob,
iti-napkin pél immal-kunaimogalen, nabirbalinye.
Imi pe anka imisga mas-kunned ukoye.
Pabye, imi tulemal-ankin-walmajadbal
an nega iskued pelgus chogzhadyob,
an-pegin-walmajad pe anka pelgus chogmogoye.
Imi pedin wilub-takleged-naidzhik,
pe melle an-palmioye.
Tenal an-iskuedgin-yoledanikid-akar pe an-onobaloye.
(Imi pedin ilagwen-nadgu pél neg-takmaiye.
Tegin pe pel-kwapa-kannaleged nikabalye,
tegin pe nug pela-pela okannolenonibaloye. Pitogua.)”
