Thai Phum

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Thai Phum Holy Bible (cth), © 2020 Batupuei Christian Fellowship, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Kalvan kho kaawm maimae na Pa, na min barinae awm lawiseh.
Na uknaeram phat lawiseh. Na lungngainae kalvan vah akuep e patetlah talai van hai kuep lawiseh.
Von kawk khuem rawca sahnin kaimouh na poe awh haw.
Ayâ ni ka tak dawk a yon e ka ngaithoum e patetlah kaimae yonnae hai na ngaithoum haw.
Yon tacueknae dawk ka bawk payon hoeh nahanlah a thoenae koehoi na rungngang haw. Uknaeram, bahu, hoi bawilennae pueng teh a yungyoe nange doeh. Amen. telah ratoum awh.
