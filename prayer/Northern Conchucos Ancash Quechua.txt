[Verified from Señor Jesucristopa alli willacuynin (qxnNT, ISO 639-3 qxn), © 2002 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Cieluchö caycag, Dios Yayalläcuna,
Respetashga shutiqui cuyay catsun.
Mandacuy Reynoyqui nogacunaman shamutsun.
Munangayqui ruracätsun cay patsachö janag patsachönö.
Micuylläcuna llapan junagpag garaycayämay.
Jutsalläcunata gongaycuy, nogacunapis runamajïcunapa jutsanta gongayangänö.
Tentacioncunaman mana ishquiycuyänäpag tsapaycäyämay.
Llapan imayca mana allicunapitapis geshpiycatsiyämay.
Tsaynö catsun.
