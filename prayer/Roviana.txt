[Verified from Buka Hope (rug, ISO 639-3 rug), © 2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tamamami pa Maṉauru,
Mani tava lavata sa Pozamu Hopena,
va mae ia koa gami sa Butubutu Binaṉara Tamugoi,
Mua hiniva sapu tavetia Agoi pa Maṉauru,
si Mu guni nia tu Goi koa gami pa pepeso.
Mu ponini gami ginani ṉinoroi meke ninae rane.
Mu taleosoni gami koari na mami sinea,
gua sapu taleoso ni gami saripu hoke ṉoṉovala mae koa gami.
Mu lopu turaṉa lani gami pa tinoketoke d ,
ba Mamu harupu va seu gami koe Setani.
