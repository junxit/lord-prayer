=== Traditional ===
[Verified from In yancuic mononotzalistli ica Totecohtzin Jesucristo (ncjNT, ISO 639-3 ncj), © 1979 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tohueyitahtzin aquin ilohuac neluicac,
mahuesticatzintli Tehhuatzin.
Huan maquintequiutilo nican tlalticpac,
ijcon quen mochiutoc neluicac.
Matechmacalo axan in tlacuali tlen techpolohua.
Matechtlapohpoluilo totlahtlacoluan, ijcon quen tehhuanten otiquintlapohpoluihque in aquin techchihuilihtoque in ahmo cuali.
Huan ahmo matechcahualo tlen techyoltilanas, huan matechmaquixtilo den ahmo cuali.
Tleca tehuaxca in tequihuahyotl, huan in chicahualistli, huan in mahuestilistli para nochipa. Ijcon mayeto.
