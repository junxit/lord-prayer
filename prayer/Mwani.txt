Kimwani

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Habari Ngema (wmw), © 2013 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Baba wetu uri binguni,
rikurisiwe zina rako,
uje ufalume wako,
vitendiwe vyausaka
kamba vitendiwa mbinguni,
na mulumwengu‑mu vitendiwe novyo.
Tipe luziki rero ratisakula kila suku.
Tiswamii makosa etu,
kamba ofwe novyo vyatiwaswamii watikosa.
Usitase kutingira ushetwani,
fala tinusuru mmakono mwa Mbaya.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
