Dholuo

=== Traditional ===
Wuonwa manie polo,
nyingi mondo omi luor.
Lochni mondo obi,
kendo dwaroni mondo otimre e piny kaka timore e polo.
Miwa chiemo moromowa kawuono,
bende wenwae richowa,
kaka waweyo ni joma timonwa marach.
Kik iwe wadonj e tem,
to reswa e richo.

Nimar loch gi teko kod duong' gin magi nyaka chieng'. Amin.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
