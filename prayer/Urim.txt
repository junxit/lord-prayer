=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kla Weten A Maur Wailen Lam (urim, ISO 639-3 uri), © 2024 SIL Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

'Yaiyai amenen kaino kitnong, men lala nang akitnen pa itni klalen wakget.
Kitn itni wailen ikglen men wrong kin a kipman nimpon kweikwei wrongkwail pa. Kitn kunun wakrongen alkitn pa ntokg kweikwei itni kanokg ti kol a kitn antokg itna kaino kitnong pa.
Kitn lko okipma kol a antiwe am ti.
Kitn ungkwan paipmpaipm amenen ti, kol men ungkwan paipmpaipm a tu antokg kul men ti ase.
Kitn iyewo ngkom nilokgen Maur Paipm a ningkailo la men ngko kai paipmpaipm.'
