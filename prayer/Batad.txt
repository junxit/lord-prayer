=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nan Hapit Apo Dios (ifb, ISO 639-3 ifb), © 2018 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Amamin wad abuniyan,
e'gonan da'a ni' ahan an Dios hinan tatagu.
Ya He"ay mumpapto' goh hitun luta
ti nan pohdom an ma'at hitun luta ya umat goh hi ad abuniyan.
Idatmu ni' di itanudmih a'alga'algaw,
ya aliwam ni' di baholmi
ti ten aliwanmi goh di numbaholan di i'ibbamin da'mi.
Ya adi ni' da'mi tapngan,
ya baliwan da'mi ta adi da'mi itudul hinan diyablu.
