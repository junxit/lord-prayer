=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Widi'zh che'n Jesukrist kwa'n kinu yalnaban (zcaNT, ISO 639-3 zca), © 2013 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Xuz no, Me nzho lo yibë',
dox ñal di'zh rë men, nazhon Go;
nzhakla no, rë men yizhyuo ziyël le' Go mandad lo bixa',
nela xmod nile' Go lo yibë' ga';
bine' Go kwa'n gao no, no kwa'n naki'n no zilita';
biya'l laxto' Go rë kwa'n gat lë' nile' no lo Go;
sa' ska ya'l laxto' no rë kwa'n gat lë' nile' men lo no.
No nala't Go zenbe' Mizhab no (wi' Mizhab xmod nak no);
gulo' Go no lo rë kwa'n nzhakla xa' li'nu xa' no.
