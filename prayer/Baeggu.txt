Baegu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Na Alangaia Falu 'ana Baea 'i Baegu (bvd), © 2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Maa kameli 'i salo,
Alu toae ki sui daka sae'inito 'ada 'ana satamu ne abu.
Lea mai 'oko 'inito naa 'ana doo ki sui guu.
Alu toae ki sui guu daka ade 'ada sulia doori lamu lao fera ne 'i ano mala lau guu da ilia lao fera 'i salo.
Fale mai si fanga kameli uri tari'ina ka bobola fai nia.
'Oko manata luke kameli faasia garo laa kameli ki, suli meli manata lukea lau guu toaa da ilia doo garo ki fuameli.
'Osi lukasi kameli lau fua ilitooe ka linge kameli.
Tafalangai kameli kau faasia 'abana waa ta'aa bae.’
