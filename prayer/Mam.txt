Qyool Mam

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mam Central (Comitancillo) (mamC), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ay qMan Dios, ate toj kyaꞌj, noqit tzꞌel kynikyꞌxjal qa xjanxixjo tbꞌiy.
Noqit tkuꞌtzjo Tkawbꞌila tzaluꞌn twutz txꞌotxꞌ,
tuꞌntzintla tbꞌantjo tajbꞌila tzaluꞌn,
tzeꞌnkuxjo nbꞌant toj kyaꞌj.
Qꞌontzjiy qwaꞌy nimxix te qꞌij jaꞌlin.
Ex najsimjiy qila, a o bꞌant quꞌn,
tzeꞌnku qeꞌ, nkubꞌ qnajsiꞌn kyilxjal nchi bꞌinchin mibꞌin qiꞌja.
Mi ttziyiy tuꞌn qkubꞌ tzꞌaqa toj jun joybꞌil, aj ttzaj qiꞌja;
qalaꞌ qo tkloma toj tqꞌobꞌ tajaw il.
Quꞌn tex te tkyaqil Tkawbꞌil, ex nimx te tipin, ex jniꞌ tqoptzꞌajiyila jaꞌlin, ex te jun majx.
