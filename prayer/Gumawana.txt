=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Buki Kimaasabaina (gvs, ISO 639-3 gvs), © 2019 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Tamama guma Yábana, yoim kakimasabe.
Kūma kukalibubūma bei kai tomota liliuma poyapoya ame goi im nuwonúwana kaguinuwe nakae tauyadi da yábana siguuinuwe.
Ama máliya ame manuna neta kuvinīma.
Ima goyo neta kunuwotaōidi nakae kai semao idi goyo yaima kanuwotaoidi.
Neta taabu kutaagonēma bei vakakona goi kaakapusi, go sem goyo goi neta kuyavēma.”
