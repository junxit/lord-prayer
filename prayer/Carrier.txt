=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yak'usda Ooghuni (crxNTpo, ISO 639-3 crx), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘NeBá yak'uz sinda.
Nyoozi' dóti'.
Dahoont'oh whe lerwe inli,
'et cha nets'ó toolts'it.
Nts'en'a la hukwa' ninzun, 'et ndo honeh.
'Ink'ez ndet la yak'uz ts'e hukwa' ninzun dahooja la, ndi yun k'ut cha,
'et whuz un'a za 'et dúhoneh.
Ndai 'andit dzin uzti'ulh-i,
negha nín'aih.
'Uyoonne bulh dulhyoh ts'uwhulh'ai de,
buba whunats'odoolnoh.
'Et whuz un'a za nelubeshi neba oonaoodolnoh.
'Ink'ez ntsi'-un, 'en ch'a neghinli.
'Et yak'uz k'une' 'int'en-un 'int'oh, 'ink'ez be ilhtus-i cha,
'ink'ez bé dinti'-i cha, 'i 'ilhiz wheni 'i ts'iyawh nch'e' 'unt'oh.
'Et ndo honeh.’
