Nagamiz

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Indian Standard Version (ISV) Nagamese (nag), © 2019 Bridge Connectivity Solutions, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amikhan laga Baba jun sorgote ase,
Apuni laga naam pobitro hoi kene untcha hobi.
Apuni laga sorgo ahibole dibi.
Apuni laga itcha hobole dibi, etu prithibi te jineka sorgote ase.
Amikhan ke hodai din laga kha luwa dibi.
Amikhan laga dhar khan maph kori dibi,
Jineka ami pora amikhan laga dhar thaka khan ke maph kori dise.
Amikhan ke porikha te giri bo nadibi, kintu biya jon pora amikhan ke bachai lobi.’
