=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dibabawon Manobo texts (did, ISO 639-3 mbd), © 1988 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amoy noy no diyà to langit, matahudan podon to ngadan nu.
Umabut podon to pagsakup nu dini to kalibutan. Matuman podon to pagbo-ot nu dini to kalibutan no angod dà kunto-on no natuman on diyà to langit.
Bogayi koy nu podon kunto-on no adow to kapookanan noy no ogkako-on noy.
Ogpasayluhon koy nu to saà noy ikow su pigpasaylu noy on to nakasaà dini kanami.
Og-aha-on koy nu dow wadà buwa dini kanami no igkahimu ni Satanas no pa-antihan. Dì pagawangon nu nasì si Satanas dini kanami.’ “Na og-angodon now sikan no pag-ampù.
