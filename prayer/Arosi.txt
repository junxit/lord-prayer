=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Taroha Goro mana Usuusu Maea (aia, ISO 639-3 aia), © 2005 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Amameeu na'i aro,
na atamu 'ai maea.
Na houramu 'ai boi.
Na hei'irisimu rai haua na'i anoi bahai
'onaai haua ada na'i aro.
Haua tana'ameu i mahoingau 'anai dangini.
'Oi 'adoma'i nugasi'ameu 'ini'i ora-ora'a amee'i
'onaai 'adoma'i nugasi'i amee'i nei ra haa'i 'ino bwarasi'ameu.
Ma'oi 'a'auhi'ameu 'anai ura bwarasi'i heiohongi
ma'oi taatahi bwarasi'ameu baania Saetan.
