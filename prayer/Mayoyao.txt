=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mayoyao Ifugao (ifu, ISO 639-3 ifu), © 2003 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Jos Ama an gawwa ad langit, he-a adniꞌ chi daydayawon chi tatakhu.
He-a adniꞌ chi mun-ap-apo ay chaꞌmi.
Hini adniꞌ penhod mu chi aton mi heto lota an amat ay ni khun cha aton hina ad langit.
Ichat mu khu hay anun mi ad ugwani an arkhaw.
Ja pakawanom an amin chi pfahor mi, amat khu ay ni pammakawan mi hay hana pfimmahor ay chaꞌmi.
Ja i-achawwim ay chaꞌmi hana atopngan an achi ami paꞌ-ahchor, ta achi chaꞌmi apfakon ay Satanas.’
