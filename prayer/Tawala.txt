[Verified from YAUBADA A WOGATALA WOUNA (tbo, ISO 639-3 tbo), © 1985, 2014 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc. and Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Amiyai, tam hoi yada e memae.
Gowam to wiyateyateya.
Om ani taniwaga una wogeleteya.
Om nugotuhu to dewaya hoi hipuli
mei yada tu miyena he dewedeweya pite.
I yam iyeta geka binei una weliyai.
I apapoe una paligigilihi po mei iyawoi tu wiapapoeniyai to paligigilihi.
Ma ega una palihaliyai po ludadana baneihi tona ialonihi.
Ma apapoe uyahinei tam una haloiyai,
[babana tam om ani taniwaga po om manini po om wasawasa amalai po houga tuwetuwenai. Moina].
