kasɩm

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kasem (xsm), © 1997 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‹Dɩbam Ko wʋlʋ na wʋ wɛɛnɩ tɩn,
sɩ nmʋ yɩrɩ na tiǝ dɩ zulǝ,
sɩ nmʋ paarɩ ba,
sɩ nmʋ wʋbʋŋa kɩ lʋgʋ baŋa nɩ,
nɩ kʋ na kɩ n sɔŋɔ nɩ te tɩn.
Pa dɩbam wʋdiu zɩm sɩ kʋ maŋɩ dɩbam.
Yagɩ dɩ́ lwarɩm n ma n cɛ dɩbam,
nɩ dɩbam dɩ na yagɩ dɩ́ ma cɛ dɩ́ donnǝ te tɩn.
Yɩ pa dɩ́ na maŋɩm dɩlʋ na wʋ́ pa dɩ́ tʋ tɩn.
Nan vrɩ dɩbam wo-lwaanʋ tu jɩŋa nɩ.
(Nmʋ te paarɩ dɩ dam dɩ tiǝ
sɩ kʋ taa ve maŋa kalʋ na ba ti tɩn. Amina.)›
