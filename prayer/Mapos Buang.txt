=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ġaġek Mewis (bzh), © 2003 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘He Amamin sën nedo yaġek,
mehönon debo vabuung in arëm.
Ġenam ġenatu he Mehöböp noh vu nyëġ pin,
gemehönon pin degevonġ noh vu kwam gëp dob
nabë sën angër denevonġ ggëp yaġek lo.
Ġebo nos vu he vu pehi sënë.
Lob ġedahun he nġaa nipaya na
nabë sën he nedahun nġaa nipaya sën mehönon denevonġ vu he lo ya.
Su gweli he na vu pelëpinsën,
gaḳ mu gweḳo he vër in nġaa nipaya pin.
(In honġ Nyëġ og Nyëġ anon, gehonġ Anutu los niwëëk,
garëm og böpata rot kesuu nġaa pin, neggëp degwata los degwata. Yönon!)’
