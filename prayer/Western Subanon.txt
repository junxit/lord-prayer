=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Subanon, Western (suc, ISO 639-3 suc), © 1996 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Amaꞌ nami sog glangit, pogodaban og ngalan mu poꞌ mosigda a.
Bu sabaꞌ nami dun matong na dini og kopogdatuꞌ mu. Bu og glegan mu motuman dini sog glupaꞌan mamaꞌ nog mituman dituꞌ sog glangit.
Bogoyoy mu ami nog ponganon nami gondow koni.
Bu ompunoy mu na og dusaanan nami poꞌ ami siꞌoy, inompunan nami na ain og mikosalaꞌ dianami.
Bu naꞌ mu ami potiangon koyubaꞌan, sugaꞌid non, ipat mu ami sog bayaꞌ ni Kindagow.’
