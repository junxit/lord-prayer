[Verified from Lilaghanu lya shonu (ndj, ISO 639-3 ndj), © 2014 The Word for the World and Pioneer Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tati wetu yuuveli kumbindi,
litawa lyaku lijumiwi.
Wutwa waku wisi,
liwudayila ligoleki apa pamlima ngimu wuligoleka kula kumbindi.
Utugayili filivi fya nalelu.
Tupomeli fituvifyili,
ngimu yufwi wutuvapomela vala vatuvifyili.
Kotoo kututaya munyeshiwu,
lakini wutuwilyepeshi na yula mwafu. Kwanongwa wutwa ndi waku na liwovi na ukomi mbaka jola. Yina.’
