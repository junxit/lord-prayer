Tǎa vigi ŋa a chhɔ po nɛ,
ghaʼo nu pɔ ni ligi yɔ.
Shaʼafuoŋ yɔ thɔ,
nnu ŋa ɔ tāʼa nu nɛ pɔ,
ŋkuoŋ shhɛ nɔ haʼaŋ a pɔ po nɛ.
Fɛ maoŋ ŋa a pie ŋa pigi yru ŋkuoŋ ghɔ shiʼa nɛ ni pigi.
Ndɔ ndīʼɛ phɨ yigi,
nɔ haʼaŋ pigi ŋkaa pigi ghà ndīʼɛ phɨ ŋgwa pɛ ŋa pugu fāaŋ mbɨŋ pigi nɛ.
Kiʼi nchwīe pigi nii moŋ tɨtɨnɨ mōoŋ nu
ndɔ kwe vigi mbhɔ ŋoŋ mbɨmbhɨ.
