Monkole

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Akabuu Titɔ̃i Idei Ilaaɔ (mkl), © 2006 SIM International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ilɑɑɔ̃ Bɑɑ nwɑ iyi í wɑ lele,
Jɔ̀ inɛ fei ku mɑ̀ iyi ɑwɔi ì jɛ inɛ kumɑ́.
Nɑɑ jɛ bommɑɛ.
Jɔ̀ inɛ fei ku ce idɔɔbiɛ si ilɛu ihɛ̃ bɛi ɑ̀ wɑɑ ce lele.
Mu nwɑ ijɛi ɑjɔ fei nwɑ.
Kpɑ idei dulum du wɑ bɛi ɑwɑ mɔ ɑ̀ yɑ kɑ kpɑ idei kurɑrɑ iyi inɛ ŋɑ ɑ̀ ce nwɑ.
Mɑɑ̀ ti i jɔ̀ kɑ dɑsi kulɛlɛ,
Ammɑ nyɑ wɑ hɑi si ɑwɔi Seetɑm.
[Domi ɑwɔi ì nɛ bommɑ do ɡbuɡbɑ̃ do ɑmboe hee do ɑjɔ fei. Ami.]
