lal in Mwoakilloa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mokilese Bible (mkj), © 2019 Mokilese Bible Translation Committee, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Jamamai nehnloannge,
oadoammwen en joaroawi,
oamw wei en pwoaida,
kupwuroamwen en wiawi pohn jampah doahroar nehnloannge.
Kihdoa rehnnoauwe kanmai mwingehn oauwoau rehn.
Mahkoahng kamai amai japwung kan,
doahroar amai kin mahkoahng irai kan ma wia japwung oang kamai;
joah kahlwahng kamai nehn jongjong,
kapisla kamai jang ipen ih ma naunahu.
[Oamw mwei ioarroar Mwei, Manman apel Lingwa allaluhla.
Amen.]’

Same as the Traditional form above.
