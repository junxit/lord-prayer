Ch'ol

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Jini wen bʌ tʼan (ctu76), © 1976 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

C Tyat lojon, ya' bʌ añet ti panchan,
la' ch'ujutisʌntiquet.
La' yumintiquet.
La' mejlic chʌ bʌ a wom wʌ' ti mulawil, che' bajche' mi mejlel chʌ bʌ a wom ya' ti panchan.
Aq'ueñon lojon c waj i c'ʌjnibal bʌ lojon c cha'an ti ili bʌ q'uin.
Ñusʌbeñon lojon c mul che' bajche' mi lojon c ñusʌbeñob i mul lojon c pi'ʌlob je'el.
Cʌñʌtyañon lojon cha'an mach cac' lojon c pʌyol ti mulil, coloyon lojon ti i p'ʌtyʌlel jini mach'ʌ wen.
Como a cha'añʌch a yumintyel yic'ot a p'ʌtyʌlel yic'ot a ñuclel ti pejtyelel ora. Amén.
