=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Luwo (lwo, ISO 639-3 lwo), © 2003 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Wur wan ne Maalo,
nyingi ke wuoro.
Beehn ne Ruohdh wan.
Mari ke yugo Paar Maalo kedea wiih piny thiow.
Gihn me cam wane tihn,
cub ngo yihr wan tihn noono ni.
Wii anyoohne piny yihr wan.
Neehn ma wii wane giih anyoohne piny yihr nyoge thiow.
Wa keá meehg pahdho yihdh giih utum.
Abea lony wan oogo yi gihn me raaj.’
