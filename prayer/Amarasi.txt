=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Uisneno In Kabin ma Prenat: Rais Manba'an Fe'u nok Reta' Ahun-hunut (aaz, ISO 639-3 aaz), © 1999-2014 Unit Bahasa dan Budaya, Kupang NTT, Indonesia, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amaꞌ abit sonaf neno tunan! Ho kanam naan nakninuꞌ teeb.
Maut he areꞌ mansian ein ok-okeꞌ nbin pah-pinan ia ntunaꞌ ma naꞌratan Ho kanam maꞌtaniꞌ naan, tua.
Maut he Ho mjair Usif meu mansian ein okeꞌ, tua!
Maut he bifee-atoniꞌ sin arsin anmoꞌen natuin Ho preent ein anbin pah-pinan ia,
nahuum on reꞌ Ho ameupt ein anmoeꞌ natuin Ho preent ein etan sonaf neno tunan, tua.
Amturun ma mbaab maan kai nok bukaet reꞌ nanokab ansuun neno, tua.
Hai mtoit Ko he Ho msaok main hai sanat ma penu sin,
nahuum on reꞌ hai mnikan ma mniahan main tuaf-tuaf reꞌ anmoꞌen sanan neu kai, tua.
Ampafaꞌ ma mpanat kai he hai kais ammoeꞌ rais amreꞌut ma maufinu.
Ma msoi mufetin kai naꞌko niut reuꞌf ee in kuasan, tua.
[Natuin Ho kuum, Usif reꞌ ammuiꞌ kuasa maꞌtaniꞌ ma mapinaꞌ-makraah reꞌuf.
Ho kuum es reꞌ anaaꞌ aprenat nabar-baar, tua.
Hai onen ma baisenut naꞌtuuk ma naꞌpaar on reꞌ naan, tua. Amin.]’
