=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Biblia Takatifu (sbk), © 2020 Door43 Worldwide Mission Community, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Dada witu uwahumwanya itawa lyaho lyimishilwaje.
Ubhumwini waho winze, ulugano lwaho lubhombeshe pansi na humwanya.
Utipele ishalye shitu insiku zyonti.
Utukhobhoshele amalando gitu, ndeati shetihubhakhobhoshela abhimalando bhitu.
Usahatipe injelo, utidime nu setano.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
