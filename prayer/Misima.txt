=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Bateli Vavaluna (mpx, ISO 639-3 mpx), © 1947, 1997, 2011, 2016, 2018 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tamama labulabumwa,
alam bwabwalena ama gamagal naha awatauwan,
wam logugui ni nem,
wam nuwatu gamagal naha ginol panayawiya
ni ola hi giginol labulabumwa.
Lan ya enona nu pem eliyama.
Wama gegi nu nuwayoho,
ni ola alomeyau wali gegi iyaka ha nuwayoho.
Bahi nu tetelema lawakik elana,
yaa nu pwamwalima gegi elana.
Kaiwena owa tologugui, owa togasisi ge owa towasawasa,
sauga gegewena nige ni momowasi. Ibwe. I ola.
