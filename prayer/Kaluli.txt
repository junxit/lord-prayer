Bosavi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Godeya꞉ To Nafayo꞉ We (bco), © 1998-2022 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Do Gode, Hebenelo꞉ a꞉labo꞉ ge,
Gi wiyo꞉ mada malilo꞉le dofo꞉lab ko꞉lo꞉ kaluka꞉isale iliyo꞉ gi wi a꞉no꞉ wabuluma꞉ki ta꞉foma.
Gi kaluka꞉isalelo꞉ bo꞉fo꞉mela꞉no꞉ ho꞉len a꞉no꞉ fa꞉la꞉doma꞉ki ta꞉foma.
Go꞉no꞉n asulo꞉ ko꞉lo꞉ Hebene amilo꞉ fa꞉la꞉do alifa꞉ o꞉leaumbo꞉,
henfelo꞉ wenamio꞉lo꞉ kaluka꞉isaleyo꞉ gilo꞉ asulab au kudu mena꞉ki ta꞉foma.
O꞉gdo꞉ nililo꞉ ma꞉no꞉wo꞉ aumbo꞉ dimina꞉ki so꞉lo꞉l.
Mogago꞉ nolba꞉lo꞉ nimo꞉lo꞉ dimida꞉lab amilo꞉ nililo꞉ ga꞉lila꞉sen o꞉leaumbo꞉,
giyo꞉ mogago꞉ nililo꞉ dimido꞉wo꞉lo꞉ hala꞉sa꞉ga꞉ ga꞉lilema.
Nio꞉ mogago꞉lo꞉ dimidama꞉no꞉ a꞉namida꞉yo꞉ ha꞉nabena꞉ki, giyo꞉ nio꞉ tili dowa꞉bi.
Mogago꞉lelo꞉ dimida꞉sen Sa꞉da꞉na꞉ eyo꞉ nio꞉ yasa danela꞉ma꞉ib ko꞉lo꞉, giyo꞉ nio꞉ sili alifoma. Godemo꞉ towo꞉ a꞉la꞉dulugu sa꞉la꞉bi.
