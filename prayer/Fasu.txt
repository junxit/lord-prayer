Namo Me

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kotimo Kawe Someaka Masane Oyapo (faa), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the opening quotation mark the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Atao. Ne isiapo Ata hepene yapura rekenapo. Ne yano motosa kakaro kára haku kotesa susuane makama fanahoasimoripo.
Aporo sumo himua ne unihaeaka rekeasimo hemakapurakaripo. Hepene yapura nómo ní hemakapusua aina ayiáka, hauaka wo soko ayiakasimo hemakapurakaripo.
Pipu makata nakosane pipu yia nasimo, siaraka makanie.
Aporo metamo isuaki mokoatikirakano, isiapo ropo mokoatikinane mafaraka mamerarurakano asekea, isiapo watikisane soko, nómo mafaraka mameraruhoanie.
Isu kau aseakosane peakohoamo nómo isu asekea rekeanie. Satanemo isu mokopipihokohoamo isu mafasinie.’ Aíyaka kurikurihoanie.
