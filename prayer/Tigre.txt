Tigre (ISO 639-3 "tig"; Eritrea/Sudan, Ge'ez script) is a distinct language from Tigrinya (ትግርኛ); no Tigrinya text was substituted here.

A genuine Tigre New Testament exists (Asmara 1902, rev. 1934; the 1931 printing) and a complete Tigre Bible — ክታብ ቅዱስ "Kitab Qidus," 1988, Bible Society of Ethiopia / United Bible Societies — both in Ge'ez script and both containing Matthew 6:9-13. The verbatim Tigre Lord's Prayer could NOT be retrieved here: there is no online text edition, and the only located genuine-Tigre rendering (christusrex.org, "TIGRÉ (Khasa/Xasa)," from the 1931 Asmara NT) exists only as scanned JPEG images that could not be OCR-read in this session. To avoid fabricating a sacred text, no verbatim Tigre wording is given.
