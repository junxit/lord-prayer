=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Amo New Testament (amo), © 2020 Door43 World Missions Community, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

'Ucif bit nanya kitene kani, kusu lissafe.
Na tigofe dak, na isu imon nayife nyii nafo na idin sue kitene kani.
Nanari kitimone imonli liyirin.
Kala nari tire bite, nafo na arike wang din kalzu na lenge na tidin dortu nani tire.
Tutun na uwa do ninghirik kitin maluzu ba, ama bolo nari ncara shintan: bara kipin tigowe kinfere, likara nin gongon uyaunu nene udu saligan. Usonani.'
