Kaqchikel

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from GT:cak:Kaqchikel (cak), © 2007 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Katata' Dios ri ratc'o chicaj,
Xtinimirises ta c'a ri lok'olaj abi'.
Can ta nic'uje' yan ruchuk'a' ri a-gobierno pa quivi' conojel,
Y achel yanimex ri chicaj, can ta quiri' mismo choch'ulef.
Ri kavay ri nic'atzin k'ij-k'ij, ja jun ri vacami taya' chike.
Y tabana' perdonar ri kamac, achel roj kabanon perdonar ri quibanon mac chike.
Man kojaya' richin chi nicanox chikij chi nkuka pa mac, xa kojacolo' chuvech ri itzel.
Quiri' ri nikac'utuj chave, roma ja rat yabano gobernar ronojel, c'o avuchuk'a', y c'o ak'ij richin jumul. Amén.
