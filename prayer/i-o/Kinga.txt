Mahanji

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mahanji Bible (zgam), © 2020 Door 43 International Mission Community, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Daada veito vakhukyanya, eilitawa lyakho leivalanchiwe.
Uludeeva lwakho lwinchage, uvunogwe vwakho vukongiwagwe pasi apa nda khuyanya.
Utupinchange ifinu fyieto ifya eilelo iyi.
Utusyiekhilage inogwa ncheito, ndufwe vutukhuvasyiekhila avanyanogwa khulyufwe.
Ulekhe ukhutuveikha palugelu, pu utupokhe pa mbivi, pu uludeva lwakho na makha gakho nu vudwadwa vwakho sikhu nchooni.
