Gaina

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from God Da Geka Seka (kpr), © 1984, 2023 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Namanenda Afa, utuda iresa! Ninda javo kakara use jighoro erare!
Utuda ninda uju avanembo siroreraira. Kau daba aminga ava, enda eminda sirorare! Amingare dae sedo, fu namane kaifa ururaso, namonde irore!
Oroko eminda, namanendae bayau mutaso baore!
Evetu genembo namanekena ari ekoko urureoro, namane gido kote doyeraera. Aminga ava, namanenda ari ekoko eraera amo, kote doyaso dae resera!
Erama namane derarikena budo yeose! A Satan da derari aminda, namanendae kosege fetaetesi!’ Kau aminge banungevu!

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
