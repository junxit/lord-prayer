=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mamamili Wangka (mpj, ISO 639-3 mpj), © 1999 Wycliffe Bible Translators Australian, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Mama! Nyuntungan ngurra ngarnawarrapurluka, yilta Mama-lampajun nyininpa. Nintijula-lanyaju!, nyuntulajunta marninypungkuraku.
Ngampurrju-lanyaju kanyinma!, nyuntumili walyjakaja. Maaja majulu-lanyaju nyuntulu nintijula!, junga-lajungku nyinaraku. Mamamili wikarrukajaya junga nyininpa nyuntuku, palunyayuru-lajungku junga nyinaraku.
Yuwa-lanyaju mirrka!, ngalkuralaju karrpuparaku karrpungka.
Jamparni martu kujupalu putamalku, mirtarna puntajilu putamalku, yankurnalu wangkaku kawalyaliju nyinakuraku. Jilanyarnalu wangkaku, ka palujanu ngayulurnanta japini, walykukajaju warniwa!
Jampa-lanyaju muunpungku malpulu, mirtalaju kulilkuka ngapilku, paki. Ngampurrju-lanyaju kanyinma!, malpulu-lanyaju kujupawana kangkujaku. Yiltan nyuntu maaja majuwarta-lampajun, wulikajaku. Nyuntun kunyjunyu, maparn majuwinti nyininpa. Wulun jilanya nyuntu nyinamalpa. Amin.’
