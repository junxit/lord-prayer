Ömie

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from God-are Jögoru I'oho (aom), © 1991 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Nosi apajo. Öꞌidöre hijanuoho
jero ae ahoꞌobëhe nosi dë vövöbajoꞌoho avohahuego
noro ja gemu mae uehorovavoꞌirarëjo.
Jero böröme namiromo ahoꞌobëhe muebejavuego
no jasi aharire gemu raromoꞌirarëjo.
Jasi aharire gemu raromoromo jaehu nimanuoho ruhe öꞌidöre mae ejaharuëhi
ave saꞌarohuro ëhi ejahoꞌirarëjo.
Nosi ioho jaruvo iꞌimamuijego no ueꞌahego daꞌuaguajo.
Nosi sisëho uehorovoromo vuonugonëjo.
Ae göehu no sisë vaejavuego noehu uehorovoromo vuonugaruëhi jero ëhi ajamuinëjo.
Nadi vöröꞌeroho ramuego rireromavuoꞌi
aꞌi ajamuinego A Sisëho bogo ruahöꞌöjavuoꞌirajo.
