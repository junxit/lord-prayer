Nara

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Luva'ana'i Namana Makamakana (nrz), © 2024 Global Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Kamamai e, vutuvutuai omimia.
Oni vamu 'eva lai bavakabukabua.
Emu lovia vanuana emai,
boumaiai emu ula lai bavavaia kanobatai, vitaitana vutuvutuai devavaia boina.
Vali'u melala kanania amai lai aulaveni davata bovenimai.
Kau kataua kele'emaiai devavai si'asi'a kauta eta si'avana
lai a'ou kao,
vitaitana emai si'avana lai avavai davata 'ou kao.
Sia bovada'amai ivakovo laloatai,
beia diabolo enai bovamaulimai.
(Lovia vanuana oni emu, Siavu oni emu, Nuavi oni emu, beda'amo bemia keinikeini. Amen.)’
