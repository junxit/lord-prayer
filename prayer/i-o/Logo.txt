=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Logo (Logoti) (log, ISO 639-3 log), © 2015 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‹Àma kɨ Atá bhù na dhɨ,
lè móndyá wä́yi dhɨ ɨ̀ kònì dhɨ, mɨ Gìká lólo ꞌɨ dhɨ be.
Ámɨ Òpɨ̀ kàtsá móndyá wä́yi ɨ kòfalé wà.
Tà mɨ́ dré adrélé lèle ꞌòle dhɨ ɨ̀ kòꞌo ru kònwa bvò dri,
ngóró dré adrélé ꞌòle bhù na dhɨ tɨ́nɨ.
Mɨ́ kàfè àma dré mápà kɨtswálépi àma sè kìtú ándrò kònɨ̀dhɨ sè dhɨ wà.
Mɨ́ kòtrì àma kɨ tàkonzɨ̀ ɨ,
ngóró mà dré kpà adrélé móndyá tàkonzɨ̀ ꞌobhá àma rú dhɨ kya kɨ tri dhɨ tɨ́nɨ.
Mɨ́ kòtayɨ́ àma tà dré tabhìle, àma kɨ ꞌòzo dhèle tàkonzɨ̀ na dhɨ ko.
Be ró dhɨ, mɨ̀ kàpá àma Dhya kònzɨ drɨ́gásè.›
