Nāhuatl

=== Traditional ===
Totahtzine, in ilhuicac timoyetztica,
ma yectenehualo in motocatzin.
Ma hualauh in motlahtocayotzin.
Ma chihualo in tlalticpac motlanequilitzin yuh in ilhuicac.
In totlaxcal momoztlae totechmonequi, ma axcan xitechmomaquili,
ihuan xitechmopopolhuili in totlahtlacol,
in yuh tiquintlapopolhuiah in techtlatlacalhuiah,
ihuan macahmo xitechmomacahuili tihuetzizqueh ipan teneyeyecoltiliztli,
ma xitechmomaquixtili in ihuicpah in ahmo cualli.

Ma yuh mochihua. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.

(Traditional = the classical Catholic "Totahtzine," from the 17th-century devotional tradition; like the Latin/Vulgate rite it ends "Ma yuh mochihua. Amen" without the doxology. A Protestant Nahuatl New Testament renders the doxology as: "Timitztlahtlaniliah yejhua in pampa ticpia tequihuahyotl, niman poder, niman hueyilistli para nochipa. Amén." — that line belongs to a different, modern regional Nahuatl text and orthography and is not part of the classical Totahtzine above.)
