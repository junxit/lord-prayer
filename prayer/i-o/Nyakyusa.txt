Ngonde

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ʉlwitɨkano Ʉlʉpya (nyy), © 2019 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Taata gwɨtʉ ʉgwa kʉmwanya,
ɨngamu jaako jɨɨmɨkigwege.
ɄBʉnyafyale bwako bwisege.
Ɨsi kʉlonda sibombigwege pakiisʉ apa,
bo ʉlwa kʉmwanya.
Ʉtʉpege kʉkʉtɨ isikʉ ɨfindʉ fyɨtʉ.
Ʉtʉswege ʉbʉtʉlanongwa bwɨtʉ,
bo ʉlʉ nuuswe tukʉbaswa boosa aba bikʉtʉla ɨnongwa kʉmyɨtʉ.
Ʉtwepʉsye mu ngelo,
ʉtʉpoke kʉ mbiibi jʉla.
[ɄBʉnyafyale, amaka nʉ bʉsisya, fyako bwila na bwila! Ameni.]’ ”
