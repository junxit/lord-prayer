Ikirundi

=== Traditional ===
Dawe wa twese uri mw'ijuru, izina ryawe nirininahazwe;
ubwami bwawe nibuze; ivyo ushaka nibigirwe kw'isi nko mw'ijuru;
uduhe ivyokurya bidukwiye uyu musi;
uturekurire ibicumuro vyacu,
nk'uko natwe turekurira abatugirira nabi;
ntudutererane ibitwosha nabi,
yamara udukize akabi.

Kuko ubwami n'ububasha n'ubuninahazwa ari ivyawe, gushika ibihe bidashira. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
