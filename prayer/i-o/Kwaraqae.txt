Fiu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Fau Alanga'inga Faolu Ana Ala'anga Kwara'ae (kwf), © 2003 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘MaꞋa kami Ꞌi langi Ꞌae,
Kami oga ngwae ki taꞋifau kike faꞋadoea satamu, osiꞋana nia abu liu.
Dao maꞋi Ꞌani gwaungaꞋi Ꞌanga Ꞌoe fafia maurilana ngwae ki taꞋifau.
Ma na kwaiogaiꞋanga Ꞌoe kike sasia naꞋa Ꞌi ano diꞋia logo Ꞌi langi.
ꞋOke kwatea maꞋi fuamiꞋa na fanga neꞋe bolo fuana asoa neꞋe.
ꞋOke rufuanata ana taꞋangaꞋa kami ki, diꞋia logo neꞋe kami rufuanata ana taꞋangaꞋa ngwae ki taꞋifau neꞋe kira fulia ru taꞋa ki amiꞋa.
ꞋOe kosi danga Ꞌani kami fuana ilitoꞋonga ka liufi kami.
Ma Ꞌoko olitaꞋi kami faꞋasia sa Saetan.’
