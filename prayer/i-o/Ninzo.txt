Ninzam

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ungbamvu Ku Abachi Azhi Ahe̲he̲ Ka (ninNT), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“ ‘Aki munta wa nggo se
nu nkplassu,
di inta gri isa muwa yi ndindi,
nga ni hru iso munta yi ziza nggo,
du anishirr we a kru isisu imuwa ni ingbingbru yi
nâ ta si nggo a di na nu unkplassu nggo.
Ni inta ila iri imunta yi ni ivivi yi.
Ni nggurr ila ure munta yi zhi ki inta,
nâ ta si nggo inta di nggurr zhi ku abangga ba nggo a di kpala ki inta.
Si di inta jaku nu nggo a ti tsarr inta kye hen, ni
kpa inta ttungo na ango ka Umimi wa.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
