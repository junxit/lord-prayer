Ivadoy

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dibshon Mengi-kan Ni Pansigshan (ibl), © 1978 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“ ‘Si‐kam ma Amemi ja ẃared dangit, si‐kam koma i mayshedjaw ni olay.
“ ‘Sota pantoshayan mo, pasebim koma ali, jet sota piyan mo, si‐katoy medeka shiya dobong, ja inges to ngod dangit.
I‐kan mo koma sota kenen mi ni inakew.
Si‐kam i memekawan son si‐kami ni nanbasolan mi, ja inges toy mika pemekawan ni kait min too ja ẃaray nanbasolan sha son si‐kami.
Karam itolok koma ja manbesol kami, no diket si‐kam i mengiredan son si‐kami say eg kami manbesol.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
