=== Traditional ===
[Verified from YAUBADA BONANA VEYAO IVAGUNA Bona Iduna (viv, ISO 639-3 viv), © 1983, 2003 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tamama lobameya umimiyami,
a egana mahamahalina anaveꞋamohimohinena.
Ya abavehimeya ana tova gidalakayemu aseꞋasemeya.
Ya vehimeya lobameya hiꞋitaꞋitaveꞋavina amine,
meda ime babiya adaꞋitaꞋitaveꞋavina.
Aiyeta saꞋeyana saꞋeyana amaga udavelevelema.
Ime nagona tobohiyameyao yadi koyona
avenuwauluyena amine,
meda yama koyona kadu O udavenuwauluyena.
Keke abaꞋaladibidibi againe unahawahegema tu
kaliva koyona Setani againe unalugolegolema. Au hidede amine wanaveꞋoꞋola.
