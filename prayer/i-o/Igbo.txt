Asụsụ Igbo

=== Traditional ===
Nna anyị nke bi nʼeluigwe,
ka edo aha gị nsọ.
Ka alaeze gị bịa.
Ka eme ihe na-achọ,
dịka esi eme ya nʼeluigwe, ka eme kwa otu ahụ nʼụwa.
Nye anyị nri taa nke ga-ezuru anyị nʼụbọchị taa.
Gbaghara kwa anyị ụgwọ niile anyị ji,
dịka anyị onwe anyị si gbaghara ndị ji anyị ụgwọ.
Edubatala anyị nʼime ọnwụnwa,
kama dọpụta anyị nʼaka ajọ onye ahụ.

Nʼihi na alaeze bụ nke gị, na ike, na ebube, ruo mgbe ebighị ebi. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
