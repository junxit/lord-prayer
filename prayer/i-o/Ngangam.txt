Gangam

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Uwien ya Jɔtiefɛ̀nku ya gbɔnku (gngNT), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‹TiʼBaa wà te paaki bó,
cère binib kɛ ń li boh aʼyel,
Cère aʼbɛl ń baa ní,
cère aʼyíem ń tien tingi ni tɛn paaki bó.
De tɛ dinnɔ ya wentunl ya jier,
fère tɛ tiʼbiil,
tɛn tiʼmɔ fèreh ma bà biih tɛ nnɔ.
La cère tí lu mibiikm ni.
Ama fie tɛ usɛnpol ubiɛrɔ ya nuɔ ni.
[Kimɛ sin nɛ si libɛl nin ufaa nin ukpiɛke
uyo wà ŋa ŋmɔbe gbenm.]›
