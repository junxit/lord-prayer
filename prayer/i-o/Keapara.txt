Kalo (Keapara)

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from VEKAPAWAI VALIGUNA (khz), © 2005 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ai Tamamai kupai
goi aramu pene veaga;
gemu Basileia pene vogomai;
gemu ririwa paia kalaa tanoparai,
kupai gekalaana vetaina.
Etoma gama gauganigani ono venimai.
Gema rakava ono tugamagi-piatogara,
ai gevega-rakavamaina talimara
geria rakava gatugamagi-piatogarana gelegelena.
Veripagani ai ragai pono vaimai,
a Rakava Talimana, Satani,
gimana na pono vega-magulimai.
[Kwalana Basileia goi gemu, tiavu goi gemu,
ralema iwavagina goi gemu,
toma pene ago-vanagivanagi, Amen.]’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
