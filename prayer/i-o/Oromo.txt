Afaan Oromoo

=== Traditional ===
Yaa Abbaa keenya, kan samii irra jirtu!
Maqaan kee haa qulqullaawu!
Mootummaan tee haa dhuftu,
fedhiin tee samii irratti akka taatutti, akkasumatti ammaas lafa irrattiis haa taatu!
Soora guyya-guyyaan nu barbaachisu arra nuuf kanni!
Akkuma nuti deeyni namaaf oomfii goonutti,
atiis deeyni teenya oomfii nuuf godhi!
Fitinatti nu hin naqin,
hamaa jalaa nu eewali malee;

mootummaan, humni niis, ulfinni niis baruma baraan keetuma. Aammiin!

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
