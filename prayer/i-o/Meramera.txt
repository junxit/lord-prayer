Melamela

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Laulau na Vinalu mine Salemo (mxm), © 2023 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamane amiteu, oo tubu na langi, aisamu ei ni muina maolo.
Niitealalena minio ni sibitala usinani. Toesagali e amiteu namiteu ogomuli a masagana minio
na magamaga tomane sou ogomuli na langi.
Oo no bilii e amiteu a gona inani mapigogoi a malada seidei ie.
Me oo no lilotase a sinusu mine amiteu,
tomane amiteu lilotase a sinusu sou goli le amiteu.
Oo no ma itetasetase lei e amiteu namiteu bosa na tinovo,
moni gualeliu e amiteu maino na golugolu pagu.’
