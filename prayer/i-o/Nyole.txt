Lunyole

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Endagaano Epyaha ko n'ebitabo ebindi eby'Endagaano Ekayire mu Lunyole (nuj), © 2019 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“ ‘Seefe ali mwigulu,
oŋeewe eŋono,
obuŋugi buwo buuje,
ebi wenda biholewe hu hyalo ngʼolu biholewa mwigulu.
Tufunise ebiryo ebyolwa leero
era tusoniŋe ebibi byefe,
ngʼolu hwesi husoniŋa hiisi abatuhola ebibi.
Era otatuleha hugwa mu huhemewa,
aye tunoŋole mu bubi.
Obuŋugi nʼamaani ko nʼeŋono,
bibyo emirembe nʼemirembe. Amiina.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
