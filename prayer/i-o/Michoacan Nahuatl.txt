Pómaro Nahuatl

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ilajtulisli Yancuic toTata Jesucristo (nclNT), © 1998 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

ToTajtzin hual tiunca pin cielo, ma momajtican moxtin pampa motoca unca hué.
Ma ajsi in tonali quiman tihuelitis pa mochi cajcapa, ma mochihua quinami ticnequi pin lalticpan, quinami yoje unca pin cielo.
Xitechmaca pa tijcuasi hual monequi pa tijcuasi mojmosta.
Huan xicpujpuhua len yes amo cuali timijchihuilije, quiman tehuanten ticpujpuhuaje yehuanten hual tejchihuilije len amo cuali.
Huan amo xilamacahuilti pa techajsis len hual tejchihuilis pa timoijlacusi.
Quema, xitechmacahuili pa camo techmatoca in se hual amo unca cuali, in lili,
pampa unca moyaxca pa tihuelitis huan unca moyaxca mochi lahueliltilisli, huan unca moyaxca in laixpelalisli cualtichin huan in lahueyilisli. Amo mocahuas pa yes yoje.
Yojque ma ye.
