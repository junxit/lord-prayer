Koh

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kuo (xuo), © 2013 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Bǎa ɓuru ká mù kaw nulue,
Ndaɗ ɓay haŋa nzoɓ ha̰a ha̰a lɛ,
ka tuu mù ɓo huo-ɔ kḭ hɔy mù ɓa Ŋgɛrɛwṵru.
Ndaɗ ɓay haŋa mu ví re mbay ká tusiri key.
Ndaɗ ɓay haŋa nzoɓ ha̰a ha̰a ká tusiri key na,
ka ɗaa fe ká law-a hii, munu ká i ɗaa ká nulue na.
Mu ha̰ ɓuru ŋgaw, ɓo ɓuru re maa ɓuru ká vuri key.
Mu nda buma ka ziŋ ɓuru ká tul fe ɗáa ka̰aya ɓuru,
faa mbḭw munu ká ɓuru kara ɓuru nda buma ziŋ nzoɓri
ká ɗaa ka̰aya ziŋ ɓuru nda̰w na.
Mu ha̰ ɓuru se ɓil nzaa úla ya,
roo lɛ, mu ya̰a ɓuru ya̰a mǎa ká ɓil nduo ŋgɛrɛtemndaya.
[Tusuɛke lɛ, réke mbay nda̰w, hṵrusuo nda̰w,
rɔɔ riŋ ɗika nda̰w na ɓa taa ɓo ɓaŋguɔ kpaɗara.
Amɛm. Ndaɗ ɓay haŋa ni ka ɗo munu na kḭ.]”
