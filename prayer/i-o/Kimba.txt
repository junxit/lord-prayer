Tsikimba

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kazuwamgbani ka savu n Tsikimba n aɓon a ɗa a ɗanga̱sai a kazuwamgbani ka cau (kdlNT), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“ ‘Tata u tsunu uza ɗa wa̱ri a zuba. A kirana n kala ka vunu n uwulukpi.
Tsugono tsu vunu tsu tuwa̱. A̱sa̱ka̱ a yain ili iꞌya va ciga a yain a likimba, tyoku ɗa a ka yansa iꞌya a zuba.
Ne tsu ilikulya i ara.
Vu cimbusuka̱ tsu unyushi u tsunu, tyoku ɗa tsu tsu cimbusuka̱ aza ɗa a tsu nusuka tsu. [
Kotsu vu a̱sa̱ka̱ tsu tsu rukpa̱ a ukondo wa, shegai vu wauwa tsu a asu u uza u cingi vi.’]
