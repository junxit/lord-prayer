Jamamadí

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Teoso Ka Yama Hani (jaa), © 2020 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

'Ota ka abi Teoso tiwinate ama tike neme ya. Me nafi tiwa kahiyarahi.
Tika towisawateba kakeho ota ni ya. Yama tinofa me hiri nahi, bofe ya me winaha mati, neme ka me ihi nima me ihi nabone mati.
Haha yama wehe ka ota tefebone ta tinahabone tike.
Ota ehene hiyara ota ehene sa tisahabone tike. Ota ka one me ihi hiyara me ihi ota sa tosa na otake.
Satanaisi otara totomi rayaho tiwati ihi', te ati nabone teke, te ora na te.

Same as the Traditional form above.
