Kala Lagaw

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Minar Poelayzimayl (mwp), © 2014 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Ngoeymun Thathi gimal dapara,
ngoeymulpa koey ubilnga nginu maygi nel thawmanin,
a kapu za nginu Basalaya danapa yakamiz
a mura mabaygal ayman nginu ubilnga ina apal,
matha kedha ngoedhalnga gimal dapara.
Ngidh ngoeymulpa matha ngoedhagidh poeybar
ngoeymun nubi goeygipa ay.
Ngidh ngoeymulpa ngoeymun ipidhadh gegeyadh gud wayar,
a ngoey lak kedha ngaru war mabaygaw ipidhadh gegeyadh gudwoeyamoeyn,
thana mi thonara nanga ngoeymuniya bal pudhemipa.
Ngidh gar ngoeymun balbayginga yabuya yoelpar,
seyki setha mura nuthayl zapuya lawnga.
Ngidh ngoey gudaz puydhaypa mura kedha ngoedhal wati zoengu.”

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
