=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kaipimi Taita Dius Rimaku (inb), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Nukan­chipa Atun Taita, suma luar­pi­mi kangi.
Kam tukuipi mas alli kagta tukui­kuna ni­sun­chi.
Nukan­chi­wa samui, iaia kan­gapa.
Imasami suma luarpi kam munas­kata ruran­kuna:
chasa­lla­ta kai alpa­pipas rura­sun­chi.
Kuna puncha, tanda minis­tiska nukan­chita kara­mui.
Nukan­chi ima panda­ris­ka­kunata pasin­siai.
Chasa­lla­ta nukan­chipas, sug­kuna ima llaki­chis­ka­kunata pasin­sian­chimi.
Mana sakii, nukan­chi mana alli iuiai­kunata iuia­na­kun­gapa.
Tukui mana alli­kuna­ta­pas nukan­chita kispi­chii.
