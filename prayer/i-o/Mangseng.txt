[Verified from God Ile Riong Kinnga Ponganga nge Mangsengre Okei (mbh, ISO 639-3 mbh), © 1999 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Vovo a God, ong voth a Pen a Urvet,
na iom i riri rintet.
Aro lom menong e nepes aro i patea.
Aro them panes lomum ake ru a ulue,
ranga ve ako areko o voth a Pen a Urvet o panes i.
Aro ong sung emem aning ako i pavurvur nge nang ake.
Aro lomum simikal lemem vothung kerenga,
ranga ve ako lemem simikal areko o oma vothung kerenga nge them.
Ong mothong tova paeso them a tongporumong engenga lemi,
ave aro ong sivlel ur kerengare a them nge.’”
