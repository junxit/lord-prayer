[Verified from Jur Mödö (bex, ISO 639-3 bex), © 1998 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. ꞌBu ze nï ame mïtɔrɔ ne, möyï yï köꞌbö rɔ ma koro.
Iꞌdi ŋere löbu ꞌbï kako, akoꞌdɔkɔ ꞌbï koꞌdɔ rönï mï dɔliŋɔ kɔzɔ ma këdï koꞌdɔ rönï mïtɔrɔ.
Oja gɔ akonyo ꞌba mï kada ma tɔne zize.
Ola ze gɔ lende kirasi ze ga kɔzɔ ma dëdï dola lëpï ze ga ma kirasi lende zize naga tara.
Kinza koto ze dë mï bi ꞌba akiyɔzɔ. Tine olɔ ze di zi bo bɔ ma kënyë nime.’
