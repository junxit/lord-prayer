=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Irinibare Tosorintsi Ocomantëgotëri Antigomi Jesoquirisito (notNT), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Pabati nasiatiegui, ainta Obiro Jonogaguitequë, nocogaigui cara iraniacaninataiguemi omagaro matsiguenga Obironta Natingomisonori.
Nocogaigui pigáantasantaiguena yaca quibatsica; aisati nocogaigui nanganque antagaisati ora picocagaiguina. Aisati nocogaigui inganque omagaro matsiguengajegui ora picocagaiguiri aisati ingantaiguëmari iraegui timaigatsi cara Jonogaguitequë icantaigasegui omagaro ora picocagaiguiri.
Pomapinintaiguena noperi antagaisati cataguiteri.
Aiti nacantomotaguequimi ora te onganinate. Pingábintsasantaiguena, pijocajinaro quero piquengainaro aisa ocanta nojocajineri ora icantaguetomotina matsiguenga ora te onganinate, teni nanguisaneintajiri.
Quero pinintacaguiri camagari cara irantacaguina ora te onganinate. Përosati cara pigáantasantagueti omagaro. Pasintaguitetaro antagaisati. Përosati pitasongantasantagueti Obironta sintsirisonoriguinte. Përosati picobeingaguitetasanti. Aro ocarati, amen”. “Pijocajineri ira basini ora icantomotaguequimi”. (Mr 11.25-26)
