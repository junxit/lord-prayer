=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Subanen, Northern (stb, ISO 639-3 stb), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Gama' nami riin se dlangit,
pegbesaan siya sug bala'an ne ngalan mu,
Mateng'a siya
bu' medlegari'a ri senami,
arun mektuman seni'a su kada sala rini seg benwa,
maa' rema sek pektuman seni'a ritu se dlangit.
Begay mu senami nandaw sung me'aan nami se gendawendaw.
Bu' pasaylu' mu ami ri se ngak sala' nami
maa' rema sek pekpasaylu nami tu se ngak sala' nu ngak sementaun nami.
Bu' ndi' mu tugut neng metintal ami,
ma'ad peneliperay mu ami buwat ri seng Menulay.’
