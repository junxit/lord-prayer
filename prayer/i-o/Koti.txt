Ekoti

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kote New Testament (eko), © 2005-2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Papeetthu oriiye pinkuuni,
littitthimisiwe nzina lawo loottakatthifu,
wiishushe ofwalume awo,
mattakhelo awo arettikhane
vathi apha nkhama etthiipo enrettikhanaazo otulu!
Oninkhe wakhula siikhu etthu-yoojiwa ninihithajiyaaye.
Oniswaamihi koosa zeetthu nkhama etthiipi na-fweyo nininswaamihaaye wakhula otule annikosaaye,
na ohinilikhelele nizayiniwaka,
ila nuusuru khwa ntakhali,
[maana ofwalume, owahi,
na siifwa zooshi pi zawo, mileele. Amiina.]’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
