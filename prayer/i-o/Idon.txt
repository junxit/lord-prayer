Ajiya

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ajigha NT Portion (idc), © 2025 The Seed Company, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the opening quotation mark the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Ate yi ka ayaya, Ta ni ajọọ uca ngọ;
ta ni ẹrẹgọm ngọ ba; ta ni imuwang ngọ na ju ka asing ri kini mani ishi ka ayaya.
Mayi aidi imila mani iyi wang.
Cira uvulu yi kọ, kini mani iyi kpa aghọmani avulu ni yi.
Ma kyaa ni yi ọka mani aki mra yi ọnga shi umọọ, ni kyo yi ọka mani aghọ shi na aghing ababi shi bọ ya ba.’
