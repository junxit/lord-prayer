Kingulu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ndagano mp'ya kwa wanth'u wose (ngp), © 2014 The Word for the World and Pioneer Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tati yetu ywa kwe mbingu.
Zina dyako ditogolwe,
Useuta wako wize,
vinde viya ukuunga weye
aha he isi enga ukwo kwe mbingu.
Ukink'he dielo nk'hande ya mazuwa yose.
Ukiusile wavu wetu kitendile,
enga naswe viya kikuwausila wadya wakukitendela wavu.
Usekukigela mwa magezo,
mna ukikombole kulawa mwo Mwavu.’
Kwaviya Useuta ni wako, no udahi no uwedi utendese, ulo na ulo. Taile.
