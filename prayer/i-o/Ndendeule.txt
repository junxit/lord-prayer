=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ndendeule (dne, ISO 639-3 dne), © 2020 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tate gwito jhumii kunane kwa Chapanga,
Liina lino lilombwe.
Unahota nkolongwa gwino uhike.
Limipala lihengeke pannema ngita kunane.
Mutupele chakulyegha chito cha kila lichiko.
Mutulekekehe uhakaho gwito,
ngita twenga chitwalekekeya bhibhatulemwihe.
Nkotoka kutujhingiya mu majhighanikilo, nambo mutukombole na jhola jwanganandela.
Nhwalo unahota ndi gwino, na likakala na ukolongwa kwa machiko ghoha ghangapeta.
