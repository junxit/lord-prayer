Chimpoto

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Chimpoto (mpa), © 2020 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Atati witu weubhi kunani kumahunde.
Lihina laku lilumbalikaje.
Ukolongwa waku uhikaje.
Pani pundema ngati kunani kumahunde hoku.
Utupekya chilebhi chitu cha kila lichobha.
Utulekakyaje mahakau ghitu,
ngati tepani motubhalekakela bhabhatuhokiye.
Ngawatujeghela mu malengakeku,
nambu utukengalyaye na Muhakau jola.’ Kwa kubha Ukolongwa nde waku, na makili, na ulumbalilu, hata mileli. Amina.
