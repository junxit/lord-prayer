Nahu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Iyo New Testament (nca), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“‘Awanani, ke samboko yote,
keŋo owéŋge surumí yoní,
keŋombo nore wiri yereyote naruke ŋu reka mahiní,
samboko ŋuno iŋo-iŋoke hamó tunoqeyote,
ŋundiro naŋge meté nokono ŋuya hamó tunoqiní.
Itaka naru ŋano o qonanimboro nuno.
Kowe noreŋo utatánani ŋu se roto nuno,
dokoro, utatá noreya taŋgurí ŋu soso se roto yunoyoteto.
Ko nore norika okato kama towo nereweya,
kini, o piyimi horé mu noŋgo nore qembe.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
