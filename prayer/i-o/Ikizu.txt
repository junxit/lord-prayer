Ikikizu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ʉbhʉragɨ Ubhuhya Mʉkɨgambʉ chi Ikikiizʉ (ikz), © 2024 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Bhaabha wɨɨtʉ wʉnʉ uri mwisaarʉ,
iriina ryazʉ rikumibhwɨ.
Ʉbhʉtɨmi bhwazʉ bhuuzɨ.
Ubhwɨndi bhwazʉ bhokorwe mʉʉsɨ hanʉ,
chɨmbu mwisaarʉ iyo.
Otohe reero ibhyakurya bhyɨtʉ bhya kɨrakabhu.
Otobheerere ʉbhʉbhɨ bhwɨtʉ,
chɨmbu na niitwɨ tʉkʉbhabheerera bhanʉ bhatʉsaririiryɨ.
Ʉtatusikirya mubhitiimotiimo,
nawe ʉtʉsabhʉrɨ nʉ ʉmʉbhɨ wurya,
[kʉ kʉbha ʉbhʉtɨmi, na zinguru, nu ubhukumibhwa, ni‑bhyazʉ kemerano.
Garamɨ!]’ ”
