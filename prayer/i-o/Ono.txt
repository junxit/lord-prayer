=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Waom Yesu Wane Sigi Maep Don (ons, ISO 639-3 ons), © 1991 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Ŋenane Magaze ge qeliwo metmaine, geŋane eŋetŋone koboboine payake,
Sari koto baŋem kepe baŋem Waom okanŋonnom, qeliwo geŋane aŋa tewe tewek pamaike, yale waka kepeo ŋenano wakoniake.
Ge ukude ŋaraze ŋebonikene,
Ge borikineze korop qeliŋ warekene, ŋene yaleka matali ŋonmami, ŋei yewa eŋane borikineene qeliŋ waremaine.
Ge liwe liwek togolo misuk biraŋonikene, siluŋ borikino ŋine makok ŋonikene.’
