=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Manö Kamɨŋ (kpw, ISO 639-3 kpw), © 2005 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Bapi kub kumi kabö adö laŋ mɨdpan;
hib ne i adö laŋ mɨdöp.
Ne hanɨp udmön abad mɨdaimön.
Ne kumi kabö adö laŋ hagpan rö nɨŋbal u,
hon aui mɨnöŋ naböŋ iƚ i gɨ ñɨmön nɨŋun hon abe manö nöp nɨŋun.
Hanɨp nan ñɨŋeb rɨmnap nɨme lɨmön;
pör pör ñɨban rö u ñɨn añɨ añɨ ñɨmön.
Nɨbi bɨ rɨmnap hanɨp gɨ naij gɨpal nɨŋun arö gɨpun rö,
hon gɨ naij gɨpun u u rö nöp nɨŋmön arö gɨmön.
Hon gɨ naij gun rö löp u,
ne hanɨp abad mɨdö, hon gɨ naij gagun,’ a gɨmim.
