Ixhil

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Viakʼla txumbʼal u tioxh (ixlCNT), © 2001 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

«KuBꞌaals Amlika, tioxhla bꞌii tatin vabꞌii.
Uloj abꞌan vaQꞌesalail.
Utz bꞌan vatxumbꞌal tu u vatz txꞌavaꞌ tzaꞌ,
echaꞌ vaꞌl naꞌan tu Amlika.
Aqꞌ u qechbꞌubꞌal tetz jun qꞌii.
Utz kuy qꞌu kupaav, echaꞌ oꞌ nikukuy qꞌu aanima qꞌuꞌl kam niꞌan qe.
Yaaqꞌ tzii sakuꞌoꞌ tu qꞌu paav qꞌuꞌl nitok sukuvatz.
Eesaꞌoꞌ vatz u txꞌiꞌliꞌinaj.
Tan eetz u Qꞌesalail,
eetz u ijleꞌm utz,
eetz u techalil bꞌenamen yeꞌk iyaꞌeꞌ. Aꞌi.» Chajex.
