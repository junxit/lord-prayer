Obo Manobo

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Moppiyon Dinoggan, Moka-atag ki Disas Krays (oboNT), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amoy roy riyot datas to langit, muopa nod posivontuhon kos ngaran du.
Muopa no iddos kikow'n kodhari, od inguma rinit ampow't ingod
amoy iddos kikow'n id kopi-i, od kotuman dinit ingod iling taddot diyot datas to langit.
Boggayi koy to koka-an nod ko-ilonganon doy inin allow.
Posinsiya-i koy to mgo saa roy, iling to kodposinsya roy taddot nokosaa konami.
Tovangi koy no dii koy'd kopiyod to kodpuung to saa,
woy id polegguanga koy riyot kororattan.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
