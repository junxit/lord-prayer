Chimakonde

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Lilailano Lyahambi Lya Nnungu Kwa Vanu Vammalele (kde), © 2020 Pioneer Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Atata wetu uvêle kulihunde,
lina lyako litumbyangwe.
Ufalume wako uide.
Na apano pachilambo, latendeke àlalá lulalembela wako, malinga chilatendeka aneko kulihunde.
Utwing'e nelo chakulya chimbetutoleja kwa nelo.
Utusamehe makosa letu,
muchi hwetu chituvasamehe vanu vatukosêla.
Na patulingwa, unatuleke ututenda masambi,
henga utupohe mmakono mwanga Nanchindenga. [Kwa kuva, Ufalume na dimongo na ukulu nni vyako, vyaka na vyaka. Amina.]’
