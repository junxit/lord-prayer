Koma

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Konni (kma), © 2004 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tɩ chɔɔŋ dieke dɩ benne arɩzanna ma:
Vaa aŋ ba bɩrɩ fʋ saaŋ kasɩka;
Vaa aŋ fʋ naarɩ keŋ;
vaa fʋ dʋŋŋʋ yi tɩŋgbaŋka gie me sɩba ka sɩna die arɩzanna ma wa
Yɩ tɩ jinne nyindiike dieke tɩ yaalala.
Nagɩ tɩ hamɩŋ a chaa tɩ,
sɩba tɩ nagɩna vuodiekemba dɩ tʋnna tɩ tʋmbɩatɩ a chaa ba wa.
Da a keŋ vaa tɩ juu magɩsɩ dieke dɩ hagɩrɩna ma,
ama ta vʋarɩ tɩ a nyɩŋ wʋbɩaŋ ma. ((Dama fʋnɩŋ fʋ sɩna naarɩ aŋaŋ yiko aŋaŋ jɩlɩma yaa gamma haahuu. Wusie.))’
