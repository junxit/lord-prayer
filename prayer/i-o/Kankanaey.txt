=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kankanaey Bible (kne, ISO 639-3 kne), © 2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators of Canada, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Ama mi ay wada ed langit, madaydayaw koma din nasantoan ay ngadan mo.
Domteng koma din panturayam isnan daga ta say matongpal din laydem isnan daga ay kaman ed langit.
Iyaam dakami ed wani sin kanen ay kasapolan mi.
Pakawanem din basbasol mi, tan pinakawan mi abe din binmasol en dakami.
Bomadang ka abe ta adi kami masolsolisog mo adi et mailisi kami si lawa.’
