Luqa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Luqa Full Bible (lga), © 2024 The Seed Company and Islands Bible Ministries, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

<Tamamei, ao pa noka,
mi tapangaga na isongomu.
Mu valamea na mua binangara.
Mu vagore votua na mua nyorogua,
isa sa vevei pa noka sa mu veinia pa peso.
Mu vanigei na tetekuna mene toania pa ngeni.
Mu taleosonigei ira na mei sela,
vevei agei qe taleosoniria sira qai roiti lame sela tamigei.
Muke toni laonigei pa totoke,
goto mu vasaregei tana kutana nikerena,> mugu.
