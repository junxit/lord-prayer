Kindengereko

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ndengereko Bible (ndg), © 2020 Door43 World Mission Community, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the opening quotation mark the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Tate bitu waubile kumaunde, lina lyako litukuzwe.
Upwalume wako uiche, champendile kipangilwe hapa pakilambo kati kwo kumaunde.
Utupei lino nkate witu wa kila lisoba.
Utusamee madeni yitu, kati twenga twatuwasamee wadeni witu.
Kana utupei majaribu, lakini utulopwe ni yulu nchela.

Same as the Traditional form above.
