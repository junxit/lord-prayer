Mountain Koiali

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Buka Tumute (kpx, ISO 639-3 kpx), © 1981 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“No Mama, otogoe umale no isiviale ataeau a ivie tumu hoesehavogei.
No isiviale a matamae dogau lohoale kela abu otogoe a isivi vale ke nahate ata bahata vata komo a isivi vai.
Isi nova no isivie lovi ke noevenela.
Isi ata degomoleau noemo vaveve toela vage no ke huhuoholisito momahavoale ke nahate no vaveve toela abuemo vale ke ulihanela.
Ainaho no halevesege no Setene lobomo dobuluvilive.
Isito vaveve toela vamale kemo no malevei halio.
Kosealemo ataeabuna a adae vuvunemo vani bahata a atagamo ui tigei.”
