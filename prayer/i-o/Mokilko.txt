Guerguiko

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mattup ki Taasuwa ere Aware (moz), © 2019 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Meegey wede a kandaane,
kee kʼiso ɗuwo pay an di suune kee Raa kamilen̰ ,
Mozikadá kʼa-di tʼedi.
Munɗa wede addá i dehu kʼaase a siiɗo ette, ki yʼiso aakede ki dʼisiyo a kandaane.
Aŋki kee kʼay eeli omɓadey ere ay di tʼîde.
Kʼay tʼisi tambobino a munɗa wede ulsu ay ize a kee,
aakede kaye toŋ ay an tʼize tambobino a kane ɗoŋ ay ize munɗa ulsu kay.
Kʼaynʼooli adda naamiya daggiya i ceera eedey ay tʼetta adda kʼolɗiko ye,
wo ɗe kʼay tiʼn aɗɗi ti bey Mohita kʼolɗiko .
[Geŋ moziko, toogo wo tamma, kane pay ki kee ki daayum. Eyye.]
