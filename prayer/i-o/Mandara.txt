=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mi Namani Tino (tbf, ISO 639-3 tbf), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“‘Tamagei die i kur simi rangit,
a kalei va gei ta ngas mangaran mi asa.
A kalei va ke vinuor ta ngas vuortie gei.
A kalei va gei ta tor sevoi ine simi mangmagoso enaenamon
malan gi ta tor sevoi die i kur simi rangit.
Tabarie gei ngan mi nginonginou ta oit simi ra vere.
Vodon rivie kegei sar sinavei tsokor
malan egei gei ta vodon rivie bu sinavei tsokor gi ta tsana segei.
Ma kian va e ta tsupuk rivie gei sibu konokonon,
eiekesen, vatoa gei i riman mi Tsaka Ka.
[Mi mangmagoso Nguts, ma mi ngeisngeis, ma mi matvinavaso ta mon sevoi mo sibu ra bu ra. Amen.]’
