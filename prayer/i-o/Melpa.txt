=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Got Nga Nambuha Ik Kai (med, ISO 639-3 med), © 1995 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Tʉn nga muⱡ kona Wuta o! Nim nga mbi ei, tʉp wingti rʉndamin!
Nim nga wamp tʉpa mumuk rui uⱡ ei wangga! Muⱡ kona ila nim nga man ik pʉtmʉn ei mel, ya mʉi kona mbila i ku itamin!
Akup nga nuimin rʉng mbʉ nguiyo!
Tʉn nga uⱡ kit imʉn mbʉ wak rundi! Tʉn ndi, i ku etpʉn, tʉn kʉn uⱡ kit eng wamp mbʉ nga uⱡ kit mbʉ wak rundmin.
Tʉn uⱡ kit mbila oⱡa tʉkʉn kʉni nʉkʉnʉi! Wote tʉn wu kit ei nga kan ila tinga ndukʉn tiyo!’
