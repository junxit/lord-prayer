ꯃꯩꯇꯩꯂꯣꯟ (Meiteilon / Manipuri)

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from the Meitei New Testament, "Anouba Warepnaba" (Roman Script edition, "RSM", The Word for the World, 2022), Matthew 6:9-13. The Manipuri/Meitei Bible is published in the Bengali script, Roman script, and Meitei Mayek; only the Roman-script edition was available for verbatim quotation, so the authentic Roman-orthography text is given. Endonym shown above in Meitei Mayek.]

Swargada leiba eikhoigi Ipa,
Ibungo Nahakki ming asengba oisanu.
Nahakki leibak laksanu;
swargada nahakna ningba toubagumna taibangpanda oisanu.
Numit asigi nongmagi chara eikhoida pinabiyu.
Aduga eikhoida laman tonbasingbu eikhoina kokpibagumna, eikhoigi laman kokpiyu.
Aduga eikhoibu athembada tahanbiganu, adubu phattaba adudagi eikhoibu kanbiyu.

Maramdi ningthou leibak, panggal amasung matik mangal lomba naidana nahakkini. Amen.

Same as the Traditional form above.
