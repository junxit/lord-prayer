Muìnánɨ

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Moocaani iijɨ (bmrNT), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. Moocaani niquejefaño idiicaabo, jaafa dimomo imino ijeevadɨ ficucafetehi.
Jaafa dihaivojɨ saahi. Jaafa dijeebɨɨ onoobadɨ eetavocafetehi jiinɨjehallɨvɨ, oono niquejefaño iicabadɨ.
Mɨɨhaico daacɨ machutaatɨ fafucuvaacɨvɨro.
Jaaboro mɨɨhaico mohimitɨco buusu, oono bu mɨɨhai, mɨɨhaico imitɨno omoonɨmoco toonoco mihimibachubadɨ.
Jaaboro mɨɨhaico ɨnɨsunofaño gañɨɨsudino. Mɨɨhaico imitɨ ɨjɨ usetɨ taajasu. Fɨne.
