Nyang

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ɛkáti Nku Nkɔ (kenNT), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ɛtayɛsɛ, wɔ mmu ɔ́chí amfay:
Kʉ bo mánchyɛ́ nnyɛ́n ɛna kɛnókó;
kʉ bɛ́ mandɔ wɔ ɔmbak Mfɔ wap;
nɛ bɛ mánkʉ́ nɔ́kɔ́ chí mɛnyɨŋ ɛbhɛn ɔ́yaŋ bo mankʉ fá amɨk,
mbɔ ɛnyǔ ɛchi awu amfay.
Chiɛ́ bhɛsɛ nɛnyíɛ́ ɛnɛn ɛchɔŋ;
Ɔ́mfoŋori bɛbʉ́ bhɛsɛ,
mbɔ ɛnyu sɛ́fóŋórí bo abhɛn bákʉ́ bhɛsɛ bɛbʉ́.
Nɛ ɔ́kɛ́ ka sɛ́nchwe ndǔ nɛmɔ ɛnɛn nɛ́kʉ sɛ́nkwɛ́n ndǔ bɛbʉ́,
kɛ fɛrɛ́ bhɛsɛ ndu amɔ mbʉ́mbʉ.’
