Numu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Te Naa Besa Unnepu (paoNT), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the opening quotation mark the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

U, Ne Naa ne pa'anikwatu u tubetse besa'yoo.
U no'oko numme-kooba poohakatudu. Numme no'oko numu yaa teepu-koobatu ume mu nakabetseana soogwa'e, ka u-baatu-kwa'nesoo.
Nonotsa tu tabemea-kwi besa ne sootuhi.
Ka ne tuma'emukwana soomu'wana, ka mu numu suda numme matugu numme puu oo soomu'wa.
Ne matzino, kaaheno ne mayugwe. O'no numme besa-kwitoo tsebooe petugakwu.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
