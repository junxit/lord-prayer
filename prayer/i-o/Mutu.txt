Saveeng

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Maaron Saveeŋ Toni Patabuaŋ (tuc-o), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamamai to uneep sambam,
ugham tamtoghon tipatab pa yom izam.
Pooz tiom inim irau ndug le isob.
Ugham tamtoghon titaghon lolom izi taan, inimale titaghoni izi sambam.
Ugham aniiŋ to aazne payei.
Ureu sosor tiei, inimale yei nipul sosor to tamtoghon tigham payei.
Upul tovaaŋ ivot payei malep.
Uvool tiei, ve upoon ghei pa mbeb samsamia tisob.’
