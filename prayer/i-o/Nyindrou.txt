=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Sahou ta Kindrei (lid, ISO 639-3 lid), © 2001 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tama aso, ou ado kandrah.
Nyaram badeh, ki ndrabun.
Ma ou ame atondrok kon mari.
Ahi kamesekei lahou na so-ok tam udu munyak,
i le buku ndramesekei udu kandrah.
Ou anyohan aso na nyamon, winiyan ta len dah.
Ou mani kandriyen ndra-in taso,
i le buku aso mani iri kandriyen ndra-in iri amekes eme taso.
Ma ou satehei aso sala bolo iri none.
Arile, ou sebenei aso ta sala bolo kandriyen ndra-in ta balik.
