Koronadal Blaan

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from I Falami Kasafnè (New Testament) (bpr, ISO 639-3 bpr), © 1995 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Màmi di tah i langit, kayèmi moon ku mangamfù di ge i kdee.
Na kayèmi ku ge magot i kdee, na ku gdohò i kdee knayèam dini di tah tanà salngad i kagdohòan déé di tah langit.
Blém di gami i gablà knaanmi di mdu ani.
Fnasinsyam i dad salàmi, salngad i kfasinsyami salà i dad to gamsalà di gami.
Nangam faloh di gami i dad tilew gamgebe gami di salà, bay kanlungam gami di ku Satanas, gumdà i kdee sasè.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
