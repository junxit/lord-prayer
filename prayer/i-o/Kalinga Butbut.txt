=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kalinga, Butbut (kyb, ISO 639-3 kyb), © 2019 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amami gway agwad ad langit
machaychajaw nan ngachannu.
Umali nan Mantuyajam.
Ma'gwa nan whayugwom ad asna luta
as isunad langit.
Atchan cha'ami as anonmi san sana erkaw.
Ad ligwatam chanan whersumi
as isun nan malligwatanmi sasan whersun chanan na'awhersu an cha'ami.
Achi cha'ami ituyung san masulisukanmi jon
iligtas cha'ami san lagwingay o'oon Satanas.’
