Kimatuumbi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Injili ya Yesu (mgw), © 2019 Pioneer Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tati' bitu ba kunani,
Lina linu likwi'yi'lwe.
Ukulungwa winu wise,
Lyamupala lipangike, padunia kati kunani.
Mutupei li'no kilyo situ sa kila lisu'ba.
Mutusami makosa gitu,
kati twenga mwatwaasamya babatukosile.
Kanemutuyingiye mumapayilwo,
lakini mutukosopoli na yu'lu' nnau, Ibilisi.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
