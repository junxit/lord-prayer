=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tayta Diospa Guepacag Testamentun (qvnNT, ISO 639-3 qvn), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Jana pachachru cayag Tayta Diosnïcuna,
gamga lapan juchapita caruchrümi cayanqui.
Reguechicuynar runacunawan munayniyog cangayta. Shamuynar mandag canaypag.
Chaynuylar mandarcayämay imaysi.
Gam munangaytachu jana pachachrüsi rurarcayan chaynuy cay pachachrüsi rurapäcushag.
Canan junagpag micunäcunata gopäcamay.
Jucha rurapäcungäta perdonayärimay,
lutan ruramagnïcunata nogacunas perdonapäcungänuy.
Juchäcachiyta Asyag munapäcamaptin amar cachraripäcamaychu.
Antis washayärimay lutan ruraycunapita’ nirmi manacurcanqui.
