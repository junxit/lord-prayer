Kyaka Eŋa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from BUKU BAEPOLE (kyc), © 1973, 1980, 1987 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Namwana Rakane yake panda karene doko,
embena kingi doko poo pyasi ingyuo palenale.
Embena isingi yulu doko epo palenale.
Yake panda dokona lapinyili pyuo karaminyi
doko suu pyao isa yuu dakena pyuo karamanale.
Yuu peparae namwa nenge napengele doko joo karape.
Namwa kando koo pyakaenge dupwana koo dupwa
kame sakamilyamano dopako suu pyao
namwana koo dupwa nyuo kame sakao karape.
Namwa makande pira ingilyamo dokona
embame namwa lanyuo paa napala
koo dokome maralyasara lao kyawa minyuo karape. Doko kinyi. Pii dopale lao ateŋa lalapape.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
