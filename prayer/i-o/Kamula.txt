Wawoi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ka꞉dle Yu (xla), © 2005 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Di A Pepele di dla hallalo,
hatlo wa꞉ hita alila hapuloma dulu dla aiyalma depo hallepe.
Epame di solotama, wa꞉ mo kokota pulupo depame.
Epame Pepele dimo dla wa꞉ta solotamale koko halomha꞉ha꞉ epapele kokoye
ma ta꞉lamo sota halomha꞉ha꞉lepe.
Epame alila hapuloma dulu dla dita dlowe alisisiye halopimhalane.
Epame di bobtalimaleye hihiyotipo woweline.
Adipalati, di mape diamkadulota dia꞉ dla batalimale koko halomde hapulomaye wowelimdepo.
Epame dia꞉ye elemala natili kokalle dla elemtaiya demiya.
Amasiyama dia꞉ye Saitenta itiyamale tle alikili dla eleme woyamane.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
