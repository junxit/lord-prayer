Chimatengo

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Amatengu (mgv), © 2020 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Atati bitu mombii kunani kwaka Sapanga kwaka Sapanga,
liina linu lilumbalika ngani.
Ubambu ukolongu waku guhika,
gagupala gahengika pundema ngati kunani kwaka Sapanga kwaka Sapanga.
Gutupekiya lelenu posu jitu ja kila lisoba,
Gutulekakiya mahakau gitu,
ngati twepani etwalekake babutukosi.
Watujingisa mu milengu,
lakini utuhokua kuhuma kuku Mbaja jola.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
