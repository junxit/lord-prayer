=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nongan Hiyan (nss, ISO 639-3 nss), © 2008, 2012, 2020, 2025 PNG Bible Translation Association, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tama yowu ayera kol paingan yang,
Ngalam ara ita haiyan.
Yowu tohun tehe kultuwayi kingtom tam mepo ngara aro mu kiya aliy ara kisa yelan,
pe namiliwam pwokeyan kirayah mannan kiro kol pwan,
kiya harong o tehe ita kol paingan yang.
Lang nepo ahang yowu anandrinai, anowu para lang sih tehe sih.
Pe atali hu pakut mwomwan towu,
tehe yowu ngara katali hu pakut mwomwan
tahu ndramat mepo
hu ngara kambusi pakut mwomwan kisa kili yowu.
Pe mbuna aluwani yowu kaya nondriya nohonou,
hapeko asopwat yowu kasa mawen kili Sinai.
Paratesah, kingtom mepo ngara aro mu kiya aliy, pe pwoke namandran, iyapolo nonowa nganam,
ara masih atam, pe andre kinto-kinto. Ndrokonan.
