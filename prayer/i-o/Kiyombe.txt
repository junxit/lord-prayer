=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Biblica® Open Nkanda wu Moyo, Nguizani Yimona ayi Minkunga 2002 (yom, ISO 639-3 yom), © 2002 Biblica, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tatꞌeto widi ku Diyilu,
bika dizina diaku diba dinlongo
Bika Kipfumu kiaku kiza.
Bika luzolo luaku luvangama va ntoto,
banga bu lueti vangimina ku Diyilu.
Wutuvana buabu bidia bieto bi kadika lumbu.
Wutulemvukila mambimbi meto ;
banga beto bu tunlemvukilanga batu
bobo batuvangila mambimbi.
Kadi kutu bika tubua mu phukumunu,
vayi wutukula mu mvukumuni,
[Bila ngeyo vuidi kipfumu, lulendo ayi nkembo
mu mimvu mi kamimani.]
