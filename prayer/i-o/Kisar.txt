Yotowawa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Makromod lalap lirna wawan (kjeNT), © 2008 Yayasan Sumber Sejahtera, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ainim Pape man minle a'am raram,
Na'amoli O naran man moumou dewdewe, ri na'akeme rahiyene arore.
Na'amoli mamwali Rai ma nodi molollo ri noho wawan na'akeme,
la malala molollo mayai leke am derne makani Onum panaeku na'akeme lolo noho wawan naise man derne nakani O lolo a'am raram.
Lere eniyeni mala hanana'an man kukkuku noknokor mayai,
la malala ampun ainima sale naise ai mala ampun ri man hi'i sala mayai.
Ewekala kawala mayai leke yono am lernala kenene panatal,
la morle'ule'u morwaliwali yai leke yono hayakyaka makromon nodi molollo mayai,
[ono O penia Rai man siksik lerlere, la ma nodi molollo laa ewi-ewi.’]
