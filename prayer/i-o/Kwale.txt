Kwale

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Buka Zaḡoḡa Ḡoitore Iziga (ksj), © 2017-2024 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

'Emeḡe Mama adureo,
eme umiꞌeꞌohe, ḡa niḡoꞌe zaḡoḡa reꞌuma,
ḡaeꞌa ḡau niduꞌa zuhiꞌaziꞌuma,
zu adureo ḡae uraꞌe hudauꞌeteꞌe teige, raha ꞌadao zuni hudaꞌuma.
Izidi emeḡeꞌa atiteꞌe ogomuḡa rifiꞌenoꞌi,
ebu emeꞌa emeḡe mazao sauꞌa haḡairoteꞌe azaḡa haḡaize sauꞌa rae modiroteꞌe teige, emeḡe haḡai sauꞌa rae modoꞌi.
Ebu eme haḡai sauꞌa haḡaihi raꞌeteꞌe zamaroneḡanonoꞌo tutufe vaḡifoꞌi,
ebu Satani mazaonoꞌo mae ḡabofoꞌi.'
