=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Aadi keyri: linjiila iisaa almasiihu (ffm), © 2005 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Baabiraaɗo amen gonɗo dow kammu,
yo innde maa teddine.
Yo Laamu maa war.
Yo muuyɗe maa ngaɗe dow leydi no ngaɗiraa dow kammu nii.
Hokku min hannde ɲaamdu heƴooru min.
Yaafa min luutti amen,
hono no min njaaforii tooɲuɓe min nii.
Pati accu min ceytee,
hisin min e Bonɗo oo.
[Sabi laamu e baawɗe e teddeengal ana ngoodan maa faa abada. Aamiina.]
