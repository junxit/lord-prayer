Iny rybè

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Deuxu Rybe Tyyriti (kpjNT), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Aiòlòna heka wymy kanake. Iny adeewile riwinykemy iwese biu‑ki relemyhỹre wese.
Wadeereny txuõ txuõ rỹsỹna bidiwahinyke.
Waòrarunareny õhõti‑ribi bitosadỹỹnyke. Iwese jiarỹ inyõ òraruna wanohõti iribi aritosadỹỹnymyhỹre wese.
Isiratyhy urihixina‑ribi heka biwayrubunyke, òraruna‑txi reseke lau. Aõna aõna bina‑ò bidiwaijebenyke. Iòlòna, ruru, loosòna heka ahõrõhyy rare. Kie.
