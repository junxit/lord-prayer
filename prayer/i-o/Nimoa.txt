Saisai

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Metiu ge Efeso (nmws), © 2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators, inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tamamai he bulubulu,
hidam i gabub gen hamai ha hawatauyan,
lam bagalogugui gen i luwom,
lam nuwotu hamai gen ha ginawi he panayawi
sohode sidiye si ginawi he bulubulu.
Yan ya kaiwane nahamai gen u giyau heyai.
U nuwoyok lama gegi,
sohode hedi hamai ha nuwoyok hene-bwaimai wedi gegi heyai.
Tab u ehoteiyai he hamnohon,
agegene gen u pwamwadai he gegi.
Kaiwane huwo tologugui, huwo togaisi ge huwo towasewase,
sauge hohoyowate taine i edemwao. Bam. Sohode.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
