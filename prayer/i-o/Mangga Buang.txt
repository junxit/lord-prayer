Mangga Buang

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mangga Buang New Testament (mmo, ISO 639-3 mmo), © 1981 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer largely as running prose, and the eBible USFM leaves cross-references unmarked (e.g. "+ o 6:10 Mat 7:21; 26:39; Luk 22:42*"); those unmarked cross-references and the narrative introduction to verse 9 ("Om ham anohak naambêênjo naambe,") have been removed here, and the verses are set one per line.]

‘Aamangg, hong sen onando baandoni, hil lambu vaambuung in ohaalem.
Ganaam naatu heyate Omaaho Bop-ambe ganohvu taaku pin, de hil pin lambiing nohvu gakam nanjip voon naambe sen angela dawiing vu baandonii-to.
Gambu vanôôn in dôôvu hey peey-anjo,
de okawiiy heyate va nipaya na nohvu hey oo navu kawiiy hil-ate nyevahaan sen dawiing vu hey-ato nala.
Game naanggiiy hey ana ngaaho vu palapin e, nganjo ganôôh hey vêêl in omaaho nipaya. [In goo timu nanggin va pin, tonde niwêêk osin nikaapulis pin neep vu hong-ambe le nanjip danggata. Keen.]’
