Mangga Buang

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mangga Buang New Testament (mmo, ISO 639-3 mmo), © 1981 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

9 Jon 17:6*Om ham anohak naambêênjo naambe, ‘Aamangg, hong sen onando baandoni, hil lambu vaambuung in ohaalem.
+ o 6:10 Mat 7:21; 26:39; Luk 22:42*Ganaam naatu heyate Omaaho Bop-ambe ganohvu taaku pin, de hil pin lambiing nohvu gakam nanjip voon naambe sen angela dawiing vu baandonii-to.
Gambu vanôôn in dôôvu hey peey-anjo,
+ o 6:12 Mat 18:21-35*de okawiiy heyate va nipaya na nohvu hey oo navu kawiiy hil-ate nyevahaan sen dawiing vu hey-ato nala.
+ o 6:13 Mat 26:41; Luk 22:40; Jem 1:13; Jon 17:15; 2Tes 3:3; 2Ti 4:18; 1Kro 29:11-13*Game naanggiiy hey ana ngaaho vu palapin e, nganjo ganôôh hey vêêl in omaaho nipaya. [In goo timu nanggin va pin, tonde niwêêk osin nikaapulis pin neep vu hong-ambe le nanjip danggata. Keen.]’
