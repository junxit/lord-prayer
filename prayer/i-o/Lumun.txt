Kuku-Lumun

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Lumun (lmd), © 2020 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ngappa pïn ipaik tothiꞌrot,
akꞌran kang othiakineik,
angili ngang anthan,
alon ila ngkwongothe itti lakkattat,
alokkattat nocapu ammakka lokkattathe tothiꞌrot.
Ethet nïn thuꞌrit ithekko nanïn inenni.
Occïkïnthet nïn lon lïn ilokithak
akka onïn cakuruk thaccïkanthet okkwion ithokkinthent nïn lon ilokithak.
Kirrnni apiet nïn ithenekketta
anaruk oꞌrethenïn nti ilon lopul pothopulut.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
