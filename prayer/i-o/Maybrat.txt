=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Watum ro Tuhan Bosi ro Tna (ayz), © 2005 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Nyõ fi amu Nja ramu ro nhau mam sawro aNyõ. Nno re kbe raa mari tibyo bibi Nyõ nasom aNyõ.
Nasen namo Raja tna nama nbo raa woNyõ. Bo ro Nyõ ksoh to, ja raa mbo mno mam sawro aNyõ oh. Nno re raa ro mhau mam tabam refo mbo mno bo ro Nyõ ksoh mfi feto ye.
Ifo to, amu nyoh boit mbam kʼNyõ natem aNyõ re boit wamu to mabut fee.
Nyon iro wamu sor. Nyon iro wamu mnan fi amu nesait ne nhaf moof nsya raa anya ro mno bo ro mkair mai kʼamu ye.
Tna nakah amu namo mam wore wo bosanet aro besruf amu to ma. Nama nitsnok amu frok nbam wore wo mkair-kar. [Nyõ Allah ro nase. Nyõ natak nbo ayoh msya tabam refo. Kbe raa bibi Nyõ maban mfo refo mamo snok mam hame wajin ye. Amin : Mabo oh.]
