Matigsalug

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Meupiya ne panugtulen: Bibliya ne Matigsalug (mbtNT), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amey ney ne diye te langit,
egdayanen ka matulus ne ngaran nu.
Eghari ka kayi te kanami,
Egkatuman ka igkeupii nu kayi te ampew te tane iling degma te katumanan diye te langit.
Ibehey nu kanami ka egkeilanganen ney kuntee ne egkeenen
wey pasayluwa key te me sale ney,
iling degma te pegpasaylu ney te nakasale kanami.
Ne kene key ipabaye nikeykew diye te pegtintal,
ke kene, luwasa key nikeykew puun ki Meibulan.’
