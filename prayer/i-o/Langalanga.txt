Langalanga

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Alafuuna Fa'alu Ala Saena 'I Wala (lgl), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Mama 'ami 'i nali,
'Ami oga ioli gi sui geraka fabaelaa ratamu, sulia 'e abu rasua.
Dao mae 'alia 'ilitoana 'o fafia maurinala ioli gi sui.
Ma liomu 'ami ka tau sulia 'i wado malaa lou 'i nali.
'O kwatea mae famami fana 'e totolia fe atoa 'e li.
'O kwailufa 'alia ta'ana 'amami gi, malaa lou 'ami kwailufa 'alia ta'ana ioli 'e fulida amaami gi.
'O ala lugatae na mami fala ilitoona gi fala liufi namami.
Ma ko lau 'ali 'ami faasia Saetan.’
