Cimwera

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Malagano ga Ambi (mwe), © 2020 Pioneer Bible Translators and The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Atati nni kunnungu,
Lina lyenu likuywe.
Upalume gwenu guishe.
Linkupinga mmwe litendeshe pa shilambolyo malinga shiikutendeka kunnunguko.
Ntupe lelo shalya sha togolela lelo.
Ntuleshelele itulebhile,
malinga na uwe shitukwaaleshelelanga bhatulebhilenje.
Nnatubhishe mmalinjilo,
ikabheje ntuembuye naka Lishetani jula. Pabha upalume ni gwenu na mashili na ukonjelo pitipiti. Amina.’
