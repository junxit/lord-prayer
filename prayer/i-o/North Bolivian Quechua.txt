Apulu qhichwa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mosoj Testamento (qulNT), © 1985 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Janaj pachapi cachcaj Tataycu, sutiyqui
yupaychaska cachun.
Gobiernoyqui jamuchun; munayniyqui ruwaska
cachun, imaynatachus janaj pachapi,
ajinallatataj cay pachapipas.
Sapa p'unchaypaj t'antaycuta cunan kowaycu.
Juchaycuta perdonawaycu,
imaynatachus nokaycu contra
juchallicojtapas perdonaycu jinata.
Ama tentacionman urmachiwaycuchu,
astawanpas tucuy mana allincunamanta librawaycu.
Kanpajta gobiernoyqui, tucuy atiyniyqui,
tucuy gloriayquipas wiñaypaj wiñayninpaj.
Ajina cachun.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
