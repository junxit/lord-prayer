=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Hutngin kamkabat si Káláu (sgz, ISO 639-3 sgz), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Kák gim imi bát,
gim nem ngo matananu no da para aposoi ngo ngisam a pilpil má dák rumrum on.
Lolsit kaiam imi bát,
gim sung ngo na sangar i hut main i naul bim.
Kam nemnem a ian támin ami bát,
má gim sung ngo na ian támin mul main i naul bim.
Una tabar gim mai ángim balbal i bung onin.
Má una hol palai kángim tan sápkin tatalen
ngorer gim lu hol palai tan sápkin di lu longoi uri narsá gim.
Má una áir kalar gim gima káp pur i pákánbung án artohtoh,
ái sár una sáras pas gim tili limán ái Satan.
