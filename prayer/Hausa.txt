Harshen Hausa

=== Traditional ===
Ya Ubanmu, wanda yake cikin Sama,
A tsarkake sunanka.
Mulkinka yă zo.
A aikata nufinka a duniya kamar yadda ake yi a Sama.
Ka ba mu abincinmu na yau.
Ka gafarta mana laifofinmu,
kamar yadda mu ma muke gafarta wa waɗanda suke yi mana laifi.
Kada ka kai mu wurin jaraba,
amma ka cece mu daga Mugun.

Gama mulki, da iko, da ɗaukaka, naka ne, har abada abadin. Amin.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Ya Ubanmu, wanda yake cikin Sama, a tsarkake sunanka.
Mulkinka yă zo, a aikata nufinka a duniya kamar yadda ake yi a Sama.
Ka ba mu yau abincinmu na yau da kullum;
ka gafarta mana laifofinmu
kamar yadda muke gafarta wa waɗanda suke yi mana laifi;
kada ka kai mu wurin jaraba,
amma ka cece mu daga Mugun.

Gama mulki, da iko, da ɗaukaka, naka ne, har abada abadin. Amin.
