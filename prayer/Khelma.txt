Sakechep

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Pathien Lekhabu Inthieng Chongtung Thar (sch), © 2017 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Kan Pa invana mi:
Nirming inthieng mirit om rese.
Na Rêngram juong tung rese;
nu lungdo invâna mîn an sin angin nuoia mîn sin rese ngei.
Anîngtina kin nâng ngâi dôr sâk avien hin mi pêk roh.
Kin chunga sin minchâingei kin ngâidam anghan,
nang khomin kin minchâinangei mi ngâidam roh.
Sietna ranga methêmna tieng mi tuong nônla,
Asaloina renga chu mi mojôk roh,’ tiin.
