=== Traditional ===
[Verified from YESUS KAMO WAKAI (kue, ISO 639-3 kue), © 2008 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“‘No Neno ene heven suna
motnga, ene kangin holi panambiwo.
Ene kingdomnen unano.
No ene pitnere mere
makandle yegl keunde
eramniwo, i heven suna erimere yegl.
No kaiya mokna enge
yomere yomere ninamga i noratniwo.
No tandaglmeno kinde
kondo noro, no yomba
tau ye kinde ere norikwa
i kindekondo tenamga.
Ene no auro i baglnu
mina pikro. Taragl kinde
mina ene no i-eglke endo.’
