Washkuk

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from GOD RIITI MAJI KEPI (kmo), © 1974 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Noti Apoko mii hevenek tawa. Miiti hi sa kepi wey ba ta.
Miiti siitii kowu tawa kigdom sii sa ya. Miiti inyakan hevenk sumowutawaga nota nosapek tawa ma na eechaba sumowuba ta.
Nota yadii pochi dopo tawa aboboyen miita nona na ha ta.
Ma ye nona kapasek otii tawa boboyen nota kwoyavayatawaga, miita na noti kapasek boboyen eechaba na kwoyava yeechi ta.
Mii opoche nona otiihechawa boboyen heechanak, ya nona otii hechanakech. Miita nona na Gaba riina na ya.’ Miiti kigdom, miiti hadabas boboy miiti hapaga boboy miitabaka eechaba eechaba takiita. (Omutiik)

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
