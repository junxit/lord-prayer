=== Traditional ===
[Verified from 'Abi'uai Apa'uana Mahamahana (rro, ISO 639-3 rro), © 2002 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Hamamai e, kupai nomiaho.
Oi atamu hanona katabarobena.
Emu obia aiarana kemai,
ikoimaiai emu nuatae
katababaina hanopakai,
ihobona kupai tibabaina 'abana.
Bariu rani neianai aniani
ṯanuatae aina hauna kobenamai.
Emai ki'a tababai haukia
mohina hao,
ihobona weiakia heremaiai
tebabai ki'a haukia ekia ki'a
tahina hao 'abana.
A'i koaoainamai baihobo 'ekakiai,
ia tiaporo herenai kobamaurinamai.
(Kupa aiarana oi 'eumu, Hiabu oi 'eumu, Nuabi oi 'eumu,
keaomo banai banai. Amen.)’
