=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nan Kalin Apu Dios (ifk, ISO 639-3 ifk), © 2004 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Ama min wadah langit, hana ot ta he-ay midayaw
ya he-ay mun-ap-apu. Hana ot ta hay pinhod muy maat tuh luta, umat hi langit.
Hana ot ta idatan dakamih itanud mih kaalgoalgo.
Ya kal-iwam di liwat mi tedeyan kal-iwan mi damdamay numbahulan di ibba min dakami.
Ya baddangan dakami ta adi kami maliwat. Ya hana ot ta baddangan dakamit adi dakami haulon ke Satanas.’”
