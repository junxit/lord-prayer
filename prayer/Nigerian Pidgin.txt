Naijá

=== Traditional ===
Awa Papa wey dey for heaven,
wi praiz yor name,
make yor Kingdom kom,
make wetin yu wont happen for dis world as e bi for heaven.
Give us di food wey wi go chop evriday,
forgive us all di bad-bad tins wey wi don do,
as wisef dey forgive odas di bad tins wey dem do us.
Nor allow us enter temptashon,
but make yu save us from bad tins.

Na yor own na di Kingdom, di pawa, an di glory, naw an forever. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
