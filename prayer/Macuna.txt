Masa ye

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dios Oca Tuti (myy), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Gʉa Jacʉ, ũmacʉ̃jʉ gagʉ ña mʉ. Mʉ wame masicõri masa ñajediro rʉ̃cʉbʉojaro ĩna.
Masa jeyaro Ʉjʉ mʉ ñaroti rʉmʉ ejare ãmoa gʉa. Ũmacʉ̃jʉga tuti cʉni mʉ ãmoro bajiroti ñayija. Ito bajiri adi tutijʉ cʉni mʉ ãmoro bajiro riti ñare ãmoa gʉa.
Co rʉmʉ rʉyabeto gʉa baroticõti gʉare naju ĩsiña.
Gʉa ñeñaro yirisere ãcabojoya. Gʉa cʉni ito bajiroti gʉare ñeñaro yirãre ãcabojoa.
¿No bajiro yʉre cʉdirã yirãjida ĩna? yi tʉoĩabesa gʉare. Gʉa ñeñaro yibititoni gʉare ejabʉaya. Mʉ ña ʉjʉ ñatĩñarocʉ. Mʉ ña bʉto masitĩñare gaye rʉcotĩñarocʉ. Mʉ ña masa jeyaro ĩna rʉ̃cʉbʉotĩñarocʉ. Itocõ ña. Amén. Ito bajiro Diore mʉa bʉsija quena, yiyijʉ Jesús ĩ rãca riasotirãre.
