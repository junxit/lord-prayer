Xicotepec Totonac

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Huan Xasāstiʼ testamento: Huan xatzey tachihuīn ixpālacata Jesucristo (tooNT, ISO 639-3 too), © 1978 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

QuinTāta'ca'n a'ntī hui'lachi' nac a'kapūn, ca'a'cnīni'ni'ca'.
Camilh a'xni'ca' natzucuya' nacāmāpa'ksī'kō'ya' huan tachi'xcuhuī't.
Catlahuaca mintapuhuān huā'tzā' nac ti'ya't chu a'cxtim hua'chi nac a'kapūn.
Caquilāmaxquī'uj chuhua'j quilīhua'tca'n a'ntū icmaclacasqui'nāuj.
Caquilāmāsputūnu'ni'uj a'ntū iccālaclē'ni'yāni' como iccāmāsputūnu'ni'yāuj huan tamā'na' a'ntī quincātalaclē'ni'yāni'.
Tū' camakxtekti naquincāpātle'keni'yāni' a'ntū naquincāmātlahuīyāni' talaclē'i'.
Xmān hui'x xaMāpa'ksīni' lā' mila' ixlīhuāk huan līmāpa'ksīn lā' ixlīhuākca'n natamācā'tanīyāni' para pō'ktu.
Amén.
