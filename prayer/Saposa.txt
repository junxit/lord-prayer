Fa Saposa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from U VURUNGAN ROF FOUN TEN GOV NANE JISAS KRAIS (sps), © 2001 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamamamam te kaa rom Gormirmir, Asanga Manyi na taabos;
Waam Manyi ma naa me ma kat arin mangiir Tsumanyi nei petoo faarei te kat finy rori ya naa Gormirmir.
Faan mamimam a kainy ainy te onot maromam roman.
Nyi te anofe ravainy men kat iring te kat amam, faarei mam te anofe ravainy kat iring te kat a mesapan tsumam.
Fatamee vaare mamimam tan fiamus, Nyi te bei ot mamimam tana Vinasaar.
A Waam Manyi, na reits, an siinaiv kaa na suu. Man ovei.’
