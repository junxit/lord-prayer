[Verified from Golin New Testament (gvf, ISO 639-3 gvf), © 1980 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Na Abe kamin mina milinga, i kaanin wi yebe namua.
I kenin ere ke milangwa kaun unama di pirebinwa.
Kamin mina giranin wiina pay tongwa mere gariba gul iru erama di pirebinwa.
Kemina komina kuunin biinangwa mere na to.
Ibal kobe kal digan ere na tongure pirin paangwa gule ere tobinga mere, kal digan ere taalime erebingire pirin paangwa, i pirin gule ere na to.
Kunibe kura taalime terabina di erebinga, i na awli suna pekio.
Kal ki erungwa yal na sirala di erungwa kuman kine na to.’”
