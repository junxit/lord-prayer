Kiluguru

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Laghano Lya Sambi Kwe Iwanu Wose (ruf), © 2014 Pioneer Bible Translators / TWFTW, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Aba wetu yuli kulanga,
Taghwa lyako litunyighwe,
Undewa wako utse
ghaubama ghatendeke mne isi ka kulanga.
Utwing'e leloli ichijo chetu cha chila litsuwa,
Utuleghusile ghamatotso ghetu,
ka twetwe pfatuwaleghusila awala iwatutendile ghamatotso.
Uleke kutwingitsa mne ghamaghetso,
ila utukombole na ayula Yehile.
Kwaapfila ghamatsuwa ghose, ukulu una ghweghwe na ingupfu na utunitso.
Amina.’
