Kikongo

=== Traditional ===
Tata beto yikele na zulu,
Dizina ya nge ya sakumuka,
Kipfumu ya nge ya kuiza,
Luzolo ya nge ya vangama
na ntoto mutindu yake vangama na zulu.
Pesa beto bubu madia ya beto ya kilumbu yayi,
lembolo beto ba mfuka na nge
mutindu beto pe kelembolo na bayina beto bakaka ba mfuka,
pe nge bika beto ve kubua na mpukumunu,
kasi kula beto na yimbi.

Kimfumu, luzingu, pe lukumu ke ya nge tina tsuka. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
