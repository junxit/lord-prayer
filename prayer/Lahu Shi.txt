=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Lahu Shi (lhi, ISO 639-3 lhi), © 2015 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Muhd nuh maq lo chehd che ngag hehd ve Awr paog,
nawg ved awg meh haq khar thad liz yawz yan che cawg vid-oq.
Nawg ved muhd mig mig gig thang huh cawg vid-oq.
Muhd nuh maq lo nawg ved a lor cawd che heh
mig gig yad teq pa liz nawg ved a lor cawd che haq ghaq te vid-oq.
Yaq ni lor che cad tug dawg tug teq pa
yaq ni ngag hehd haq pehar laoq.
Su ngag hehd haq te yaq ax lar che vend bax haq
ngag hehd phehd kha vid che heh te lehq
ngag hehd ve vend bax haq liz phehd kha ax laoq.
Kheh te lehq, vend bax ber che awg khad teq pa khuhn,
ngag hehd haq taq kae cuha.
Kheh te lehq haeg che awg khad khuhn
ngag hehd haq pong ax laoq.
