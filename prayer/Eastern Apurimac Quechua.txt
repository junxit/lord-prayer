=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mosoq Testamento (qveNT, ISO 639-3 qve), © 2013 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Hanaq pachapi Dios Taytayku,
sutiyki yupaychasqa kachun.
Hamuy noqaykuman sumaqta gobiernawanaykikupaq.
Munayniyki ruwasqa kachun, imaynan hanaq pachapipas hinallataq kay pachapipas.
Sapa p'unchawtaq t'antaykuta qowayku.
Tukuy huchaykutapas perdonawayku,
imaynan noqaykupas perdonayku mana allin ruwawaqniykutapas.
Amataq dejawaykuchu tentasqa kanaykuta, aswan mana allinmanta waqaychawayku.
{Ichaqa qanpan, kay pachapi, hanaq pachapi gobiernasqaykipas, atiyniykipas, hinallataq sumaq k'anchariq kayniykipas wiña-wiñaypaq. Amén}”, nispa.
