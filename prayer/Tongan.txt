Lea faka-Tonga

=== Traditional ===
Ko ʻemau Tamai ʻoku ʻi Hēvani,
ke tāpuhā ho Huafa.
Ke hoko hoʻo Pule.
Ke fai ho Finangalo, hangē ko ia ʻi he langi ke pehē foki ʻi māmani.
Ke ke foaki mai he ʻaho ni haʻamau meʻakai ki he ʻaho ni.
Pea fakamolemole homau ngaahi moʻua;
hangē kuo mau fakamolemole foki ʻa kinautolu ne moʻua kiate kimautolu.
Pea ʻoua naʻa ke fakahū kimautolu ki he ʻahiʻahi;
ka ke fakahaofi kimautolu mei he Fili.

He ʻoku ʻoʻou ʻae pule, mo e mālohi, mo e nāunau, ʻo taʻengata. ʻEmeni.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
