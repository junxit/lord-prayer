=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yaubada wasana: Buka Hauhauna tupwana (sbe, ISO 639-3 sbe), © 2007, 2013, 2017, 2021 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Tamamai galewa ne unai! Kowa hesam ka hededetausaei.
Yom basileiya bena ye laoma. Yom nuwatu bena ka ginauliwatanidi tanoubu ta unai, doha galewa ne unai yom nuwatu se ginauliwatanidi.
Mayadai ta kaina ku leyama.
Yoma yababa ku nuwatugabaeidi, doha hinage kai tauheyababagai yodi yababa ka nuwatugabaeidi.
Bena ku kitahetetegai na taba nige ka lulau noho luwanaena, na tauyababa yona nuwatu udiyedi ku gilihaigai.’”
