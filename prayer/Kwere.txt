Chikwere

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Biblia Ching'hwele (cwe), © 2014, 2019 The Word for the World and Pioneer Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Uching'he diyelo ndiya yetu. Uchilekelele ubananzi wetu uja uchitendile,
fana cheye vichiwalekelele wamwenga wowachihasanye. Sekeuchigume muna yamagezo,
mbali uchikombole na ija mwihi,
kwaviya ufalume wako una ludabwa, na utunhizo wa siku zose.’
