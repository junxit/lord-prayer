Garuh

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from KAYAKNU NAI Tituanak Bau (gaw), © 1990 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the opening quotation mark the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Ig Mamenig, na hab aṯan daaṯem. Na hep̱e, danab oh ag na onin, nug onilag laa oh eḏadṯe doon, aṯannab human, dab menana oiyeg!
Na hep̱e, ḏo maḏoḏ nahip wan imup̱a dayaḏ! Na hep̱e, ig hab daup keeke dab menan heṯeb amubia ig ele dim lamidtu!
Ig e megṯem ele amu gemunu ele meḵe!
Laa hip̱unin megṯeb, ig uhuqta madaṯem, amubia na hip̱unin ihinig uhuqna meḵe!
Na ip̱un ena, ig nau heḵunignu eeḏak iiṯap̱a omaigna, Satan ep̱egp̱anu awiḵe! Ḏo maḏoḏ, g̱agaṯag amu binag oḵai ele am hanhan nahipp̱aib daaṯe, oo mak ena, g̱agaṯag oh ele. Hanhan na binan ele daaḵut! Amen,’” awa aum.
