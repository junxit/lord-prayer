Sεlεε

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Selee (snw, ISO 639-3 snw), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Teewo wɔ ninkpe ni di osi,
ta balɛɛ leyooto lɛfɔ klekle nwu ku dibu.
Sɛka kalekɔ lɛfɔ diwa wo kaamɛ.
Ta babla fɔ dibiesaa
kaasɔ ka mmle suoto fɛ mmle dikpe ni di osi.
Ta wo miɛ alesaa ya niefĩ wo ni.
Faayɔ sikpile loo fakye wo,
fɛ mmle okle di awo kafɔɔ buɔyɔ buɔkyɛ ni batii ba niɛbla wo ni sikpile.
Tankpaa wo faakpee di ɔsɔ onyu siene kamɛ,
kafɔɔ dikiwo fakyeko kpiletɛ nnɛɛ kamɛ.
Diekye lelefɔ ninle sɛka kalekɔ ku osie ku leklekle ninnaa ni kaloo.’
