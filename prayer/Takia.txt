=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Anut san bilaluŋ foun (tbc, ISO 639-3 tbc), © 1999 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

O Bai, oŋ did lo mado da.
Oŋane yaŋa gun migane da.
Oŋane kagin panek idu ipalu wa.
Oŋane awa mi muri mupani wa,
tan na nek ibol did lo digane dak ago mi a.
Gamu nal en san anaŋ panama wa.
Maŋsama gubun oŋ lo migane yan pasi yau a,
nek ibol aenta sad gubun maŋ lo diganeg mupasi dak ago mi a.
Maŋ kubianek lo madu san an lo ta giniama na wa.
Tia, Satan tuon lo patuma pasi wa.
