Yareni Zapotec

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Zapotec, Yareni (zae, ISO 639-3 zae), © 2014 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tata getu do yebaa, ri'itu rugwetu lidhakalu kumu ankalu Diosi.
Utá, unnabia ri'itu.
Benchi kini yiesi lo yu‑ni aka nu redá le kwinalu, beni ttiba rakaba yebáha.
Bete benne gotu ttu ttu sá
lhe uxí elha xen lasi ge nu satsa ruintu ttiba ri'itu gwa ridíbatu elha xen lasi ge ka nu rudhaka satsakana ri'itu.
Bittu ugwelhalu ixú yietu uintu nu satsa.
Bodiláttu ri'itu kini abittu iginnitu lo ná nu xxegwiha,
[kumu kwinalu satíaba ankalu benne rinnabia
lhe ankalu benne ra tsitsi lelu
lhe ra xxeni lelu ttu dia lii lhe. Amén.]
