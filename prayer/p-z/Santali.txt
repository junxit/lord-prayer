ᱥᱟᱱᱛᱟᱲᱤ (Santali)

=== Traditional ===
[Verified published text from the Santali New Testament (Bangladesh Bible Society), Matthew 6:9-13. The Santali Bible used by Santali churches in Bangladesh is published in the Roman/Latin orthography shown below; the language's own Ol Chiki script edition was not available for verbatim quotation, so the authentic Roman-orthography Bible text is given. Endonym shown above in Ol Chiki.]

Onate ape do nonka leka koejoṅpe; E aleren sermare menam Baba, amakʼ ńutum sonotokʼ ma.
Amakʼ raj hijukʼ ma. Amakʼ sana leka hoyokʼ ma, cetʼ leka sermare, onkage otre hõ.
Aleakʼ jạruṛ jomakʼ de teheń emalem.
Ar aleakʼ kạiko ikạ katalem, cetʼ leka ale hõ ale reakʼ kạkại kole ikạ katako kan;
ar biḍạute alom aderlea, menkhan bạṛicʼ khon bańcaolem.

[Ente raj ar daṛe ar ṭhonok jaejug hạbicʼ amakʼge, Amen.]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above. (The doxology is present in the source, set in brackets as a textual variant.)
