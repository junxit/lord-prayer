[Verified from Eriiragano Eriihya (zak, ISO 639-3 zak), ©  Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Taata weetu wa mwiisaaro,
eriina ryaazo riguungibhwe.
Obhutemi bhwaazo bhuuze.
Obhuseegi bhwaazo bhukorwe mukyaaro hano,
na kyeego bhukukorwa mwiisaaro.
Otuhe eziisiku zyoosi ebhyaakurya bhyeetu.
Otwaabhire ebhibhi bhyeetu,
kyeego etwe twoosi tukubhaabhira bhano bhakutusariirya.
Oturiihe mubhitiika,
nawe otutuurye no omubhi wurya,
[kwo okubha obhutemi ni bhwaazo, na amanaga, no obhuguungo, kirakeego!
Gabhe ego!]’ ”
