Sama Pangutaran

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kitab Injil (slm), © 1994 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘O 'Mma' kami Tuhan ma sulga', bang peen pig-addatan ōn nu sutsi uk aa kamemon,
Bang peen 'nsa' taggōl bo' kau magparinta ma manusiya' kamemon ma dunya itu,
Bang peen du bine' kabayaan nu ma dunya itu sali' iya asal bine' maiyu ma sulga'.
Buwanan nu kami balanja' ma 'llaw itu.
Ampunun manga dusa kami, sali' kapangampun kami ma sayi-sayi makabuwanan kami dusa.
Daa kami sulayan nu biyo ni diyōm sasat, suga' papuwasun kami min nakura' sayitan, iya makalaat.’ Salaihi' subay panambahayang bi.
