Yongkom

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Buk Baibel long tokples Yongkom (yon), © 2003 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Nub Ambe Ewen ari dibeeb,
ewaningko kuu karadmo ande kankoonuub.
Ebbed korok kere ooni yeman kuu munuk,
eb dowakenmo yaambed korem kwanok,
Ewen ari kwanimaib areb okad kiri kwamune kamimamok.
Kibikee nub dobiri ye animan kuu bangkandembere.
Kaneyambed nub yaa ambarakmiwen kuu are nonondanduwen areb kuu,
kwamune ebbed nub ambarakmi yaa kuu are nonondandunde.
Kwane, ambarakmaniib dee andi ye kiwaan ari nub ben nongkobaab.
No kwane awad arewambed nub yaa manaan ye dowad kuu nub benkoobe.’ kwane andime.

Same as the Traditional form above.
