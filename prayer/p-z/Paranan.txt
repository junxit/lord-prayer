[Verified from En Maganda A Bareta Biblia (prfNT, ISO 639-3 prf), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ama mi a ked dilanget,
sambaán nakuwan en ngaran mu.
Maghari ka nakuwan dikomi.
Masunud nakuwan en kaluuban mu ti lutaiday a kona dilanget.
Átdenan mu kami nadid ti pagkain a aldew-aldew;
sakay patawadán mu kami ten kasalanan mi hidi, a kona ten págpatawad mi ten magkasala hidi dikomi.
Sakay dyan mu kami pabayan a matoksu, nan iligtas mu kami ten Madukás!’ [Gapu kao mu en kahariyan, en kapangyariyan sakay kaluwalhatian a awan ti katapusan! Amen.]
