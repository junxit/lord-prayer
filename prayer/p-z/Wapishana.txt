=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kaimana'o Tominkaru Paradan (wapNT, ISO 639-3 wap), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘WaDaru aokazi iaꞌo,
marii ipai pidannao taapan pugaru umanawunuꞌo nii.
Marii kizi punaobanai-tinan.
Marii kizi shaꞌapatin-karu puaipan kawanaꞌati
daꞌaꞌa imiꞌi baaraꞌa,
naꞌapa aokazi iainao shaꞌapatinan kawanaꞌati.
Marii putaan waꞌati aizii wawanyukunuu nii.
Pumarinaꞌata ipai wuruꞌu oiaꞌo washaꞌapatanii kidaz,
naꞌapa wamarinaꞌatan kawan ipai oii kidaꞌo shaꞌapata-kariwaiz waꞌati.
Aonaa putaapadaꞌanaꞌa wanyukunuu tiwaa-kidapa-kao oiaꞌo diꞌiti,
mazan kaiman putaapa waunao oiaꞌo ai.
[Karikaonanuꞌo pugaru wuruꞌu naobanaiz,
maꞌozakaꞌoraz, naꞌiki konaunamaꞌoraz.
Amen.]’
