Fulfulde Burkina

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Fulfulfe Burkina, Dewtere Laamɗo Amaana Keso (fuhbkf), © 2012 SIM International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Baaba amin gonɗo dow kammuuli,
innde maa laatoo seniinde.
Laamu maa wara.
Muuyɗe maa ngaɗee dow leydi ɗo hono no ngaɗirtee dow kammuuli ni.
Kokkaa min hannden nyaamdu heƴooru min.
Njaafonoɗaa min hakkeeji amin,
hono no min njaafortoo waɗooɓe min hakkeeji ni.
Taa accu min njarribee,
ammaa hisin min e Bonɗo oon.
[Sabo laamu e baawɗe e teddeengal ina ngoodani maa faa abada. Aamiina.]
