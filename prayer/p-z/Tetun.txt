Lia-tetun

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tetun Alkitab (tet), © 2013 Unit Bahasa dan Budaya, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amikaan Ama iha laleꞌan! Ita Boot naran mak lulik kaliuk.
Nebee ema hotu-hotu foti-nahaas Ita Boot naran.
Nebee Ita Boot dadi Naꞌin bodik ema hotu-hotu!
Nebee ema hotu-hotu nalo tuir Ita Boot harukan iha raiklaran,
oin nudaar makbukar laleꞌan sia nalo tuir Ita Boot harukan iha laleꞌan.
Foo baa ami haan mak toꞌo sura loron.
Maluꞌa lakon amikaan salan,
hanesan ami moos haluꞌa lakon salan mak ema nalo baa ami.
Daka ami nebee ami keta halo aat.
Sori ami mosi diabu niakaan aꞌaat.
[Tan Ita Boot beran kaliuk, makaꞌas kaliuk.
Ita Boot mak kaer ukun hori nima-nimak toꞌo nima-nimak.
Nunia tebes. Amen.]’

Same as the Traditional form above.
