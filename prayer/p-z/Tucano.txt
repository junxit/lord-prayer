Dahseyé

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Õ'âkĩ̶ hɨ yeere uúkũri turi Tukano (tuoNT), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

”‘Pacʉ, mʉꞌʉ ʉꞌmʉsepʉ niꞌi.
Nipeꞌtirã mʉꞌʉrẽ wiopesase meꞌrã ẽjõpeoato.
Aꞌti turipʉre mʉꞌʉ beseꞌcʉ maata wiogʉ sãjãgʉ̃ aꞌtiato.
Ʉꞌmʉsepʉre mʉꞌʉ ʉaro diaꞌcʉ̃ weesama.
Aꞌti turicjãrã quẽꞌrã mejãrõta weeato.
Ʉ̃sãrẽ ʉmʉcorinʉcʉ̃ baꞌase oꞌoya.
Ʉ̃sã ñaꞌarõ weeꞌquere acobojoya.
Ʉ̃sã ãpẽrãrẽ acobojoꞌcaro weronojõta ʉ̃sã quẽꞌrãrẽ acobojoya.
Ʉ̃sã ñaꞌarõ weesĩꞌrĩsere cãꞌmotaꞌaya.
Tojo nicã ñaꞌagʉ̃ ʉ̃sãrẽ niquesãcã, cãꞌmotaꞌaya.
Mʉꞌʉ niꞌcʉ̃ta dutimasĩꞌi.
Tutuayʉꞌrʉgʉ niꞌi.
Mʉꞌʉ niꞌcʉ̃rẽta masã ẽjõpeorãsama.
Mʉꞌʉ ninuꞌcũcʉ̃saꞌa’, ni sẽrĩña Õꞌacʉ̃rẽ.
