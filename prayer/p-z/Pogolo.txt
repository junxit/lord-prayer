Chipogolo

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Lipatanu Lya Syayi Kwa Wantu Woseri (poy), © 2014 Pioneer Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tati gwetu gwa kumpindi,
Litawu lyaku likwiswi.
Ufalumi waku wizi,
Shagufira shitendeki pasipanu gambira kumpindi.
Gutupanani leru shiboga shetu sha kila lishaka.
Gutulekiziyi vidoda vya twenga, ntambu yatuwalekeziyiti yawatutendiriti vidoda.
Nagutwingiziya mukujerwa,
Kumbiti gutulopoziyi kulawa kwa Mkondola ulii’.

Same as the Traditional form above.
