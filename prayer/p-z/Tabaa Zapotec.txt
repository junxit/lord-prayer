=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Xela wezria cube nen salmo caʼ (zatNTps, ISO 639-3 zat), © 1981 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Xrantu' Dios, zu' xabáa,
gaca lá'azxaqueze Lau'.
Gudá nna be'u lu xe̱zr la xu nigá.
Gaca lu xe̱zr la xu nigá ca dxaca lazru' Lue',
cáte̱ze dxaca xabáa.
Be̱nna netu' da gáguntu' na'a.
Bnite lau chee̱ntu' ca naca da cale̱la da be̱ntu',
ca netu' ba bnite launtu' chee̱ benne' belune̱' chee̱ntu'
da cale̱la.
Quebe gu'u lataj nu gun zréaje netu',
2 san bselá netu' lu na' da xriwe̱'.
Naca chiu' gápaquezu' xel-la' dxenná bea,
ne xel-la' waca,
ne da xabáa chadía chacanna. ¡Ca'an gaca na!
