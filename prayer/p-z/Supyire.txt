sùpyìré

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from KILE JWUMPE SEMƐŊI JWUMPE NINTANMPE (spp), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Wuu Tuŋi u ɲyɛ nìɲyiŋi na ke,
mu mɛge ku táan.
Ma pyi saanwa sùpyire puni ɲùŋɔ na.
Mu ɲyii wuuni li pyi ɲìŋke na,
bà li ɲyɛ na mpyi nìɲyiŋi na mɛ.
Wuu niɲjaa ɲjyìŋi kan wuu á.
Wuu kapegigii yàfa wuu na,
bà wuu maha wuu shinɲɛɛbii wogigii yàfani pi na mɛ.
Ma hà yaaga yaha ku wuu yyaha kɛ̂ɛnŋɛ kapegil'á mɛ,
maa wuu shwɔ Sitaanniŋi na.
[Ɲaha na yɛ mu u ɲyɛ fànhe ná síŋi ná pèentefoo,
fo tèekwombaa.
Amiina.]”
