Witu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Weneya Totono Keraisu Yesunomo (wiu), © 1990, 1992 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Toneketai a tibuke mea kakago neke ibinipa ali atoame paya tetepo takameneya kei pimotono ta.
Neke ali atoa pawe toa talo toano ta. Neme ta wa okole-kama a tibuke meki-kitame litia tiki toa itonoke meki ali atoameka edikoa litia tomotono ta.
Tone opi noateko neepa neme moa lawetamene.
Tone tokoo poaneleke po letu mekakana keetapeamene. Nipa toto-pala poanele tokoi yene kini eni tokoi poaneleke totome po letu mekakoona keetapekete toko toa nitamene.
Padele padeleme toto etepea liamomotono, yopikoa kamene. Poanele-kama tokagoke toto toa moamene wa Akolali kowitikala tamene.
