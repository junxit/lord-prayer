=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yaweyuha New Testament (yby, ISO 639-3 yby), © 1983, 2011 Biblica, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Okulumau ahotelika, ulikaya eto oko minadeyo.
Numuto namato ujapa oletokaka elewoleyakama soto peko suwideyo. Okulumauya emo ladawotoka moladawotoka iki elekele igitikaka niae. Sa-ikaka niabo nesa maya ma mikau lemotokakiya asako soto pideyo.
Muki yulakaya nodenesate lemo.
Lemoya iwetelesida lihimanipo le hulobetudawo nesa leko lemo lihimatema le-minadawo maya le huloleto.
Emokahoya muluteu hitibo nesalo lilime molamo. Lahelamibo weloya le olowe oleto.
