OluBwisi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ndaghaano Mpyaka na bimui haa bitabo byꞌomu Ndaghaano Nkulu mu Lubwisi (tlj), © 2019-2025 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“ ‘Eseetu̱we wʼomu eghulu,
bantu boona bahu̱ti̱ye li̱i̱na lyawe.
Bukama bwawe bwise.
Ebi obbali̱ye bikoluwe
munsi ngoku bikukoluwagha mu eghulu.
Otuhe obwalo byokuliya byatu bya bu̱li̱ kilo.
Otughanile bibhi byatu,
ngoku naatu tukughanilagha abakutukolagha kubhi.
Otatu̱si̱i̱mi̱lani̱ya kwohebuwa,
bhaatu otucungule ku̱lu̱gha ewaa Mubhi.
[Nanga bukama, maani̱ kandi bu̱toki̱ mbyawe bilo nʼebilo. Aami̱i̱na.]’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
