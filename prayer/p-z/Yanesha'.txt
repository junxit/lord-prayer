Yaneshac̈h

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yompor Poʼñoñ ñeñt ̃ attõ Yepartseshar Jesucristo eʼñe etserra aʼpoctaterrnay Yomporesho (ameNT), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Yompore Partso ñeñt̃ep̃ peʼntañoʼtsen, yentapepaʼch p̃a atarr Partsop̃.
C̈hapuepaʼch Nompore allempoch atarr p̃aʼyochreshat̃terra arr patsro. Yetsotonapepaʼch arr patsro ñeñt̃ atet̃ pemnen p̃a Nompore ñeñt̃ atet̃ penet ñam̃a all peʼntaño.
C̈hocmuepaʼch Nompore p̃apueney ñam̃a yocrram̃ ñeñt̃ allpon yepalltaʼyena pat̃eʼtets yet̃.
Pep̃retnaʼhuonayepaʼch Nompore allohuen yoʼchñar ñeñt̃ ama pocteyeʼ pentapretenye att̃o Nompore yapaʼc̈hoʼña yep̃retnannena ñerraʼm esempo errot̃ yep̃annena.
Ñam̃a amach Nompore pemnatatsche sosyaʼtsañ oʼch atarr topateney. Añchaʼña c̈hoch Nompore p̃aʼypoʼñataterrey allohuen sosyaʼtsañec̈hno. T̃arroʼmar eʼñe p̃a Nompore ayochreshat̃tetsa ñeñt̃ep̃ atarr Partseshatets, eʼñe p̃apt̃a ñeñt̃ep̃ Nompore ayeʼchoc̈htataret̃tetsa eʼñe errponañohuen. Ñeñt̃ach nenamap̃; ñeñt̃paʼ att̃epaʼch Nompore.
