tu’unsavi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mixtec, Santa María Zacatepec (mza), © 2015 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Yo'o ra kùu Tatá ndi, ra ìyo andivi,
na ya'aga ií kùu ma siviun.
Na kichi ma nuu chà'ndaun tiñu.
Ta na kuu ma naja kua kùuniun ma nuñu'u', ma takua kùu yi iti' andivi.
Na ku'vaun ma ña chìniñu'u ndi kachi ndi vitin.
Na jàka'nuniun ma kuati jà'a ndi,
takua jàka'nuni ndi kuati ma ndian jà'a ña kini chi'in ndi.
Ta koto kü'vaun ña titunini ndi jà'a ndi ma ña töve va'a.
Va na ja'un ña na kaku̱n ndi nuu ma ña kini.
Vati maun kùu ma ra chà'nda tiñu, ta maun kùu ma ra ìyo ndatu,
ta juuni maun nduva'a ka'nu kùu̱n ndia.
Ta takan endeeni na kùu yi. Amén.
