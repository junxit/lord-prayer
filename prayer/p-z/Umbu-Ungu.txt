Umbu-Ungu Andelale

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from PULU YEMONGA UNGU KONDEMO (ubu-andelale), © 1998 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Olionga mulu-koleana moleno Tata,
yombomane ‘Nu imbi ola molopa
ye kake seli peangamo moleno.’
ningu molangi.
Nu ye nokoli kingimu molko
yomboma nokoni walemo wendo opili.
Nunga ungumu mulu-koleana pilku liku selemele mele
aku siku ya ma-koleana pilku liku sangi.
Olio kiniá kere-langi nomolo mele kiniá siyo.
Yombomane olio seko kinjengi kinia
siye kolopo ulu se naa semulu mele
aku sikula olione nu sepo kinjemulu uluma
siye kolko olio ulu se naa seyo.
Olio ‘Ulu sene kondi topili.’ naa ningu
ulu-kirimanga pali ulu-pulumu pelemo ⸤kuru⸥muni
‘olio naa ambolopili.’ ningu yunga kína wendo liyo.
⸤Nu koleama kinia mélemanga pali
ye nokoli kingimu molko,
tondolo olandopamo nu-kinia pepa,
nu imbi paa olandopa molopa
nu-kinia pa tondolo sepa pepili
alieli molko mindi pulinu kene
aku sipa sepili.⸥” ningu aku siku Pulu Yemo kinia ungu niengi.
