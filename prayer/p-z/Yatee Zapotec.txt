[Verified from Dižaʼ chaweʼ kub len Salmo kaʼ (ztyNTps, ISO 639-3 zty), © 2002 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

X̱ato' Dios, zo' ya'abá, ga'n ẕen Lo'.
Da, nna bi'o yežlyó nga.
Žénelto' gaken yežlyó nga kan žaz ḻažo'o Le'
kan žaken ya'abá chio'.
Benn da' gawto' na'a ža.
Benít ḻaw cheto' da' ẕinnj da' žonto',
kan ženít ḻawto' che no benne' žone' cheto' da' ẕinnj.
Bi go'o latje gon da' x̱iwe' ga bága'to' doḻa',
san beslá neto' ḻo na' da' žonen,
dan' zej nak chio' dot yeḻa' wnná bia',
na' dot yeḻa' wak, na' dot yeḻa' ẕen,
da' ka' náptezo'. Ka'kze gaken.
