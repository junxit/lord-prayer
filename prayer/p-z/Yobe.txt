=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Nkomine Fal Ritɛlɛ́ (soy), © 2010 UEEB/SIM au Benin, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Urɔ́sɑ́ɑ, pɔ́ɔ kɛyómɛcɑɑ-pɔ ń we,
Pisoi pikɛ́ céri rɛ tipɔ́nyíri yɛ kóólɛlɛnlɛ,
Ipɔ́yɔ́ɔpi ikɛ́ kɑm,
Mɛpɔ́lɑ mɛkɛ́ kɛtẽ kɛcɑ́ɑ́ wɑ yɑrɛ kɛyómɛcɑɑ-pɔ.
A yɛ ḿpɑ́ kɛweesi kɛ́ye ilukɛ rɔ́ hɛ,
A ɑrɔ́kópɛ rɔ́ sɑ́rɛi yɑrɛ kɛ tɔ́ɔ yɛ kɔ picɔ n sɑ́rɛi
pin ɑkópɛ rɔ́ n wɑ mɛcɔ.
Kɑpɛ tíyɛsɛ tɔkɛ́ pipeikɛɛ kɛ́mɛɛ loó.
Amɑ́ ɑ kuníri ɑnipɛ-i rɔ́ lesɛ.
Li we rɛ pɔ́ɔ iyɔ́ɔpi nɛ ńnɑŋɛ nɛ
ḿmulɛ te ḿpɑ́ píyei-pɔ.
Amí.
