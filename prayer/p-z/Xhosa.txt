isiXhosa

=== Traditional ===
Bawo wethu osezulwini,
Malingcwaliswe igama lakho.
Mabufike ubukumkani bakho,
Mayenziwe intando yakho emhlabeni njengasezulwini.
Siphe namhlanje isonka sethu semihla ngemihla.
Usixolele izono zethu,
njengokuba nathi sibaxolela abo basonayo.
Ungasiyekeli ekulingweni,
koko usisindise enkohlakalweni.

Ngokuba bobakho ubukumkani, namandla, nozuko,
ngoku nangonaphakade. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
