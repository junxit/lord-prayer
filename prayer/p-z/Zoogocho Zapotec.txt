Diža'xon

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dižaʼ güen c̱he ancho Jesucristo (zpqNT), © 1987 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

X̱ato' beṉe' zoa yaba, chṉabto' yogo'ḻoḻ beṉe' yesonxene' le'.
Zelaže'to' ṉabi'o yic̱hjlaždao' yogo' neto' beṉac̱h.
Gacšca can' chazlažo' yežlio nga can' chac yabana'.
Ca nac dan' che'ej chagwto' tža tža, beṉšcan ṉa'a.
Na' bezi'xenšca c̱heto', ḻe lencze neto' chezi'xento' c̱he ḻjež beṉac̱hto' bite'teze da' chesonde' neto'.
Na' bitw go'o latje soa da' gwžigan' neto' gonto' da' mal, san bcuas bcue'ejšca neto' len da' mala'.
Le'na' chnabi'o na' napo' yeḻa' guac na' naco' beṉe' zaca' juisyw toḻi tocaṉe.
Ca'czen' gacšca.
