Wolayttatto Doonaa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Wolaytta Bible (wal), © 2004 SIM, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Saluwan de7iya nu Aawau,
ne geeshsha sunttai anjjetto.
Ne kawotettai yo;
ne shenee saluwan hanidoogaadan,
sa7ankka hano.
Hachchi hachchi koshshiya qumaa
nuuyyo hachchi imma.
Nuuni nuna naaqqidaageetuyyo
atto giyoogaadan,
neenikka nu naaquwaa atto ga.
Nuna iitabaappe ashshanaappe attin,
paacen gelissoppa.
Kawotettai, wolqqainne bonchchoi
merinaukka neessa. Amin77i’ yaagite.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
