Sarangani Blaan

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from I tnalù dwata (bps, ISO 639-3 bps), © 1998 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Màmi déén di tah langit,
kayèmi moon ku kdee dad to mangamfù di ge.
Kayèmi ku ge magot i kdee dad to,
na ku gdohò i kdee knayeam dini di tah tanà
salngad kagdohoan déén di langit.
Blém gami gablà knaanmi di duh ani.
Na nlifetam i dad salàmi,
salngad klifetmi salà i dad to gamsalà di gami.
Nangam faloh di gami i dad tilew gamgebe gami di salà,
bay falwaam gami di ku Satanas gumdà i kdee sasè.

Same as the Traditional form above.
