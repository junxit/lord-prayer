=== Traditional ===
[Verified from AIREPA VAE REO OVOI (roo), © 1982 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Igei Aitearo eisi toupari vuvuiua-ia, vii vaisiaropa avivikepaiei vii vaisiaro kae piepaoro rutu.
Vii vo Saraaro urio pieri. Vii vo vovouaro puraive eisi vuvuiua, oiso osa raga igei uvui iraopaiei ra va sirova utuio vo rasito.
Vo vokio igei vateri aioa vao vo vokio iare.
Igei varaaro vuri vovouro verari, vo rara oeavu iava oaravu vuriara vera rovopaio voeao oea igei vuri piepaiveira.
Opeita igei tovopari kokara-ia. Vara asavira igei vatatopo piepari kokarapatoapa tugarato Seten.
