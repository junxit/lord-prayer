=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tuhun tsaa tsa nacoo̱ jutu mañi yo Jesucristo tsi yo (mjcNT, ISO 639-3 mjc), © 1994 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Jutu Mañi ndi, tsa nyaá un gloria.
Jacahnú xaan ndi tsi un.
Na quitsi tsa ndaca ñaha un nu ñuhu ñayɨvɨ ihya,
tan na javaha tandɨhɨ nyɨvɨ tandɨhɨ cuhva cuñí maa un, tumaa cuví nda gloria.
Cu̱hva tsa catsi ndi tahan tahan quɨvɨ.
Ja̱ha tucahnu iñi tsi ndi náa tsa ña vaha javaha̱ ndi, tumaa tsa jahá maa ndi tucahnu iñi tsi nyɨvɨ tsa javahá tsa ña vaha tsi ndi.
Ña cuhva un tsa nducu cuhva nu ña vaha tsi ndi, coto javaha ndi cuatyi nuu un. Tyin maa un cuví ra jacacú tsi ndi ndaha nu ña vaha.
Tan maa un cuví ra ndacá ñaha tsi ndi. Tan iyó tandɨhɨ tunyee iñi tsi un. Tan taahán tsi tyin jacahnu tandɨhɨ nyɨvɨ tsi maa ñi un tandɨhɨ maa quɨvɨ tan na ña cundɨhɨ tsi. Na cuvi tsi tacan ñi. Tyehen taahán tsi ndacan tahvi ndo tsi Nyoo.
