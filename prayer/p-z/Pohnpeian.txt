mahsen en Pohnpei

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kadede Kap Pjam Akan (pon), public domain, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Jam at, me kotikot nanlan. Mar omui en jaraui
Wei omui en pwaida. Kupur omui en wiaui jappa dueta nanlan.
Kotikido ran wet at tunol kaukaule.
Lapwa jan kit dip at, dueta kit me kin lapwada koroj, me wia dipi on kit.
Eder kaluan kit ren me katikatiamau kan, a nekid kit jan ni me jued akan,

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
