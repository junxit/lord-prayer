Tandroy

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Saontsin’ Añahare (tdx), © 2023 Steve Lellelid, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ry Rae’ay andindiñe ao,
ho masiñeñe ty tahina’o.
Ho tahieñe ty fifehea’o,
hanoeñe an-dindiñe ao
naho an-tane atoy o satri’oo.
Atoloro anay nainai’e ty mahakama’ay,
Apoho o tahi’aio,
manahake ty ihevea’ay o anaña’ay hakeoo.
Ko minday anay mb’an-kaozaram-panjiziañe,
fa kalaño amy ze atao raty,
[toe Azo ty fifeheañe, ty haozarañe vaho ty engeñe nainai’e.] Amene.

Same as the Traditional form above.
