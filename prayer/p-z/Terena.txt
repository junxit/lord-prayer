=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Emo'u Itukó'oviti (terNT, ISO 639-3 ter), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Iti Ha'a ûti íhae vanúke, émbemo téyeamo sasá'iti îhe ne uhá koeti xâne.
Émbemopi ukóponeamo ne netíxi yâye xapákuke ûti, uti xâne. Itúkapu yâha kousókovo yara poké'e kuteâti koeku koúsokeovo ya vanúkeke.
Peréxavi nika ûti itukovo káxe.
Ketúyapa pahúnevo ûti koêkumaka kóseanayea ûti ne po'ínuhiko xâne vaheréxinoviti ítuke, kutí kixoâti uti ákoti pahukápu.
Hako sike'évi víkorokeovo pahunévotike itea peréxavi víteova ne váhere kixovókuti, (vo'oku poéhane îti itukóvo hána'iti nâti ya uhá koêti. Poéhane îti apê xunáko oúkeke uhá koêti. Poéhanemaka îti itukóvo hána'iti ihayú'iuti ya uhá koêti, ákotinemo hunókoku. Itúkapu énomoneoxo koéneye)” yákoe itîka orásaum —kíxovokoxoane Jesus.
