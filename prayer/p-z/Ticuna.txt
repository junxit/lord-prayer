Duüxügu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tupanaarü Ore i Tórü Cori ya Ngechuchu ya Cristuchiga (tcaNT), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Pa Tonatü ya Daxũgucüx, rü aixcüma üünecü quixĩ i cumax.
¡Rü núma naxũ na torü ãẽ̱xgacü quiĩxü̃cèx! Rü tanaxwèxe i curü ngúchaü̃ na naxügüxü̃ i duü̃xü̃gü i ñoma i naãnewa ngẽxgumarüü̃ i daxũguxü̃ i naãnewa na curü ngúchaü̃ ínaxügüxü̃rüü̃.
¡Rü toxna naxã i torü õna i ñoma i ngunexü̃cèx ixĩxü̃!
¡Rü toxü̃́ nüxü̃ nangechaü̃ i torü chixexü̃ i taxüxü̃ ngẽxgumarüü̃ i toma rü tüxü̃́ nüxü̃ na tangechaü̃xü̃rüü̃ ya yíxema chixexü̃ tomaã ügüxe!
¡Tãxṹ i ṯacü rü chixexü̃pẽ́xewa toxü̃ cuwogüxü̃ na tama nagu tayixü̃cèx! Natürü tanaxwèxe i toxna cudau na tama ngẽma chixexü̃ taxügüxü̃cèx. Erü cuma rü guxü̃guma guxü̃ãrü ãẽ̱xgacü quixĩ rü cuporaxüchi rü cumexẽchi. Rü ngẽmaãcü yiĩ”.
