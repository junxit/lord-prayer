Chingoni

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ngoni Bible Version Translation Project (xnj), © 2020 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Dadi witu wewitama kunani,
Liina laku lilumbiwa neju.
Unkosi waku utibwelelai,
chewigana veve chikitika, pamulima apa ngati cheyivii kunani kwa Chapanga.
Utipela lelu chakulya chitu cha magono goha.
Utilekekesa tete getihokili,
ngati tete chetikuvalekekesa vevatihokili.
Ukotoka kutiyingisa mukulingiwa,
nambu utisangula na mhakau yula.
Ndava muni Unkosi na ukulu na makakala ndi vyaku hati magono goha gangali na mwishu.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
