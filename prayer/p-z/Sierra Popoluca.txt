Nuntajɨyi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Jém jomipɨc trato jém iwatnewɨɨp tánomi Jesucristo (poiNT), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

ManJa̱tuŋ Dios, miit́wɨɨp sɨŋyucmɨ,
agui cuáyñe mich iñɨ̱yi.
Wɨ̱ iga míñiñ jém ja̱ma iga iniŋjacpa yɨ́p naxyucmɨ.
Wa̱tɨ yɨ́p naxyucmɨ juuts mich iŋwɨ̱aŋja̱m, juutst́im iŋwatpa sɨŋyucmɨ.
Achiit́aamɨ yɨ́p ja̱ma t́it́am aŋcúttámpa.
Awadayt́aamɨ perdón jém antáŋca juutst́im aɨcht́am aŋwadayñeta perdón jém amalwadayñewɨɨp.
Awattaamɨ cuenta iga odoy i̱ wɨa̱iñ acutɨ́tsta iga acmalwattámpa.
Agui miwɨ̱, manJa̱tuŋ Dios.
Mich más mɨjpɨc miŋjagooyi.
Iniit́ it́u̱mpɨy jém impɨ̱mi.
D́a nunca micuyajpa. Amén.
