isiZulu

=== Traditional ===
Baba wethu osezulwini, malingcweliswe igama lakho;
mawufike umbuso wakho; mayenziwe intando yakho emhlabeni njengasezulwini;
usiphe namuhla isinkwa sethu semihla ngemihla;
usithethelele amacala ethu,
njengalokhu nathi sibathethelela abanecala kithi;
ungasingenisi ekulingweni,
kodwa usikhulule kokubi.

Ngokuba umbuso ungowakho, namandla, nenkazimulo, kuze kube phakade. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
