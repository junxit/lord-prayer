Chizigula

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Lagano hya (ziw), © 2014 The Word for the World and Pioneer Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tati yetu ywa kwembingu,
zina dyako Dikukile ditogolwe,
Useuta wako na wize,
hano he isi saviya vi kwembingu.
Chinke mdyo wetu chikunga dyelo.
Chilekeile masa yetu,
saviya chikulekeila masa chidamanyizwe ni watuhu.
Usekuchijela mwe majezo,
mna uchambule na yudya Mwavu.
Kwaviya uzumbe ni wako na udahi na ukulu mazuwa yoseni.
Navitende ivyo.’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
