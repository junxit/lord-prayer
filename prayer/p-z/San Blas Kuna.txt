San Blas Cuna

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Bab-Dummad-Garda-Islidikid (cuk), © 2014 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Anmar-Bab-nibneggi-maid, be-nug swilidik obariledii gusun.
Be-neg-dakmaid anmarse be sedage.
Igi nibneggi be-sogedba be ibmar imake, deyob, we-napneggi be imakdamo.
Emi-ibagi mas unnitibali anmarga be udage.
Igi dulemar anmargi nosale, anmargi-nossulidyob anmar dakdae,
deyobi, anmar-begi-isgusad begi-isgussuliyob be anmar-dakmo.
Isguedgi-wilubdaklegedse mer warmaked be anmar-imak. Bur isguedgi-obined be anmar-sae.
Birga-miliba degisadegu, be-neg-dakmaid begadid.
Be-gangued, degi, be-gangued-yeerdakleged, begadbalid.
Deyob gusun.’ ”
