Taap

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Anututane Wam Kwikwiu Kayuk Kaknga Jesu Kraisda Takepbut Taknga Anin gatu Sam Kuupbam Lotutane Kap Taknga (wnc), © 1975, 2001 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Nanin. Gata enandang yuayak.
Gatane umanda yatangenatningge nataamang.
Gata amin panangge tasinggayak kakngata ninde asapsok.
Asapan gatane banip enandang nana aminda tawakaing binga ninu kepdakane yuamang aminda unzakan tawanim.
Apmanu nanaminu tapduu anggwene dapmakamangu nimuyo.
Aminda ninde waiaknga pasinima sandeyamukamang binga
gata waiaknganinu sandetnimuyo.
Siwan gata sanga nin pasiwan sinimde ma ninipakuwim.
Sike gata nin gatanimi Setenda ninu dua pasiwa mainim.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
