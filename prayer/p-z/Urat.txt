[Verified from Wusyep Bwore tikin Got (urt, ISO 639-3 urt), © 2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Yai poi, nin narp nanah moihlaꞌ, naŋ nin ki yirise supule.
Lemame nin ka ot tus halhale.
Pe miyeꞌ tuweinge lalme kekep eꞌe ya mende gunde
ŋoihmbwaip nin murp kekep
tuꞌe ŋaiye tinge jande ŋoihmbwaip nin yarp yanah moihlaꞌ.
Tukwini ŋau eꞌe na pul poi ŋai tutume ŋaiye tukwini leꞌe.
Nin na ongohe hwap poi ŋahilyeh tuꞌe ŋaiye
poi masme hwap lenge mitiŋ ŋaiye yende yatme poi.
Topoꞌe yukur na osme dou ende ŋaiye ŋondolꞌme ende ka otme poi.
Kom na enge poi ya tupwaihme ŋaimune pupwa
ŋaiye ka ende yumbune poi.
