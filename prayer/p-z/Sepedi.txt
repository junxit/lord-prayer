Sepedi

=== Traditional ===
Tatewešo wa magodimong, leina la gago a le kgethwe;
mmušo wa gago a o tle; thato ya gago a e dirwe mo lefaseng bjalo ka ge e dirwa legodimong.
O re fe lehono bogobe bja rena bja ka mehla.
O re lebalele melato ya rena
bjalo ka ge le rena re lebalela ba ba nago le melato go rena.
O se re iše molekong,
o upše o re phološe bobeng.

Gobane mmušo ke wa gago, le matla, le letago, go iša mehleng ya neng le neng. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
