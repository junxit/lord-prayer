Tinata Tuna

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from A BUK TABU A MAULANA KUNUBU ma A KALAMANA KUNUBU (ksd), © 1882-1983 Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Tama i vevet arama ra bala na bakut, boina ba da ru ra iangim. Boina ba na vut kaum varkurai.
Boina ba da toratorom tam ra pia, varogop dia toratorom tam arama ra bala na bakut.
A bung gori una tabar i avet ma aveve nian.
Una re vue kaveve nilala, varogop ave tar re vue ka diat dia ga Iala pa ta vevet.
Koko da ben avet ta ra varilam, una valaun avet kan ra tena kaina. [Tago kaum muka ra varkurai, ma ra dekdek, ma ra variru, pa na mutu. Amen.]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
