yal’ool Yiuldiy

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Bogtag we ye Tefoy (uli), © 1995 Bible Society of Micronesia, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamem le ho mel woal laeng:
Be ssor mala idem le ye santus;
hobe budoh bo yamem Llulap;
le habe talenga yalom woal talop le be wochog woal laeng.
Fang ngalugmem mongoy la habe hangi igla.
Hachuya fael tayikof kala hasa foru,
le be wochog yamem hachuya fael tayikof kala resa foru ngalugmem.
Ho towe hasugmemloh irel hagelgel la ye weres,
bo hobe hadorahmem mo irel Moniyan.’
[Bo mala Lamliyem, mo ssor, mo falmay la yam ila tor siyal. Amen.]

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
