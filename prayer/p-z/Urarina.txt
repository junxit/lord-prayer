kachá

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Cana Coaunera Ere (uraNT), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Satiin canacaanu Inacaneein dedeeu nenaa Ofa,
Iineein neeine coitucuurumiin satiin cachaauru.
Turuhine Ii Cojoanonaa.
Caa chauratane que iicha jerei ichacurumiin,
dedeeu ichacuruhane rijijieein.
Cojoanoonetojoeein canacaanu rai lenone ocoteeu.
Baaso ichacaaunne,
canacaanu que ii suujua acateeu,
canacaanu rai baaso icharaauru que,
canacaanu suujua acatichaanune rijijieein.
Jiaonriha osa que nesaruhin
nihacaanune nainatejeein neeine.
Canacaanu caje moconajaera sitaau.
Inae,’ naain Cana Coaunera bajaauche.
