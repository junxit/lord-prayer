Tzʼutujiil

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Cꞌacꞌ Chuminem (tzjE), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

—Kadta' Dios jatc'ola chcaj, lok' ta xtna'a abi' cmal wnak.
Tkaj pc'a agobierno wawe' chwech ruch'lew, xtbantaj ta wawe' ajni' nrajo' awanm ajni' nba'na chawa tzra' chcaj ne ncatnimax wa'.
K'ij k'ij xtaya' ta kaway je nc'atzina chka.
Tecyu' kil kamac ajni' nkaban ajoj nquekacuy je' wnak tak nqueban je' lowlo' tak achnak chka.
Atet ta xtcatchajina kxin y mquita xtokkaja pruk'a' diablo, ko'at-o' pruk'a'. Jawra nekc'utuj chawa com pnak'a' atet c'o wa' njelal gobierno, y pnak'a' c'o wa' njelal poder, y noc na yabal ak'ij njelal achnak, y at cara' rxin junlic chpam je' tiemp jpenak. Amén. Cara' ta rbanic oración neban.
