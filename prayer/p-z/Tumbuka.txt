Chitumbuka

=== Traditional ===
Ŵadada ŵithu imwe muli kuchanya,
zina linu lichindikike.
Ufumu winu wize,
khumbo linu lichitike pano pasi nga nkhuchanya.
Mutipe lero kurya kwithu kwa lero.
Mutigowokere ise kwananga kwithu,
nga ndi umo nase tikuŵagowokerera awo ŵakutinangira.
Mungatitoreranga mu kuyezgeka,
kweni mutithaske kwa Muheni yura.

Pakuti ufumu, nkhongono, na uchindami nvyinu kwa muyirayira. Ameni.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
