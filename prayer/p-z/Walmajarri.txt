=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Wangki Wulyu Jirrkirlikanujuwal (wmt, ISO 639-3 wmt), © 1985 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ngarpu nganampakura, kankarnumarrangu man nguniny.
Nyuntu marnantalu yini julawu ngirrpiri nukarnirlu.
Japirlyungana marnantalu nyuntu maja ngunangupurru wapalkarrawu.
Nukarnirlu marnangulu mapunikarrku yangka marnanpangu kurajpungany jirrkirl ngunangupurru nyuntukurarlal. Kayanparni marnantalu mapunikarrku nguwakarrajiwarntirlujaa yalkirikarrajiwarntirlu Ngarpukurawarntirlu.
Yungkarnanpanya miyijaa kuyi jalarrapurru ngarnupurru yangka marnanpanya jininyarawarlany jininyarawarlany yungany.
Wajapungurla rukanyjanpangurla yakurryakurr nganampakura, yangka marnalunyanangu nganampa ngaru kirtilyjarrilany kanarlanywarntiwu.
Ngajirta ngarnanpanya kangka yakurrwarntirlal warntarrirlal. Marlamantarnanpanya warntarrirlaljangka pirlakuralaljangka yangkawurti ngarnalu tarrpantilany.
