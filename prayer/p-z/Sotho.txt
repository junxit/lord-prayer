Sesotho

=== Traditional ===
Ntate wa rona ya mahodimong, lebitso la hao a le ke le kgethehe;
ho tle mmuso wa hao; thato ya hao e etswe lefatsheng, jwalokaha e etswa lehodimong;
o re fe kajeno bohobe ba rona ba tsatsi le leng le le leng;
o re tshwarele melato ya rona,
jwalokaha re tshwarela ba nang le melato ho rona;
o se ke wa re isa molekong;
o mpe o re lwele ho e mobe.

Hobane mmuso ke wa hao, le matla, le kganya, ka ho sa feleng. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
