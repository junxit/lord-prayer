=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Chaʼ suʼwe nu nchkwiʼ chaʼ ʼin Jesucristo nu nka xʼnaan (ctpNT, ISO 639-3 ctp), © 1992 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ndiose Sti ba nu ndiꞌin nde niꞌ kwan, ska ti nuꞌwin ngiꞌni tnun nten chalyuu ꞌiin.
Kan nuꞌwin lo chalyuu re chaꞌ ka ndloo la ꞌwa.
Kuꞌnii chaꞌ ka nchga chaꞌ nu nchka tiiꞌ chaꞌ ka lo chalyuu re;
sa ñaꞌan nu ngiꞌnii nde seꞌen ndiꞌiin nde niꞌ kwan, ngwañaꞌan ka lo yuu re bra kanꞌ.
Taa nan ku ba tsan ni.
Kuꞌnii chaꞌ tlyu tiꞌ ꞌwa ꞌin nchga chaꞌ kuxi nu yꞌni ba, chaꞌ ngwañaꞌan wa yꞌni ba chaꞌ tlyu tiꞌ ꞌin xka la nten nu yꞌni chaꞌ kuxi loꞌo ba.
Ja taa chabiyaꞌ chaꞌ jwakii chaꞌ kuxi ꞌwa, kuloo ꞌwa yaꞌ kuneꞌ xaꞌan bra kanꞌ.
Nuꞌwin ndloo tñan nchga chaꞌ;
nchga chabiyaꞌ nu nsuꞌwi lo chalyuu, nchga chabiyaꞌ nu nsuꞌwi nde niꞌ kwan, nuꞌwin ndiya nchga chabiyaꞌ kanꞌ ꞌiin.
Ja tsaa tii ꞌa chaꞌ tlyu la ti nuꞌwin. Ngwañaꞌan ka chaꞌ. Ngwañaꞌan chkwiꞌ wan loꞌo Ndiose.
