Afaan Oromoo

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kitaaba Qulqulluu, Biblica® Hiikkaa Ammayyaa Banamaa Haaraa, Loqoda Dhiʼaa (gaz), © 2022 Biblica, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“ ‘Yaa Abbaa keenya kan samii irra jirtu,
maqaan kee haa qulqullaaʼu;
mootummaan kee haa dhufu;
fedhiin kee akkuma samii irratti taʼe sana,
lafa irrattis haa taʼu.
Buddeena keenya kan guyyuma guyyaa harʼa nuu kenni.
Akkuma nu warra yakka nutti hojjetaniif dhiifnu sana,
atis yakka keenya nuuf dhiisi.
Isa hamaa jalaa nu baasi malee qoramatti nu hin galchin.’
Mootummaan, humni, ulfinnis bara baraan kan kee ti. Ameen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
