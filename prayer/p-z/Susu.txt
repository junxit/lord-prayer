=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Soso Kitaabuie: Tawureta, Yabura, Inyila (sus), © 2015 Mission Evangélique Reformée Néerlandaise et les Traducteurs Pionniers de la Bible, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‹Muxu Baba naxan na ariyanna,
duniɲa birin xa i xili sɛniyɛnxi kolon.
I xa mangɛya xa fa.
I sago xa raba duniɲa ma,
alɔ a rabama ariyanna ki naxɛ.
Baloe fi muxu ma to lɔxɔɛ,
muxu hayi na naxan ma.
Diɲɛ muxu xa yunubie ma,
alɔ muxu fan diɲɛxi mixie haakɛe ma ki naxɛ,
naxee bara fe kobi niya muxu ra.
I naxa muxu ti maratantanyi kira xɔn,
i xa muxu ratanga Sentanɛ ma.
I tan nan gbe na mangɛya, sɛnbɛ, nun binyɛ ra abadan.
Amina.›»
