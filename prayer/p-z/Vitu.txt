Pole Vitu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kalohua Kemi ke Iesus na Polea Vitu (wiv), © 2019 Wyclif e Bijbelvertalers Nederland, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Tamahita heta na lagato, paleka hiza kua e tumonga ni vazaheanga.
Paleka ka kingdom ni mai, ka lohoihoia laveve hita na mumuri vona ri na garigari balika dia ta mumuri vona heta na lagato.
Vala ni hita meni hahita haninga kara dama meni;
Lohoi bala kahita moge zahazaha, balika hita ve kua hita ta lolohoi bala moge zaha kaka motu e katia ni hita.
Tabarae nu pelehita vano na parakilakilanga kua mara beta hita na madi ni bada vona, palaka pele kakava hita na limana kaka zaha.’
