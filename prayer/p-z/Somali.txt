Af-Soomaali

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kitaabka Quduuska Ah (som), © 1979, 2008 Society for International Ministries -- Kenya, Matthew 6:9-13. The wording in this file matches that edition word for word; collated against the text published at eBible.org.]

Aabbahayaga jannada ku jirow, magacaagu quduus ha ahaado.
Boqortooyadaadu ha timaado, doonistaada dhulka ha lagu yeelo sida jannada loogu yeelo.
Kibis maalin nagu filan, maanta na sii.
Oo naga cafi qaamahayaga sidaannu u cafinnay kuwa noo qaamaysan.
Oo jirrabaadda ha noo kaxayn, laakiin sharka naga du.

Waayo, boqortooyada iyo xoogga iyo ammaanta adigaa leh weligaa. Aamiin.
