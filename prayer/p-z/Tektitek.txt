Bʼaʼaj

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Te akʼaʼj tuʼjal tuj tuʼjal qtata Dios (ttcNT), © 2003 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

“Qtata Dios aj atichjax tuj ka'j, intqet xjani'xwit yatz ab'i.
Intkajxwit kyaqil tuj aq'ab' naj. Intb'antikxwit tzani twitz tx'o'tx' kyaqil iktza'x yatz agan, iktza'x nb'antik tuj ka'j.
Instzaj asi' ch'in ajna'l te qwe' aj n'oken b'ajq'ij.
B'ix intqet anajsa' ch'in te qchoj iktza'x qetza nqet qnajsa'na te nya'tx galan aj nkyek'ulun kye txq'anky qi'ja.
B'ix k'on atzoqpij ch'in tzan tok qen twitz jun tidi' aj oqotel tz'aqik titza' sinoke qo'el akolo' ch'in ti'j kyaqil aj nya'tx galan.”

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
