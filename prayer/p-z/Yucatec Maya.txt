Màayaʼ tʼàan

=== Traditional ===
C Yuum yanech te j caʼanoʼ, quiliʼichcunsaʼac a kaabaʼ.
Talac a ajawil. Beetaʼac baʼax a káat way luʼumeʼ jeʼel bix u beetaʼal te j caʼanoʼ.
Dzáa toʼon bejelaʼeʼ le waaj kabéet tiʼ toʼon sáamsamaloʼ.
Saʼates toʼon le kaas dzoʼoc c beeticoʼ, jeʼel bix toʼon c saʼatsic le máaxoʼob cu beetic toʼon kaasoʼ.
Maʼ a chaʼic c lúubul tiʼ túuntaj óolal, baʼaleʼ tocoʼon tiʼ kaas.

Tumen a tiʼal le ajawiloʼ, le páajtaliloʼ yéetel le nojbeʼeniloʼ u tiʼal kiinoʼob minaʼan u xuul. Amen.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.

(Source: Biblia Maya, San Mateo 6:9-13, © Sociedades Bíblicas Unidas 1992. This 1992 edition uses the older colonial-style Yucatec spelling — e.g. "dz" for modern "tsʼ", "c/qu" for "k" — and includes the doxology. The straight apostrophe of the source is rendered here as the saltillo ʼ.)
