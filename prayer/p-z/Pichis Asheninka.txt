=== Traditional ===
[Verified from KAMEETHARI ÑAANTSI: Iñaaventaitzirira Avinkatharite Jesucristo (cpuNT, ISO 639-3 cpu), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ashitanarí, nampitarori inkiteki,
Tasorentsi pinatzi eeroka.
Pimpinkathariventaina naaka.
Pantakainaro jaka kipatsiki okaratzi pinintakaanari eeroka,
Pinkempitakainari inkiteveri, pantakairi irirori maaroni pinintakairiri.
Pimpapiintenaro kovityiimotanari noyaari.
Pimpeyakotainaro nokaariperoshirevaitake,
Tema nomatakero naaka eejatzi, nopeyakotairi ikaratzi ovaariperoshiretakenari.
Eero pishinetana iriñaantaityaana, inkovaite irantakaitenaro kaariperori.
Ari pinkantaitatyeero pimpinkathariventante. Ari pinkantaitatyeero ontzimimotemi pishintsinka, eejatzi povaneenkaro. Ari onkantyaari.
