=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tnuʼu vaʼa tnuʼu Jesucristo (xtdNT, ISO 639-3 xtd), © 2001 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Taa‑ro Ianyuux, Ia tuu andɨu, na jaꞌan koio‑da xa ii‑n, te na koo koio‑da xañuꞌu nuu‑n.
Na jaꞌan koio‑da xa taxnuni‑n nuu‑da ntdaa‑da xa ka kuu‑da ñayiu ñuñayiu.
Nani ka kida ia xtuu andɨu xa juini‑n, dani na kada ñayiu ñuñayiu.
Taxi‑n xa kax xa koꞌo koio‑da vitna nani kida‑n ndɨkɨu ndɨñuu.
Xijan taꞌu‑da nuu‑n xa nani ka taxkanu ini‑da ñayiu kida ñaꞌa ɨɨn xa kida ñaꞌa‑i, dani taxkanu ini ñaꞌa‑n hora ka kida‑da kuechi.
Kada‑n xa maxku dajaꞌan ñaꞌa Kuiꞌna xa kada koio‑da kuechi; kada‑n xa maxku kada koio ka‑da xaloko. Duꞌa kajan taꞌu koio‑n nuu Ianyuux.
