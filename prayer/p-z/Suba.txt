=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Endagano Empia (sxbNT, ISO 639-3 sxb), © 2010 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“ ‘Soifu ori ku igulu, eriina riao rieewue owuti,
owuami wuao wuuze,
okuagala kwao kuikole ku ekialo, kiomo kuikola ku igulu.
Otu-ee reero okuria kwifu okwa owuire ki owuire.
Oturekere amawi giifu,
kiomo twona turekera awatukorra uwiiwi.
Otareka tuingire mu okugeezaniwua,
ni otununule mu amakono aga Omuwi.
Aeniki, owuami na amaani no owunene no owuao amiire goona. *Amin.’
