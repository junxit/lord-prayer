kweyol

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tèstèman nèf-la: Épi an posyòn an liv samz-la (acfNT), © 1999 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Papa nou ki an syèl,
nou ka pwédyé pou tout moun onnowéʼw kon Bondyé.
Nou ka pwédyé pou tout moun vini anba kondwit ou,
épi pou tout moun obéyiʼw asou latè-a
menm kon tout sa ki an syèl ka obéyiʼw.
Ban nou jòdi-a manjé-a nou bizwen-an.
Pawdonnen nou péché nou
menm kon nou ka pawdonnen moun ki fè nou mové.
Pa kité nou tonbé anba tantasyon,
mé délivwé nou anba lanmen Denmou-an.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
