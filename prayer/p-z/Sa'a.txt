=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Tataroha Diana i Sulie Aalaha Ikie a Jisas Kraes (apb, ISO 639-3 apb), © 2005 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Mama'a i Lengi, mwala ke ha'ama'u aana satamu.
Aalahanga i'oe ke lae mai.
Mo ola saemu eeni deuleni,
asuileni ke lae oto i aano i leu mala oto i Lengi.
O ke niie mai huni'emi mo ngeulaa e adona si'iri.
Na o ke sae'aasie huni'emi mo ooraha'aa i'emi,
mala i'emi no'one emi ko sae'aasie mo roro'anga ikire mo iini ko deu hu'isi'emi.
Na mwaanie o toli'aasi'emi huni ooraha'aa maholo malaahongalamami ko lae,
ta'e o ke lio i suli diana aamami mwaanie Iini Ooraha'aa.
