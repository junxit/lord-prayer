Saamáka

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Gadu Buku (srmNT), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ke, u Tata a liba o,
hii sëmbë musu lesipeki i e,
sabi taa i dë bumbuu limbolimbo.
U begi taa di Tii fii musu paaja a hii goonliba,
be di fii kë pasa a goonliba aki leti kumafa a ta pasa a i a liba ala.
Nöö u begi i tu, fii da u dee soni u abi fanöudu fuu pasa u libi tide.
Nöö u begi i o,
be i puu u a bëë fu dee hogi dee u ta du,
leti kumafa i si u ta puu dee otowan fuu a bëë fu dee hogi de ta du u.
Nöö u begi i,
taa na disa u da dee soni dee sa hai u tja go a di së u hogi e,
ma puu u a di didibi maun. Sö fuun begi e, dee sëmbë.
