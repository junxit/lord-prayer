Arara

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Diospan Tsain (mcdNT), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Nocon Upa nai murannoaton min fasi sharacoin.
Nantififain mihuunoa yoisharapainon.
Min apaiyai cuscan min ica
nai muranshon samamashta arisca huamisifo.
Nanscarifi nuno manifoti anaoafoan huanonfo cuscan
nanscacoinrifi nuno mai ano anonfo.
Manifotiton aton niaifo iishquin mia ifoannonfo.
Shafatifi nocon tushu non yopai cuscan noco inanhuun.
Nan noco chaca huaifoan
non ato rauhua cuscan huafain
nocon chacafori noco rauhuashohuun, Upan.
Satanás yoshin chacapan noco chaca huamapaifiaiton
nocoqui cumatima huahuu.
Nansca huaquin,” ishon cuficahuun ato huani.

Same as the Traditional form above.
