Q'eqchi'

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Li Santil hu (kekNT), © 2000 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

At Ka̱cua', cuancat sa' li santil choxa, lok'oninbilak taxak la̱ santil c'aba'.
Chicha̱lk ta la̱ nimajcual cuanquilal. Chi-uxk ta li nacacuaj, jo' sa' choxa jo' ajcui' sa' ruchich'och'.
Chaq'ue taxak ke li kacua kuc'a chi cuulaj cuulaj.
Ut chacuy chasach ta li kama̱c jo' nak nakacuyeb xma̱c li neque'ma̱cob chiku.
Ut moa̱q'ue taxak chi a̱le̱c. Choa̱col ban chiru li inc'a' us. A̱cue li nimajcual cuanquil, li lok'oni̱c ut li lok'al chi junelic k'e cutan. Jo'can taxak.
