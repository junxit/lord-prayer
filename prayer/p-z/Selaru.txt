tel Masylarkwe

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Maslyarw (slu), © 2020 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Oo, ara Amamy a mamin ti wen a sra eras so,
aramy miten ma aramy mumu ti lasmyerke khaha ne myalan a O,
kali Animkwe nelnyely.
Ode aramy miten ma mmwa kola Rajake, ma mhwareta aramy mumu,
ma nam sai mtwanuk, desikeo iry mamin ti lasmyerke khaha neke rtoha,
kola dene irire ratomolu O ti wen a sra eras mumin tike.
Ode aramy miten toha O ma senweke,
mal kotw o abw ma kyoat mamak aramy.
Ode mswos ara salamire ba,
kola dene, aramy misos iry maka salat ma aramy ne dakun.
Ode kete mlwura aramy
ma nam dum a khyury aramy ma kyor aramy ei atyatke,
de aramy miten toha O ma,
maditi aramy toha ktela atyat desikre mumu.
[Kali Oi ne lan a O,
ode mkwumak haretke,
ode wasimw madelafke
kyal nini nam ti kiba knaru ne.
Amin.]’
