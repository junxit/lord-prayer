Wa’ikhana

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dio Wadarique (tav), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Jã Pacʉ ʉmʉrecoo macacʉ, jĩcaʉna mʉ ã caroaʉ majuu, cáti nʉcʉbʉgopaʉ.
Ati yepapʉ Caʉpaʉ majuu ãña. Ʉmʉrecoo macana caroaro mʉ caboorije átinucuma. To bairo na cátore bairona ati yepa macana quena to bairona mʉ caboorijere na áparo.
Ʉgarique ati rʉmʉ jã caʉgapere jã jooya.
Caroorije jã cátaje majirioya. Jã quena rooro jãre cátanare na caroorijere jã majiriogarã. To bairi nare jã camajiriobojarore bairona mʉ quena jãre majiriobojaya caroorije jã cátajere.
Sataná jãre cʉ cawadajãro caroorije jã cátibopere jã matabojaya. Mʉ jeto carotii mʉ ã. Mʉ jeto catutuaʉ mʉ ã. Mʉ jeto camaja na cáti nʉcʉbʉgopaʉ mʉ ã to cãnacã rʉmʉ anicõa aninucuri. To bairona camaja mʉre na áti nʉcʉbʉgo anicõato. Amén.
