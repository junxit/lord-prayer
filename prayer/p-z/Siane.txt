Siane

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Gotiki olu kutifi ledami ka kofawa (snp), © 1991 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Metefo kosinau minanimo we ya wenena yate kulika olu faka lomo dito felegagale, loti minenawamo ne, loto kolone.
Ido kamo kiyabate we wekudi kiyaba o ledanimo kamena o fedenagoliye, loto kolone.
Ido kosinau weka lanido golodotiko minamo yamaidana oto wenena mikalo amau ya wekalo golodenune, loto kolone.
Ido kamena-kamena wete-neta lomo.
Ido wenena lifimani kilili o gedonimo yamaidana oto lamo lifimate ya kilili o ledo.
Ido mona nosámami kepa mele lumunami nedo ya lilimámaba, ido mona nosámamikafo nela wi ledenaiguti ya efili ledo.
[Kamo kiyaba we wekudi kiyaba o ledanimo ya minomo uto uto aumafofoka ya minomo uto uto kulika ya dimo uto uto minowaminowa oto minenami ne. Ya onale,] loti Goti loumunawamo ya eti loti loumalo.
