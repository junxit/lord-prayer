Yoem Noki

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yoʼowe Jesukrijtota itom yaʼariakaʼu betchiʼbo juʼu bemelaka liojta betana lutuʼuria yaʼari (El Nuevo Testamento en yaqui) (yaqNT), © 2003 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Itom achai Lios, teekau katekame. Em yo'orinaa'uta eu lutu'uriatune.
Che'a yo'otaka em nesaunee'uta, inim itomaktune.
Ju'u em joo i'a'uta inim ya'atune.
Jiba junaen teekau a' joowa'a benasia.
Juka' bwa'awamta jiba itou be'eemta, i'an itou autuane.
Ka tu'ik itom ya'arit itom jiokoine.
Junuen itom jume' ka tu'ik itou yaakame itom am jijiokoe'e benasia.
Unnaa itom ae ji'obilaanaa'uta, ka itou autuane.
Ala, bebeje'erita betana itom jinne'une.
Bwe'ituk ju'u che'a yo'otaka si'ime utte'ata jipueka em nesawee'u, intok ju'u yo'orisi maisi em eiyaanaa'u, jiba yuu betchi'bo eu aune.
Tua junuentune.
