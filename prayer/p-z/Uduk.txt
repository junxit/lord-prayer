=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Gwo Ma Arumgimis (udu, ISO 639-3 udu), © 2005 SIM International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ababam bam jin di’di momis,
dhalki gway pini a’di ḵogu mo ma.
Dhalki bampam pini a’di yayi’da ’cesh.
Dhali dhalki bwam pini a’di miinu a’cesh mo ma
me’d yan a’di miinu e momis.
Ka tee yansan c̱iyi am tonṯem bam jin ta ji gi isa yempa/ mo ma.
Dhal am amure/ miinthus bam ’pen mo ma
me’d yan si’da/ dhala aman ’kwani ’pen gun ’kon ka mure/ miinthus bam m’ba/.
Dhalku/ ki sus am e mo jin palu mo e miinthus.
Hili woṯẖi/ aman ’peni wathin thus mo ma.
