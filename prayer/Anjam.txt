Bom

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yesus Aqa Anjam Bole 2000 (boj), © 2000 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘O gago Abu, ni laŋ qureq di unum.
Ino ñam tulaŋ getento.
Ni bosim gago Mandor Koba sosim iga taqatgoqnime.
Laŋ qureq di naŋgi ino areqalo dauryeqnub dego kere iga mandamq endi ino areqalo dauryoqnqom.
O Abu, gago iŋgi uyo bati gaigai keretgoqnime.
Tamo naŋgi iga qa une ateqnub qaji naŋgo une kalil iga na kobotetnjreqnum deqa ni kamba gago une kalil dego kobotetgime.
Gulbe bei na iga walawalaigosim uneq waigwa laqnimqa ni iga aqaryaigime.
Yim Satan na iga ugetgwasai.’
