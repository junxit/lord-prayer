Kinyihanzu

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Isanzu Bible (isn), © 2020 Door 43 International Mission Community, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Tata witu nukoli kilunde lina lako likuligwe.
Utemi wako uze, ulowa wako witume mihi anga nuituma kilunde.
Ukupe indya yitu nia leliye.
Ukulekele imilandu anga usese nikialekela awa naiakukosee.
Uleke kuutwala muma geng'wa, ukupugamule numulugu.
