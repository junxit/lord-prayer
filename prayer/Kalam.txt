=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kalam Baybol Buk Gor minim nuk kisin angayak (kmh, ISO 639-3 kmh), © 1992 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

‘Bapiy yomb semb biyoŋ; yimb nand yiy ar biyoŋ mindip.
Nand chinup diy kond mindenimin; semb biyoŋ aŋgek niŋbay rek ak, chinup gey kun ak rek niŋjun.
Chinup tap ñiŋemb minek minek ñimbun rek ak ñinimin.
Biynimb gunap chinup timey gey niŋiy kirpun rek, tap siy tap timey chin gipun gok niŋiy kirginimin.
Chin yikop mindiy, tap siy tap timey giniŋgambun rek ayip ak; chinup kond mindey, timey ma ginjun.’
