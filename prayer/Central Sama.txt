[Verified from Central Sinama 2008 NT (sml, ISO 639-3 sml), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘O Mma' kami Tuhan ma sulga',
bang pa'in pinagaddatan ōnnu e' kamemon,
Bang pa'in mbal at'ggol bo' ka magparinta ma kamemon manusiya',
Bang pa'in bineya' kabaya'annu ma dunya itu
buwat asal bineya' mailu ma sulga'.
Jukupin kami balanja' ma llaw itu.
Ampunun saga dusa kami,
buwat kapangampun kami ma sai-sai makarusa ma kami.
Da'a kami tugutin binowa ni deyom sasat,
sagō' papuwasun kami min nakura' saitan, ya patila'atun.’
