Sona

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ina Sanaa Gagalowa (soq), © 1996 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Mako, e uboo wanwanlala, e eboa kakaisaipe wainipona.
E gonaa wisepona, ita e insaisaa uboo segaleidituwadia ruawa tawo osowe segaleipona.
Walama ruawaitone nanamba menanedia nenona.
Ita no kadiwamba inseadabuasaa, no sosomba kadiwa mainimbo dewasaiena inseitadabuasaitana rua.
Tane mai rubu bedewe itunea, kadi mamba nanausinepia sawa no oniadewasanena.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
