Umbundu

=== Traditional ===
[UNVERIFIED — the opening verses are confirmed from the published Umbundu Bible (Embimbiliya li Kola) and Global Recordings Network; the petitions for daily bread, forgiveness and deliverance below are a best-effort reconstruction and need human review against a printed Umbundu Bible, Mateo 6:9-13.]

A Isietu o kasi kilu,
onduko yove yi sumbiwe.
Usoma wove wiye.
Ocipango cove ci lingiwe palo posi ndeci ci lingiwa kilu.
Tu ihe etaili lietu lieteke.
Tu ecele akandu etu,
ndeci tu eca ku vana va tu lingila akandu.
Ku ka tu seti kelimbi,
puãi tu yovoli kuhanya.

Momo usoma, lunene, luẽ ulamba viove otembo yosi. Amen.
