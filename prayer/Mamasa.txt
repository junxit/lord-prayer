Pattae'

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Buku Masero: Pa'dandi Bakaru (mqjNT), © 2011 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Ambeki yao suruga,
dipomasero sangammu.
Paombo'mi kaparentaammu,
anna dituru' pa'kuammu
illalan lino susi yao suruga.
Beengkan nande la siruanna kiande allo temo.
Ammu garri'iangkan kasalaangki,
susi kami unggarri'imo to kasalaan lako kaleki.
Dau pabeaikan disudi la umpogau' kasalaan,
sapo la mupasikambelakan kakuasaanna kakadakean.
[Annu Ikomo to ummampui kaparentaan, kakuasaan, anna kamatandean sae lako-lakona. Amin.]’

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
