=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Vaikala Noxou Lataua (ata, ISO 639-3 ata), © 2009 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Mamixe nini nexoo teitexi no opo loxotolo, laixe nexi tatoxonu ualasine.
Laixe nini naluꞌe nasaxilaꞌu tuala ane no lia ꞌo.
Mo laixe talomuxene no lia ꞌo loxo aneꞌi ilolomuxene teitexi no opo loxotolo.
Vaimomo ꞌo nalosixe laꞌilali sou nexi taꞌou voxovoxo.
Naxiunu tumalineu ꞌiliꞌilixo masumasua noxixe
aloxo nexi taxiunu tumalixeu ꞌiliꞌilixo masumasua
noxiꞌa aneꞌi ane miꞌoxo masua muneꞌe noxixe.
Mamu sou naolenexi ulai no anu mimii sou tovotovo.
Ia naolenexi ulai vaꞌaxu no anu ꞌiliꞌilixo ane masumasua. (Xo saxisaxilaꞌu ne noxinesi mo xavi mo lamana anu kaluxu uasi. Manina.)
