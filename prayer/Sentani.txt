=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Sentani: Allahle a neme heteungekoke homofae (set, ISO 639-3 set), © 2020 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Arai surgane nekeyae,
Waei ro nene waewae hele yae mokomayewende.
Ro miyae Waei me kelee u keleene nekate yo yan meinyeibo.
Waei kenare koyae surgana wanen
nebei sului mbai nda kani ran kela ranne mokommikonde.
Maei raman mana ya naeise maere na hului yae yeumele.
Maei beko neban faeinyeumeko,
meyae hiwa yo naei beko neban mokaimekokere u bene nundaimekoke wanen mae.
Maere wabenaimele hee, ei haweumele
meyae ane nalemmihinde balemale belene.
Walobo yun, bekore mokole kiyaele me ei u eisa hole moloumebonde.
((Rabuhine ban Waei me kelee u keleene ro miyae nemene nekenate. Elae wake nane merau one hengko bele Waei einye huba ban haei ban ninae hononende. Amin.))’
