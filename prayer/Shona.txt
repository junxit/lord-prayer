chiShona

=== Traditional ===
Baba vedu vari kudenga, zita renyu ngarikudzwe;
umambo hwenyu ngahuuye, kuda kwenyu ngakuitwe panyika sezvinoitwa kudenga;
Tipei nhasi chingwa chedu chamazuva namazuva;
Tiregererei zvatinokutadzirai,
sezvatinoregererawo vanotitadzira isu;
Musatitungamirira mukuedzwa,
asi mutinunure kubva kuno wakaipa.

Nokuti umambo ndohwenyu nesimba nokubwinya, kusvikira narinhi. Ameni.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
