=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Na Rorongo Ke Toke Eigna a Jisas Krais (bgt, ISO 639-3 bgt), © 2009 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘A Tamamami i popo, o hatheghami eigna kitida ghaghana bohea na ahamu.
O mai mo vunaghi pungusira na komi tinoni gougovu
eigna kedana leghua nimua na vanohehe kori maramagna
vaghagna arahai kena mono i popo kena leghua nimua na vanohehe.
Heghami mai ghamami na vanga ke nabamami leuleghu maghavu.
Talutavogha na komi paluhamami vaghagna ighami kiti talutavogha na komi paluhadia arahai kena eia na dika itamami.
Saghoi lubatia na auau keda mai itamami.
Kari sokara pungusighami kori mana nigna a Satan.’
