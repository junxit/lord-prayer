Ndondehamba

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Ndondehamba (njd), © 2024 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Atati bhitu bhammile kunani kulihunde kwa Annoongo,
lihina linu lya kuhalala lilumbalilwe.
Umwenye gwinu guike.
Upalyo gwinu gutendeke pamba pachilambo handa kunani kulihunde kwa Annoongo. Ntupekee leleni chakulya chitu cha machobha goa.
Ntulekelele kulemwa kwitu,
handa na twenga chitutenda kwaalekelela bhabhatulemwite. Nkotokutujingia mu malengelo,
nambo ntulolekee kubhoka kwa bhenibhala abohu. Kwa kubhee Umwenye nga gwinu, na machili na ng'anyimo hata machobha goa. Elo.’”
