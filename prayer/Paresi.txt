Haliti-Paresi

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Waitare Wenati Aho (pabNT), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Wexe Enore, hiso atyo enokoa hisaonita. Kasani tyotyaha aiminisaha hiso waokita.
Hisohena ali Xekohaseti hisaona maheta. Kasani ali waikohekoa haokitere akereta tyaona waokita, enokoa nali kaomakitere akereta.
Kasani nakairati hisa womana tyotyaki ferakene waokita.
Himaotyanehetehena iniyalahare wisomitereharenai, haiyaharenai iniyalahare mohenere wiso wimaotyanehititere akereta.
Hoka awa atyo hahalakihena wiso hoka wikahasakaharetyakehena. Xakore hoka maika iniyalahare nonitata hiyehetehena wiso. Hiyaiya, homanere atyo waikohe. Iyateliti exahe kaihalaharetyaketa mene hikakoa waokita, xoaha taita.
