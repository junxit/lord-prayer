=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Triqui, Itunyoso (trq, ISO 639-3 trq), © 2019 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tata chréꞌ nne xataꞌ, huin ruhua únj si ga̱ꞌmi ꞌueé ni ngüi̱ si-xugüi manꞌán re̱ꞌ aj.
Ni̱ huin ruhua únj sisi̱ ga̱ꞌnaꞌ re̱ꞌ gui̱nicaj re̱ꞌ sun rian xungüi̱ nan anj.
Ni̱ da̱j rúnꞌ dagahuin sa̱ꞌ ni si mán xataꞌ rian manꞌán re̱ꞌ, ni̱ hué daj da̱gahuin sa̱ꞌ ni ngüi̱ mán ni̱caꞌ rian manꞌán re̱ꞌ, ruhua únj.
Ni̱ ri̱qui re̱ꞌ si xa̱ ni únj daꞌ go̱ꞌngo güi.
Ni̱ da̱j rúnꞌ guinicaj únj ꞌngo̱ niman nico rian ni tsínj guiꞌyaj gaquinꞌ rian únj, ni̱ daꞌngaꞌ daj gui̱nicaj re̱ꞌ ꞌngo̱ niman nico rian únj.
Ni̱ si̱ duná re̱ꞌ si di̱gyaꞌ yunꞌunj si quij ni únj mánj.
Sani̱ ga̱ꞌni re̱ꞌ si ga̱ ni̱ni únj rian si quij hua daj.
Daj si manꞌán re̱ꞌ nicaj daranꞌ sun.
Ni̱ nicáj re̱ꞌ daranꞌ fuerza nej.
Ni̱ hua ꞌueé ni̱nꞌ nné re̱ꞌ.
Ni̱ hué daj ga̱huin re̱ꞌ ni̱ganj ni̱nꞌ, ruhua únj. Amén. Hué daj ga̱ꞌmi a ni é re̱ꞌ ngaa ga̱chinj jniꞌyaj á re̱ꞌ rian Yanꞌanj anj.
