=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Noquen hihbaan joi (kaqNT, ISO 639-3 kaq), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Noquen naihoh queha Papan, mia Papa Dios jai honanahbo jahue.
Miin jihuetahbo non janon, noque hihbojahue. Min nai hoque hahcai quescajaquin neno rihbi hahhue, mia queenai quescabijaquin.
Nea neten piti noque hinanhue.
Noquen hohcha rihbi sohuahue. Noque qui tiromahibaan bi tah non jaa shinanxonma jato sirijaquin yohuanquihnai. Jascajaquin rihbi noquen hohcha sohuaxon jaa shinanyamascahue, Papan.
Satanasnen noquen coshi tanatihi janin noque hioyamahue, Papan. Satanas noque bihmayamahue.
