Mõxtã

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dios Cʉ̃ Cauetibʉjʉ Cũrĩcã Tuti (cbcNT), © 2012 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Jãã Pacʉ, jõbui macããcʉ̃, nipetiro camasã mʉ wãmerẽ na ĩroáto.
Tunu bairoa mʉ carotimasĩrĩpaʉ ati yepapʉ to apáro.
Jõbuipʉ mʉ carotirorea bairo to baiáto ati yepapʉ cʉ̃ãrẽ.
Tunu ãme jãã caʉgapee, jãã caborije, jããrẽ carʉsarije ũnie cʉ̃ãrẽ jãã joya.
Tunu bairoa roro jãã caátaje cʉ̃ãrẽ jãã masiriyobojaya.
Camasã roro jããrẽ na caátajere jãã camasiritirorea bairo roro jãã caátaje cʉ̃ãrẽ jãã masiriyobojaya mʉ cʉ̃ã.
Tunu wãtĩ roro jããrẽ cʉ̃ caátigarijere jãã ẽñotaya. Jãã netõõña.
Mʉ jeto carotimasĩ mʉ ãniña.
Mʉ jeto catutuanetõʉ̃ mʉ ãniña.
Mʉ jeto camasã na caĩroapaʉ mʉ ãniña tocãnacã rʉ̃mʉa. Bairo to baiáto,’
bairo Diore cʉ̃ mʉjãã caĩjẽnipee ñuña,” na ĩ quetibʉjʉyupʉ Jesús.
