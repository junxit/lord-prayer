=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kitabi Momangkilona Wolio (wlo), © 2024 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

'E Amamami i sorogaa,
Atomuliangimo o saromu.
Umbamo pamarintamu,
amembalimo opea ipeelumu
i alamu mboomo i sorogaa.
Manga dhawuaka kinande eo sii sakawana,
tee uʼamponia o kasalahamami mboomo ingkami taʼamponi kasalahana mia mosala tee ingkami.
Bholi manga bhawa i nuncana kapancoba,
maka manga rambasaka minaaka i giu momadhaki.
[Roonamo Ingkoomo mokopamarintana, mokokuasana tee momuliana saʼumurua. Aamin.']
