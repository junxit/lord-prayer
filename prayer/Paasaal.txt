Pasaale Sisaala

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Sisaala-Paasaal (sig), © 2001 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Á Kuwo Wɩɩsɩ aa we wɩɩsɩbee tɩyaŋ,
leŋ dɩ ɩ feŋ na bigisii.
Leŋ dɩ ɩ koro ko.
Leŋ dɩ nala kɩ tɩŋa ɩ nyʋwa taŋha no tɩyaŋ anɩɩ ba aa tɩŋ gɛɛ wɩɩsɩbee tɩyaŋ.
Tɩya ma á gyɩnaŋ nyʋwa kɩdiiliye
aŋ kpa á wɩbɔmɔ kyɛ ma, anɩɩ á mɛ aa kpaa á dɔŋtɩŋsɩ wɩbɔmɔ kɩ kyɛ ba gɛɛ.
Kyiyeli ma dɩ kɩŋ buloŋ ta ko mʋrɩ ma we wɩbɔmɔ yayɩ tɩyaŋ aŋ lɩɩ ma Sɩtaanɩ nosi tɩyaŋ.
