Ganoqa

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Kubokota Adapt New Testament (ghn), © 2024 The Seed Company, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

<Tamamami, ao pa noka,
mi tapangaga na izongomu.
Mu valamea na mua binangara.
Mu vagore votua na mua nyorogua,
aza za vevei pa noka za mu veini pa pezo.
Mu vadigami na tekutekuna mami toani pa ngenari.
Mu taleozodigami ria na mami sela,
vevei gami gami taleozodi ria qari roiti lame sela tadigami.
Muke toka laodigami pa toketoke,
goto mu vasaregami tana kutana nikerena,> mu gua.
