Ubuvala

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from YAUBADA YANA VONA'AWAUFAUFA 'IVAUNA (yml), © 1984, 1988, 2025 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc. and SIL Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Tamama,
'u miamia abame.
'Omu magemagetamu,
nuanuama tomotoga fuema 'a na ve'ililibuyemu.
Nuanuama 'u na veveimeyema.
Nuanuama tomia fwayafwaya yamu nuanua 'a na 'i'idewadi,
bani'odi tomia abama yamu nuanua i 'i'idewadi.
Nuanuama 'ama'ama 'u na velevelema 'aiata 'aitamogana 'aitamogana faifaidi.
Mali tomotoga i vesauluva sakoina 'waimeye 'a nuatavunidi,
'omu wata bani'odi yama sakona 'u da nuatavunidi.
'Eba sailubu 'waidie kebu 'u na tauyema,
wata 'u na 'ita'ita'iema Tosakona 'wainega.
Yamu veimea, yamu toketokena wata yamu 'ai'aiwabu,
kebu i na 'a'avaga, i na 'eno-vagata.
Vona mogitana yama nuanua bani'odi.’
