=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Yoac kekehagocac Dzadzahac gboria nga Mitiyegec (ded, ISO 639-3 ded), © 2005 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Kurumeng Mannina, geac kpac kpetiac febeso.
Nga eucererengga hanemmebeso. Kurumennu geac songo nga negen negeng modacgec fede, siriha imocngoc bangecfu nenearu fingecbeso.
Ge yaguc yaka socnina nemmena.
Nga neni ngic yeneac kopocsic waandimbe, siriha ifingoc kopocsicninaac turunga wanemmena.
Ge faitobannu mi fainununa nga manganaunac mesinunubesena.’
