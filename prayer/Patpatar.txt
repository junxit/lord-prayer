Gelik

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from No Sigar Kunubus tika ma Ira Ninge na Lotu (gfk), © 1997-2018 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Amehet Sus aram ra mawe,
mehet sip bia da nes kilam no hinsaam bia i halhaal,
no num kinkinis na harkurai ma ra harbalaurai na hanuat,
ma bia da gil ira num sinisip kai ra ula hanuo,
hoing di la gilgil aram ra mawe.
Mehet sip bia nu tabar mehet katin ma ira amehet nian ing i haruat ma tiga bung,
ma nu lik luban se ira numehet magingin sakena,
hoing mehet te lik luban se ira magingin sakena di gil ta mehet.
Ma mehet sip mah bia nu balaure bat mehet ta ira harwalaam,
ma nu kap pukus mehet tano Ut na Gil Sakena.
[Io, anum no kinkinis na harkurai ma ra harbalaurai, no dadas, ma no minamar hathatikai. Amen.]’
