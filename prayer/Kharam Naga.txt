Kharam

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from The New Testament in Kharam (kfw), © 2021 The Word for the World International, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Marwaanna kaom kin Pa:
Naraming kaya omrase;
Narengram juotung rase;
marwaanna nalungdou intho angnga nuoipilchungnga inthorase.
Keini sik arei kaom bak le in awini napero.
Halli keini chungnga kamasial ngai kinrangaidam angnga,
Keini itho masialna ngaikha narangaidamro.
Halli keini matheemna khan naminchulmaro,
takkha Kathamak ah inphut keini nasanro. Ajarchu Rengram, ranak khat-um lurna-lianna ngaihi hektik kaboiya nangta erase Amen.”
