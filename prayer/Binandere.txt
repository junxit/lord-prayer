=== Traditional ===
[Verified from God da Ge Wasiride (bhg, ISO 639-3 bhg), © 2014-2021 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Mamo natokare utuda isima edo eta awa,
dao ito kotopu tou ore.
Gisijiari ito doyo gumbare.
Nakare jipapa ito butu einda ore,
dá ouwamanemi utuda edo era ango.
Namondekare deguta da (1) dowae, indari jipapa edo era awa;
iji einde piewasi.
Edo jimbo bebegae natokare
kotembedo dowasi,
dá ango nanekare embo neneimi
jimbo bebegae ro namondekare edo era awa,
kotembedo dodo era.
Edo bawarida nakare kundo aimbote,
ata embo beyae da yai nakare toregewasi.
Imo awa natokare Daotari, Goroba Berarida Goroba,
awatedo imo iji natopo itari dasiga ore. Awara.’
