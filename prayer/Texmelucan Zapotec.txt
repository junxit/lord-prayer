=== Traditional ===
[Verified from De riidz ni trat cub nu bicy nu Ñgyoozh mbecy: Salmos; Proverbios (zpzNTpp, ISO 639-3 zpz), © 2004 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Ñgyoozh, Uz de̱, ru biñ nu zub gyibaa. Rlagy ñi ca lagy de̱ lor.
De̱ ya̱ rdiñ de̱ nu cyid gyel gubier nir lat de̱.
Rdiñ de̱ lor nu gac ñii gyishlombecy ii gal nu rlagy ru, nunu gal nu rac gyibaa.
Dzi na doo coz nu rlagy ñi nu da̱w de̱.
Bicy sulagy ne̱ de̱ ni de coz cyi nu ricy de̱ gal nu de̱ ya̱, ricy sulagyaꞌ de̱ ni de yu nu shiñ ricy nu de̱ ya̱.
Cueꞌ zaan ru nu gyet lag de̱ lo preb, sinu bloo chu de̱ ya̱ lat de coz a̱ꞌ nap.
Gun ru nac ru biñ nu riib ic
nunu ru nac ru biñ nu gac gyicy dela coz
nunu ru nac ru biñ nu lily na.
Ni nac ru par tub la. Ni gac ñii.
