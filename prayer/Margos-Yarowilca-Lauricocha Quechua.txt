=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Mushog Testamento (qvmNT, ISO 639-3 qvm), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

Gloriacho caycag Tayta Dios,
pï-maytapis tantyaycatsilay respitaycushunayquipag.
Lapan runacuna gam munashgayquinog cawayculätsun.
Gloriacho munashgayquita rurapäcushgannog cay patsachöpis munashgayquita ruracayculätsun.
Micunäpag micuynë ama pishiyculätsuntsu, Tayta.
Chiquimagnëcunata noga perdonashgänog jutsa rurashgäcunatapis perdonaycalämay.
Jutsaman Satanás tunitsimayta munaptinpis tsapaycalämay, Tayta.
Tsaynogpis yanapaycalämay jutsata mana ruraypa imaypis cawanäpag.
[Lapanpagpis munayniyog captiquimi gampa maquiquicho imaycapis caycan, Tayta.
Tsaymi imayyagpis alabashga canquipag.] Amén.
