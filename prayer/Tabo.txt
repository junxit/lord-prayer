Tabo

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from GODOKONO HIDO TABO (knv-aramia), © 2006, 2023 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea and Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Aeno Hunu Haboeno Nabiwi,
wadiyala amia꞉no talona mahilo
tawakaluba꞉te nadipata꞉mena꞉,
ba꞉gala wadiyala ama꞉le Ko Gawadubuma꞉ deda꞉na
ba꞉be komote na꞉hawakalamene.
Hibila, ama꞉le amia꞉no ubi keba ka꞉na ka꞉miya꞉teona
Hunu Habalo ba꞉ba ka꞉na bododilinakomena꞉ta,
ba꞉bema꞉ wadiyala ama꞉ hopo habalo
amia꞉no ubi komokomo atumu na꞉kododilinakuya꞉.
‘Ba꞉gala wadiyala ama꞉ ba꞉moe kapiya hegeloeno baa꞉
atamo modobolo nikaa꞉ma꞉.
Ba꞉gala komopa꞉te atamo kuba dododilina,
ale ba꞉bi kuba egebolelamete
kadodoholelamema꞉nema꞉,
ba꞉bema꞉ wadiyala ama꞉ atumu
aeno kuba egebolea꞉mete nadodoholea꞉ma꞉.
‘Ba꞉gala Saitanakono kuba gabamo emogabuimi ipuwalo
ama꞉le huiyatiya a ba owalubi
amia꞉no hido gabodo na꞉magatoenakuya꞉,
iyo, ebeno kuba ododi ipuwane
aeno ka꞉ka꞉ na꞉mula꞉lea꞉ma꞉,
mabu amia꞉no Ko Gawadubu komo,
ba꞉gala helo konomo,
ba꞉gala awagaha komo
emedena emedena tetelo ama꞉godolo ba꞉pola꞉namena꞉.
Ba꞉ba꞉la.’”
