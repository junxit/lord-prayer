Ivatan

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from New Testament in the Yaka language (Congo-Brazzaville) (iyx), © 2024 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

“Taayi a bisi wu li ku mayula,
nyaala muutu-muutu ayaaba ti kuumbu a we yiba ya Ngira.
Nyaala ipfumu kia we iyira,
nyaala muutu-muutu, nha tsi, ayiluu litoono la we weti bu lo lili mu uyilimi ku yula.
Ha bisi bia udia biafwaana mu iluumbu ki.
Nyaala mayaama ma bisi,
weti bu bisi ka dili mu unyaala bangaa mayaama.
Na a nyaala bisi dikoto mu mapuusu pe,
ka kuulu bisi kwaa Wu Mubi.
[Ka bia we ni Ipfumu, Litu na Buzitu, mu taanga di dihele uwa. Aameni.]”
