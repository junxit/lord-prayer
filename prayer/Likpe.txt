Sεkpεlé

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Sekpele (lip), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Boanto wǝ nkpe kato,
tǝ lǝ bakpasǝ fǝ diye.
Fǝ sekakedidi lǝ sibǝ.
Beyifo lǝ fǝ lelabi ǝsuǝ
lǝ kasɔ mfo fe kase inte kato.
Tǝ bo bo mmi eto didisa.
Fi bo abua atsyɛ bo
fe kase bo tsya leefi katsyɛ mba laata bo kebu.
Mǝntǝ lǝ budufǝ lǝ botokabe ǝsuǝ,
lǝǝlǝkǝ bo etsyiko lǝ abua ǝsuǝ.
Itsyi sekadidi ku ǝsuǝale
ku diyǝnde eyifo fǝ ale lefe saa.
Ibǝ itǝ.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
