Kãkwã

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Dios ã jáap naáwát tólih (cbv), © 2008 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Paá, meém jeáboó jʉmni, chah tʉ́ini ma jʉmʉchah, meemdíh jwĩ wẽina caá. Ma wʉ̃t chah yáaat caá jwiít jwĩ náahna caá.
Nihat meém maáh ma jʉmatdih jéihnit, ĩ jepaha naáh. Ma wʉtʉchah, jeáboó jʉmnit ĩ tʉ́i jepahat pah, nin baácdih moonbʉ́t ĩ jepaha naáh.
Yeó jáap jʉmat pah jwĩ jeémát pínahdih ma wʉ̃hʉʉ́.
Bita jwiítdih ĩ yéej chãjatdih jwĩ yohat pah, meembʉ́t jwĩ yéej chãjatdih ma yohoó.
Nemépwã ĩ maáh jwiítdih ã yéejaíhichah, ma teo wáacá, jwĩ yéej chãjcat pínah niijná. Páant jwĩ ʉʉ́bʉ́chah, meém jwĩ Maáh chah wẽpni jʉmna, ma jéih jepahna caá. Páant ma jʉmʉchah, yeó jáap jʉmat pah nihat meemdíh ĩ wẽi naáwát caá náahap. Páant tií ã jʉmʉ nah’, Diosdih ñi niíj ʉʉ́bʉ́.
