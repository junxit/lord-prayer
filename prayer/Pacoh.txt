=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Pacoh, Cado (pac, ISO 639-3 pac), © 2019 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Cucúh A-ám he tớq pilŏ́ng ơi!
Seq inha dyôn nhéq tưh ticuoi dyám náp nốh inha.
Seq inha cơt sút nhéq tưh ticuoi tớq cúc cuteq ndô.
Alứng seq inha dyôn ngai peh ngê inha tớq cúc cuteq ndô
muchứng ngai peh ngê inha tớq pilŏ́ng tưi.
Seq inha dyôn he bôn khám cha ingái ndô.
Seq inha táh luaih he
muchứng he khoiq táh luaih ngai táq ada he.
Seq inha êq dyôn he tumúh callong chim ntáng lư,
alứng êq dyôn yang Satán tardông he táq arnáq saq.
Co nhéq nốh sút, nhéq nốh chức, alứng nhéq nốh ang-ưr át níc alứng inha,
sút pang lang dơi. Amen.’
