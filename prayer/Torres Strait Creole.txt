Yumplatok

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Baibol Tores Streit Yumplatok (tcs), © 2016 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

PapaGod blo yumpla de antap lo eben,
mipla prea ol pipol go ona ane kare neim blo yu antap.
Mipla prea da taim go kam kuikwan wen yu go ruloba ebribodi.
Mipla prea ebridem ubi blo yu go kamaut ya lo ert,
seimkain wase i kamaut de antap lo eben.
Gibe mipla nap kaikai wanem mipla nidem po tidei,
ane pogibe mipla po ol dem nugudting mipla i bin mekem,
seimkain wase mipla i bin pogibe dem pipol uda i bin meke nugudting gense mipla.
Pliz elpe mipla so mipla nogo gibap ane meke ol nugudting,
bat elpe mipla prom no pole dat nugudwan.
Da ples wea yu ruloba ebribodi, da prapa bigpaua blo yu, ane prapa big preiz, i mata blo yu poeba. Amen.
