ܣܘܼܪܸܬ݂

=== Traditional ===
ܐܲܒ݂ܘܼܢ ܕܒܲܫܡܲܝܵܐ
ܢܸܬ݂ܩܲܕܲܫ ܫܡܵܟ݂
ܬܹܐܬܹܐ ܡܲܠܟܘܼܬ݂ܵܟ݂
ܢܸܗܘܸܐ ܨܸܒ݂ܝܵܢܵܟ݂ ܐܲܝܟܲܢܵܐ ܕܒܲܫܡܲܝܵܐ ܐܵܦ݂ ܒܐܲܪܥܵܐ
ܗܲܒ݂ ܠܲܢ ܠܲܚܡܵܐ ܕܣܘܼܢܩܵܢܲܢ ܝܵܘܡܵܢܵܐ
ܘܲܫܒ݂ܘܿܩ ܠܲܢ ܚܵܘܒܲܝ̈ܢ ܐܲܝܟܲܢܵܐ ܕܐܵܦ݂ ܚܢܲܢ ܫܒ݂ܲܩܢ ܠܚܲܝܵܒܲܝ̈ܢ
ܘܠܵܐ ܬܲܥܠܲܢ ܠܢܸܣܝܘܿܢܵܐ ܐܸܠܵܐ ܦܲܨܵܢ ܡܸܢ ܒܝܼܫܵܐ

ܡܸܛܠ ܕܕܝܼܠܵܟ݂ ܗ̄ܝ ܡܲܠܟܘܼܬ݂ܵܐ ܘܚܲܝܠܵܐ ܘܬܸܫܒܘܿܚܬܵܐ ܠܥܵܠܲܡ ܥܵܠܡܝܼܢ܂ ܐܵܡܹܝܢ܂

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.

(Transliteration: Abun d-bashmaya, nethqadash shmakh; tethe malkuthakh; nehwe sebyanakh aykana d-bashmaya ap b-ar'a; hab lan lahma d-sunqanan yawmana; wa-shboq lan hawbayn aykana d-ap hnan shbaqn l-hayyabayn; w-la ta'lan l-nesyona ella passan men bisha; metul d-dilakh hi malkutha w-hayla w-teshbuhta l-'alam 'almin. Amen. — This is the Peshitta text of Matthew 6:9-13, the Lord's Prayer in the Syriac/Aramaic tongue of Jesus; the Peshitta includes the doxology.)
