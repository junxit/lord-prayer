Wanuma

=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Qenu Qob Uber (wnu), © 2001 The Bible Society of Papua New Guinea, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the colon the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Ininou Tain nonou ginamt igoan. Nonou unum qau igoriner.
Nonou bibis eng init waga deriner. Ne munon nonou ginamt qite qob qamara igetet toat igour gas ende in ag e gor ende oboun.
Qen see in mani nioun eng ne indaran.
In qomon boru ebet igoun eng init biruet arirama ne munon qei init qomon boru ebet igour eng in wurit biruet ariramboun.
Ne inwarigesi me inquguragsi. Mi boru ger inquguragsieriba gab init biruet arirom. Ende at qamarar.

=== Literal (mirrors the canonical English) ===
Same as the Traditional form above.
