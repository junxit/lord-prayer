“ ‘Yɛn Agya a wowɔ ɔsoro,
wo din ho ntew.
Wʼahenni mmra.
Nea wopɛ nyɛ wɔ asase so,
sɛnea ɛyɛ wɔ ɔsoro.
Ma yɛn, yɛn daa aduan nnɛ,
na fa yɛn aka kyɛ yɛn,
sɛnea yɛde kyɛ wɔn a wɔde yɛn aka.
Nnyaa yɛn nkɔ sɔhwɛ mu.
Na yi yɛn fi ɔbɔnefo no nsam.’
