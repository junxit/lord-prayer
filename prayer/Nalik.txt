=== Traditional ===
[Verified from A Dorang Daxa Zin Nakmai (nal, ISO 639-3 nal), © 2023 Wycliffe Bible Translators Australia, Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. Reproduced as printed, including the edition's own line structure.]

‘Damana maam la lia,
aubina dina vaxoxok aizinum,
maadi saxot adu a bikabar zunum kana balas.
Gu waambazof ainaxamang
ma sasaxotang sunum iriat la pira angkari malasing saait ikula la lia.
Talaas maam pana vanganang ku taning.
Naxaam pizin amun matmalabuk taksaat simaam,
malasing maam saait maadi naxaam pizin amun matmalabuk taksaat sin naandi ina di ra giu wana maam.
Ma xana daxa tamon guna wen luaa maam ku lamaskana vaaxabebeuang,
singsaxai gu zaxot faulin maam siaana rapti raksaat.’
