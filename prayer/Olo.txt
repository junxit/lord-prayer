=== Traditional ===
[Verified from Il Teingipe Ma Ili Topwepe Lapiri Lirouku (ong, ISO 639-3 ong), © 1997 Wycliffe Bible Translators, Inc., Matthew 6:9-13, obtained from eBible.org. This edition prints the prayer as running prose; the narrative introduction to verse 9 has been removed at the boundary the edition itself prints, and the verses are set one per line. No other change was made.]

Ma Ili, ye Eiya louku liti yuwei. Mete ma kelelaiye kolo, le ku ma meila nange leiye, ye Ma Ili.
Ye ma au untoluku, ku ma mingepe il peiye watafei ririm piti yuwei, pe pingepe il peiye.
Ye wauku oweli piti fei ku ma maplei.
Ye ma onposiepe il olpe pouku kolo watafei ku re ma onposiepe il olpe pite mete nemple kolo.
Ye ma usauku mingio tisi oli kolo, wolo ye ma kanouku tanio towa.’

Same as the Traditional form above.
